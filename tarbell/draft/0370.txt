PERPLEXO CUT AND RESTORED ROPE

This is a logical follow-up for the rope trick just described, where performer is left with a short piece of rope and a long piece.

To PErRForM:

Let us say that you are left in position of Figs. 10 and 11 of the preceding effect. Take hold of lower end of short piece of rope with right hand and fold it up into left hand where end can be held between thumb and fingers, Fig. 1 A. Wrap long rope around left hand a couple of times, and then unroll and let long rope be free from short piece. Holding long rope in right hand, you bring left hand with loop down towards your left side and in turning left side away from audience a bit in order to show long rope, you reverse the loop in left hand into position as in Fig. 1.

Even up the long rope and hold its center between thumb and first and second fingers of right hand, Fig. 2. Ends of rope hang down.
