To Appear a Coin: Coin is in the secret pocket. Paper is shown both sides. Left hand grasps pocket and holds with opening down. Hand also presses enough on coin to keep it from slipping out of pocket.

Right hand comes up and, with thumb underneath opening of pocket, allows the coin to gradually slide out to center of paper, Fig. 7. Paper is now folded over the coin. When paper. is opened the coin is found inside.

Transposition of Coins: If desired you can use two papers. A coin that disappears from one apparently appears in the other. This, of course, requires two half dollars.

MODERN COIN VANISHING PAPER

In this method of coin vanish the performer has control of the coin as it leaves the paper and can be reproduced elsewhere. This method is for performers who like clean-cut movements.
