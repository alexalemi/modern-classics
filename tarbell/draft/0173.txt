Hold the tambourine with left hand. Outside of tambourine is towards audience. Right hand is held naturally at right side against the concealed cards under coat, Fig. 12.

Turn the tambourine over to show inside. The turning movement is made downward so that the hoops come on a level with your right hand beside coat, Fig. 13.
