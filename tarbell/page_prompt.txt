STANDING INSTRUCTIONS: PAGE READING "THE TARBELL COURSE IN MAGIC", VOL. II (1942)

Context. A restored edition of Harlan Tarbell's The Tarbell Course in
Magic, Revised Edition, Volume II (Lessons 20-33), Louis Tannen, 1942. It
is in the US public domain (registered 1942, never renewed; see
tarbell/COPYRIGHT.txt). It is a textbook of stage magic, written and
illustrated by Tarbell. We are correcting the OCR of a scan page by page,
and cutting out his drawings. Working directory:
/home/alemi/projects/modern-classics.

For each leaf NNNN you are given:
  tarbell/draft/NNNN.txt        the OCR draft (read it)
  tarbell/_src/pages/NNNN.png   the page image (view it with Read, ONCE)
You write TWO files:
  tarbell/fixes/NNNN.txt        text corrections, as patch blocks
  tarbell/figs/NNNN.txt         the page's drawings, one line each
and check the text with:
  python3 tarbell/apply_fixes.py NNNN    (prints the corrected page, or ERROR)

DO NOT rewrite or reproduce whole pages. Write only patch blocks, each as
small as it can be while matching exactly once in the draft.

PATCH FORMAT (tarbell/fixes/NNNN.txt):
    <<<
    exact text from the draft
    ===
    corrected text
    >>>
The old text must occur EXACTLY ONCE in the draft; copy it character for
character (include neighbouring words to make it unique). Blocks apply in
order. Write files with a heredoc or Python. Run apply_fixes.py and fix
any ERROR. A page with no text errors gets no fixes file.

DRAFT MARKUP (keep it; fix it where the draft is wrong):
  # leaf NNNN            first line; never change it
  paragraphs separated by blank lines
  ## Title               a heading: a lesson title ("## Lesson 21: Magic with
                         Wands"), a trick title ("## Vanishing Handkerchief
                         Wand"), or a sub-heading the print sets apart
                         ("## Effect", "## Preparation", "## To Perform",
                         "## Note"). Headings are in title case, not caps.
  ⟨cont⟩ text            first paragraph, if it continues from the
                         previous page (starts mid-sentence)
  text ⟨cont⟩            last paragraph, if it continues onto the next page
  [FIG 12]               where a drawing stands in the flow of the page
                         (see below); "[FIG 12-13]" for one drawing
                         labelled with two numbers
Running heads ("56 THE TARBELL COURSE IN MAGIC", "Lesson 21 -- MAGIC WITH
WANDS 57") and page numbers are already removed; delete any that remain.

WHAT TO CORRECT IN THE TEXT
1. Every word to match the print: misread letters ("Norte" for Note,
   "Maeic" for Magic), missing or extra words, punctuation, quotation
   marks, dashes. Small capitals in the print are written in ordinary
   case ("To Perform", not "To PERFORM"). Italic words as *word*.
2. REMOVE everything the OCR read inside a drawing: its "Fig. 3" label,
   letters (A, B), words lettered on the drawing, and stray symbols
   ("FIG.3 ) iY = FIG.4", "Sec FIG.6"). Put "[FIG N]" on its own line
   (a paragraph of its own) at the point where the drawing sits in the
   reading order -- usually between the paragraphs it sits between. A
   drawing beside a paragraph goes after that paragraph. A drawing that
   interrupts a SENTENCE (the text resumes under it mid-sentence) goes
   after the end of that paragraph: join the sentence back together.
3. Keep the text's own references to figures ("run it through paper
   tube, Fig. 3.") exactly as printed.
4. Structure: fix headings the draft missed or invented; join a paragraph
   wrongly split; split one wrongly joined at the printed indent.
5. Tables or lists the OCR scrambled: retype them, one item per line,
   items separated by blank lines.

THE FIGURES FILE (tarbell/figs/NNNN.txt), one line per drawing:
    Fig. N | x0 y0 x1 y1 | description
  - "Fig. N" as printed ("Fig. 12-13" if one drawing carries two numbers;
    "unnumbered" for a drawing with no number, e.g. a lesson's heading
    picture or a portrait).
  - x0 y0 x1 y1: the drawing's box as FRACTIONS of the page image (0 =
    left/top, 1 = right/bottom), two decimals. Generous: include every
    line and letter of the drawing, INCLUDING Tarbell's hand-lettered
    "FIG. N" label (it is part of the drawing), and excluding the body
    text around it.
  - description: 12-35 words, plain and concrete, for a reader who
    cannot see it -- what the drawing shows, using the text's words for
    the props and moves ("the right hand palms the coin while the left
    seems to take it"). No praise words. No square brackets, asterisks,
    tabs or line breaks; end with a full stop.
  A page with no drawing gets the single line:  none
  Every "[FIG N]" you put in the text must have its line here, and every
  line here its "[FIG N]" in the text.

Work leaf by leaf: write both files and run apply_fixes.py for a leaf
BEFORE viewing the next image. If you lose track, list tarbell/figs/ and
continue with the first of your leaves that has no figs file.

Report at the end: leaves done, drawings found, and anything unresolved
(an unreadable word, a figure number missing or repeated, a structure you
were unsure of).
