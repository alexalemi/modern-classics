# leaf 0065

## Paraphernalia

1—A black wand with nickeled tips. A fine black silk thread is tied to each end of the wand where the black body joins the nickeled tips, Fig. 1. When thread is pulled away from wand at center, distance should be about 4¾ inches. Length of thread depends upon size of performer’s hands. A bit of experimenting will determine the correct length for most effective working. Audience, of course, is not supposed to know of the thread.

[FIG 1]

## To Perform

Hold wand with both hands as in Fig. 2. Thread falls downward in a loop, behind the thumbs.

[FIG 2]

*“This is a study in magnetism. Some say wand magnetism. Some say personal magnetism, or advanced magnetism, or mental magnetism, while others say just magnetism, or the power of mind over matter.”*

Bring thumbs up through the loop of thread, Fig. 3. Pull thumbs outward until thread is taut and wand clings to thumbs, Fig. 4, fingers being separated from thumbs as far as possible.

[FIG 3]

[FIG 4]

Bring end B of wand down on palm of right hand, release the left hand, and raise the right thumb so thread is taut and the wand is suspended as in Fig. 5.

[FIG 5]

Grasp end A with left hand and slide right hand along the wand until it is in the position as shown in Fig. 6.
