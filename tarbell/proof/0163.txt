Straighten second and third fingers and with tips of these fingers push card down well into palm of hand, Fig. 8.

Fig. 9 shows how card is held in palm. It is pressed against base of thumb and is clipped at outer corners between first and second, and between third and fourth fingers.

[FIG 9]

To get card from this position to back of hand again, push up on card with base of thumb, Fig. 10.

[FIG 10]

Curl fingers inward towards palm, keeping fourth and first fingers well over the card. Fig. 11.

[FIG 11]

Straighten hand out and card is pivoted to back of hand again, Fig. 12.

[FIG 12]

*Practice the moves up to here until you have them mastered before you go further. When you can get the card from front to back of hand and back again easily, then continue with the next step.*

Now, you must learn how to turn your hand in Back and Front Hand Palming to show it empty. The card must be concealed as you turn your hand, and this is the way you do it. You have turned the card over onto the back of your hand, and you show empty palm, Fig. 13.
