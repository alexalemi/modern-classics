Proposition III.27 (Theorem)

[Figure aff]

In *equal circles ⟦g:03ec⟧ and ⟦g:03ed⟧ the angles ⟦g:03ee⟧ and ⟦g:03ef⟧ which stand upon equal arches are equal, whether they be at the centres or at the circumferences.*

For if it be possible, let one of them

⟦g:03ef⟧ be greater than the other ⟦g:03ee⟧

and make ⟦g:03f0⟧ = ⟦g:03ef⟧

∴ ⟦g:03f1⟧ = ⟦g:03f2⟧ (prop. III.26)

but ⟦g:03f3⟧ = ⟦g:03f2⟧ (hyp.)

∴ ⟦g:03f3⟧ = ⟦g:03f1⟧ a part equal to the whole, which is absurd;

∴ neither angle is greater than the other,

and ∴ they are equal.

Q. E. D.
