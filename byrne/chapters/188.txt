Proposition VI.28 (Problem)

[Figure ahu]

To *divide a given straight line (⟦g:0679⟧) so that the rectangle contained by its segments may be equal to a given area, not exceeding the square of half the line.*

Let the given area be = ⟦g:067a⟧ ².

Bisect ⟦g:0679⟧, or make ⟦g:067b⟧ = ⟦g:0455⟧ ;

and if ⟦g:067b⟧ ² = ⟦g:067a⟧ ²,

the problem is solved.

But if ⟦g:067b⟧ ² ≠ ⟦g:067a⟧ ²,

then must ⟦g:067b⟧ > ⟦g:067a⟧ (hyp.).

Draw ⟦g:067c⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:067b⟧ = ⟦g:067a⟧ ;

make ⟦g:067d⟧ = ⟦g:067b⟧ or ⟦g:0455⟧ ;

with ⟦g:067d⟧ as radius describe a circle cutting the given line; draw ⟦g:067e⟧.

Then ⟦g:067f⟧ × ⟦g:0680⟧ + ⟦g:0681⟧ ² = ⟦g:067b⟧ ² (prop. II.5) = ⟦g:067e⟧ ².

But ⟦g:067e⟧ ² = ⟦g:067c⟧ ² + ⟦g:0681⟧ ² (prop. I.47);

∴ ⟦g:067f⟧ × ⟦g:0680⟧ + ⟦g:0681⟧ ² = ⟦g:067c⟧ ² + ⟦g:0681⟧ ²,

from both, take ⟦g:0681⟧ ²,

and ⟦g:067f⟧ × ⟦g:0680⟧ = ⟦g:067c⟧ ².

But ⟦g:067c⟧ = ⟦g:067a⟧ (const.),

and ∴ ⟦g:0679⟧ is so divided that ⟦g:067f⟧ × ⟦g:0680⟧ = ⟦g:067a⟧ ².

Q. E. D.
