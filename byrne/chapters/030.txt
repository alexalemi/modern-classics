Proposition I.25 (Theorem)

[Figure abz]

If *two triangles have two sides (⟦g:012b⟧ and ⟦g:012c⟧) of the one respectively equal to two sides (⟦g:012d⟧ and ⟦g:012e⟧) of the other, but their bases unequal, the angle subtended by the greater base (⟦g:012f⟧) of the one, must be greater than the angle subtended by the less base (⟦g:0130⟧) of the other.*

⟦g:0131⟧ = , > or < ⟦g:0132⟧

⟦g:0131⟧ is not equal to ⟦g:0132⟧

for if ⟦g:0131⟧ = ⟦g:0132⟧ then ⟦g:012f⟧ = ⟦g:0130⟧ (prop. I.4)

which is contrary to the hypothesis;

⟦g:0131⟧ is not less than ⟦g:0132⟧

for if ⟦g:0131⟧ < ⟦g:0132⟧

then ⟦g:012f⟧ < ⟦g:0130⟧ (prop. I.24),

which is also contrary to the hypothesis:

∴ ⟦g:0131⟧ > ⟦g:0132⟧ .

Q. E. D.
