Proposition II.13 (Theorem)

[Figure adm]

In *any triangle, the square of the side subtending an acute angle, is less than the sum of the squares of the sides containing that angle, by twice the rectangle contained by either of these sides, and the part of it intercepted between the foot of the perpendicular let fall on it from the opposite angle, and the angular point of the acute angle.First.* ⟦g:02bd⟧ ² < ⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02bf⟧ ² *by* 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ *.Second.* ⟦g:02bd⟧ ² < ⟦g:02c1⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² *by* 2 · ⟦g:02c0⟧ · ⟦g:02be⟧ *.*

First, suppose the perpendicular to fall within the triangle, then (prop. II.7)

⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² = 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ + ⟦g:02c2⟧ ²,

add to each ⟦g:023b⟧ ² then,

⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² + ⟦g:023b⟧ ² = 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ + ⟦g:02c2⟧ ² + ⟦g:023b⟧ ²

∴ (prop. I.47)

⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02bf⟧ ² = 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ + ⟦g:02bd⟧ ²,

and ∴ ⟦g:02bd⟧ ² < ⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02bf⟧ ² by 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧

Next suppose the perpendicular to fall without the triangle, then (prop. II.7)

⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² = 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ + ⟦g:02c2⟧ ²,

add to each ⟦g:023b⟧ ² then

⟦g:02be⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² + ⟦g:023b⟧ ² = 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ + ⟦g:02c2⟧ ² + ⟦g:023b⟧ ²

∴ (prop. I.47)

⟦g:02c1⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² = 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ + ⟦g:02bd⟧ ²,

∴ ⟦g:02bd⟧ ² < ⟦g:02c1⟧ ² + ⟦g:02c0⟧ ² by 2 · ⟦g:02be⟧ · ⟦g:02c0⟧ .

Q. E. D.
