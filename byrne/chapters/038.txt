Proposition I.33 (Theorem)

[Figure aci]

Straight *lines (⟦g:008d⟧ and ⟦g:00b5⟧) which join the adjacent extremities of two equal and parallel straight lines (⟦g:017e⟧ and ⟦g:017f⟧), are themselves equal and parallel.*

Draw ⟦g:0180⟧ the diagonal.

⟦g:017e⟧ = ⟦g:017f⟧ (hyp.)

⟦g:0181⟧ = ⟦g:0182⟧ (prop. I.29)

and ⟦g:0180⟧ common to the two triangles;

∴ ⟦g:008d⟧ = ⟦g:00b5⟧ , and ⟦g:0183⟧ = ⟦g:0184⟧ (prop. I.4);

and ∴ ⟦g:008d⟧ ∥ ⟦g:00b5⟧ (prop. I.27).

Q. E. D.
