Proposition VI.10 (Problem)

[Figure ahc]

To *divide a given straight line (⟦g:05d5⟧) similarly to a given divided line (⟦g:05d6⟧).*

From either extremity of the given line ⟦g:05d5⟧ draw ⟦g:05d7⟧ making any angle;

take ⟦g:05d8⟧, ⟦g:05d9⟧ and ⟦g:05da⟧ equal to ⟦g:05d6⟧, ⟦g:05db⟧ and ⟦g:05dc⟧ respectively (prop. I.2);

draw ⟦g:05dd⟧, and draw ⟦g:05de⟧ and ⟦g:05df⟧ ∥ to it.

Since ⟬{⟦g:05dd⟧‖⟦g:05de⟧‖⟦g:05df⟧}⟭ are ∥,

⟦g:05e0⟧ : ⟦g:05e1⟧ :: ⟦g:05da⟧ : ⟦g:05d9⟧ (prop. VI.2),

or ⟦g:05e0⟧ : ⟦g:05e1⟧ :: ⟦g:05dc⟧ : ⟦g:05db⟧ (const.),

and ⟦g:05e1⟧ : ⟦g:01b8⟧ :: ⟦g:05d9⟧ : ⟦g:05d8⟧ (prop. VI.2),

⟦g:05e1⟧ : ⟦g:01b8⟧ :: ⟦g:05db⟧ : ⟦g:05d6⟧ (const.),

and ∴ the given line ⟦g:05d5⟧ is divided similarly to ⟦g:05d6⟧.

Q. E. D.
