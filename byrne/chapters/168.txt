Proposition VI.8 (Theorem)

[Figure aha]

In *a right angled tiangle (⟦g:05c2⟧), if a perpendicular (⟦g:004a⟧) be drawn from the right angle to the opposite side, the triangles (⟦g:05c3⟧, ⟦g:05c4⟧) on each side of it are similar to the whole triangle and to each other.*

∵ ⟦g:05c5⟧ = ⟦g:05c6⟧ (ax. I.11),

and ⟦g:05c7⟧ common to ⟦g:05c2⟧ and ⟦g:05c3⟧;

⟦g:05c8⟧ = ⟦g:05c9⟧ (prop. I.32);

∴ ⟦g:05c2⟧ and ⟦g:05c3⟧ are equiangular; and consequently have their sides about the equal angles proportional (prop. VI.4), and are therefore similar (def. VI.1).

In like manner it may be proved that ⟦g:05c4⟧ is similar to ⟦g:05c2⟧; but ⟦g:05c3⟧ has been shown to be similar to ⟦g:05c2⟧;

∴ ⟦g:05c3⟧ and ⟦g:05c4⟧ are similar to the whole and to each other.

Q. E. D.
