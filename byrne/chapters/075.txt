Proposition III.6 (Theorem)

[Figure aec]

If *two circles ⟦g:0302⟧ touch one another internally, they have not the same centre.*

For, if it be possible, let both circles have the same centre; from such a supposed centre draw ⟦g:0303⟧, and ⟦g:00b5⟧ to the point of contact.

Then ⟦g:00b5⟧ = ⟦g:0304⟧ (def. I.15)

and ⟦g:00b5⟧ = ⟦g:0303⟧ (def. I.15)

∴ ⟦g:0304⟧ = ⟦g:0303⟧ ;

a part equal to the whole, which is absurd; therefore the assumed point is not the centre of both circles, and in the same manner it can be demonstrated that no other point is.

Q. E. D.
