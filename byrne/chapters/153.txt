Proposition V.23 (Theorem)

If *there be any number of magnitudes, and as many others, which, taken two and two in a cross order, have the same ratio; the first shall have to the last of the first magnitudes the same ratio which the first of the others has to the last of the same.N.B.— This is usually cited by the words “ ex æquali in proportione perturbatâ;” or “ ex æquo perturbato.”*

First, let there be three magnitudes ⟦g:051d⟧, ⟦g:056c⟧, ⟦g:0527⟧,

and other three, ⟦g:054f⟧, ⟦g:0570⟧, ⟦g:052c⟧,

which, taken two and two in a cross order, have the same ratio;

that is, ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:056c⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ ,

and ⟦g:056c⟧ : ⟦g:0527⟧ :: ⟦g:054f⟧ : ⟦g:0570⟧ ,

then shall ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:0527⟧ :: ⟦g:054f⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ .

Let these magnitudes and their respective equimultiples be arranged as follows :—

⟦g:051d⟧ , ⟦g:056c⟧ , ⟦g:0527⟧ , ⟦g:054f⟧ , ⟦g:0570⟧ , ⟦g:052c⟧ ,

M ⟦g:051d⟧ , M ⟦g:056c⟧ , m ⟦g:0527⟧ , M ⟦g:054f⟧ , m ⟦g:0570⟧ , m ⟦g:052c⟧ ,

then ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:056c⟧ :: M ⟦g:051d⟧ : M ⟦g:056c⟧ (prop. V.15);

and for the same reason

⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ :: m ⟦g:0570⟧ : m ⟦g:052c⟧ ;

but ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:056c⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ (hyp.),

∴ M ⟦g:051d⟧ : M ⟦g:056c⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ (prop. V.11);

and ∵ ⟦g:056c⟧ : ⟦g:0527⟧ :: ⟦g:054f⟧ : ⟦g:0570⟧ (hyp.),

∴ M ⟦g:056c⟧ : m ⟦g:0527⟧ :: M ⟦g:054f⟧ : m ⟦g:0570⟧ (prop. V.4);

then, because there are three magnitudes,

M ⟦g:051d⟧ , M ⟦g:056c⟧ , m ⟦g:056c⟧ ,

and other three M ⟦g:054f⟧ , m ⟦g:0570⟧ , m ⟦g:052c⟧ ,

which, taken two and two in a cross order, have the same ratio;

therefore, if M ⟦g:051d⟧ >, =, or < m ⟦g:0527⟧ ,

then will M ⟦g:054f⟧ >, =, or < m ⟦g:052c⟧ (prop. V.21),

and ∴ ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:0527⟧ :: ⟦g:054f⟧ : ⟦g:054f⟧ (def. V.5).

Next, let there be four magnitudes,

⟦g:051d⟧, ⟦g:056c⟧, ⟦g:0527⟧, ⟦g:054f⟧,

and other four,

⟦g:0570⟧, ⟦g:052c⟧, ⟦g:0573⟧, ⟦g:0574⟧,

which, when taken two and two in a cross order, have the same ratio;

namely, ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:056c⟧ :: ⟦g:0573⟧ : ⟦g:0574⟧ ,

⟦g:056c⟧ : ⟦g:0527⟧ :: ⟦g:052c⟧ : ⟦g:0573⟧ ,

and ⟦g:0527⟧ : ⟦g:054f⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ ,

then shall ⟦g:0527⟧ : ⟦g:054f⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ .

For, ∵ ⟦g:051d⟧ , ⟦g:056c⟧ , ⟦g:0527⟧ are three magnitudes,

and ⟦g:052c⟧ , ⟦g:0573⟧ , ⟦g:0574⟧ , other three,

which, taken two and two in a cross order have the same ratio,

therefore, by the first case, ⟦g:051d⟧ : ⟦g:0527⟧ :: ⟦g:052c⟧ : ⟦g:0574⟧ ,

but ⟦g:0527⟧ : ⟦g:054f⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ ,

therefore again, by the first case,

⟦g:0527⟧ : ⟦g:054f⟧ :: ⟦g:0570⟧ : ⟦g:052c⟧ ;

and so on, whatever be the number of such magnitudes.
