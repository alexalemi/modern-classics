Proposition III.36 (Theorem)

If *from a point without a circle two straight lines be drawn to it, one of which ⟦g:0441⟧ is a tangent to the circle, and the other ⟦g:0442⟧ cuts it; the rectangle under the whole cutting line ⟦g:0442⟧ and the external segment ⟦g:0443⟧ is equal to the square of the tangent ⟦g:0441⟧.*

[Figure afs]

 Figure I.

Let ⟦g:0442⟧ pass through the centre;

draw ⟦g:0445⟧ from the centre to the point of contact;

⟦g:0441⟧ ² = ⟦g:0446⟧ ² − ⟦g:0445⟧ ² (prop. I.47),

or ⟦g:0441⟧ ² = ⟦g:0446⟧ ² − ⟦g:0447⟧ ²,

∴ ⟦g:0441⟧ ² = ⟦g:0442⟧ × ⟦g:0443⟧ (prop. II.6).

[Figure aft]

 Figure II.

If ⟦g:0449⟧ do not pass through the centre,

draw ⟦g:044a⟧ and ⟦g:044b⟧.

Then ⟦g:0449⟧ × ⟦g:044c⟧ = ⟦g:044d⟧ ² − ⟦g:044b⟧ ² (prop. II.6),

that is, ⟦g:0449⟧ × ⟦g:044c⟧ = ⟦g:044d⟧ ² − ⟦g:0445⟧ ²,

∴ ⟦g:0449⟧ × ⟦g:044c⟧ = ⟦g:0441⟧ ² (prop. III.18).

Q. E. D.
