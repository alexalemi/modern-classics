Proposition I.28 (Theorem)

[Figure acd]

If *a straight line (⟦g:0156⟧), cutting two other straight lines (⟦g:00bc⟧ and ⟦g:0157⟧), makes the external equal to the internal and opposite angle, at the same side of the cutting line (namely* ⟦g:0158⟧ = ⟦g:0159⟧ *or* ⟦g:015a⟧ = ⟦g:015b⟧ *), or if it makes the two internal angles at the same side (⟦g:015b⟧ and ⟦g:015c⟧, or ⟦g:0159⟧ and ⟦g:015d⟧) together equal to two right angles, those two straight lines are parallel.*

First, if ⟦g:0158⟧ = ⟦g:0159⟧ , then ⟦g:0158⟧ = ⟦g:015c⟧ (prop. I.15),

∴ ⟦g:0159⟧ = ⟦g:015c⟧ ∴ ⟦g:00bc⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0157⟧ (prop. I.27).

Secondly, if ⟦g:0159⟧ + ⟦g:015d⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ ,

then ⟦g:015d⟧ + ⟦g:015c⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ (prop. I.13),

∴ ⟦g:0159⟧ + ⟦g:015d⟧ = ⟦g:015d⟧ + ⟦g:015c⟧ (ax. I.3)

∴ ⟦g:0159⟧ = ⟦g:015c⟧

∴ ⟦g:00bc⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0157⟧ (prop. I.27).

Q. E. D.
