Proposition IV.8 (Problem)

[Figure agi]

To *inscribe a circle in a given square.*

Make ⟦g:00c3⟧ = ⟦g:03e3⟧ ,

and ⟦g:0046⟧ = ⟦g:03e4⟧ ,

draw ⟦g:04a7⟧ ∥ ⟦g:04a8⟧ ,

and ⟦g:04a9⟧ ∥ ⟦g:04aa⟧

(prop. I.31)

∴ ⟦g:04ab⟧ is a parallelogram;

and since ⟦g:04a8⟧ = ⟦g:04aa⟧ (hyp.)

⟦g:00c3⟧ = ⟦g:03e4⟧

∴ ⟦g:04ab⟧ is equilateral (prop. I.34)

In like manner it can be shown that ⟦g:04ac⟧ = ⟦g:04ad⟧ are equilateral parallelograms;

∴ ⟦g:03e3⟧ = ⟦g:04ae⟧ = ⟦g:0042⟧ = ⟦g:0045⟧ .

and therefore if a circle be described from the concourse of these lines with any one of them as radius, it will be inscribed in the given square (prop. III.16).

Q. E. D.
