Proposition VI.3 (Theorem)

[Figure agv: The diagram for Proposition VI.3, in Byrne’s colours.]

Aright *line (⟦g:0030⟧) bisecting the angle of a triangle, divides the opposite side into segments (⟦g:003e⟧, ⟦g:019e⟧) proportional to the conterminous sides (⟦g:00ab⟧, ⟦g:000d⟧).And if a straight line (⟦g:0030⟧) drawn from any angle of a triangle divide the opposite side (⟦g:0584⟧) into segments (⟦g:003e⟧, ⟦g:019e⟧) proportional to the conterminous sides (⟦g:00ab⟧, ⟦g:000d⟧), it bisects the angle.*

 Part I.

Draw ⟦g:0585⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0030⟧ , to meet ⟦g:0586⟧;

then, ⟦g:0587⟧ = ⟦g:0588⟧ (prop. I.29).

∴ ⟦g:0589⟧ = ⟦g:0588⟧ ; but ⟦g:0589⟧ = ⟦g:058a⟧ , ∴ ⟦g:058a⟧ = ⟦g:0588⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:0586⟧ = ⟦g:000d⟧ (prop. I.6);

and ∵ ⟦g:0030⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0585⟧ ,

⟦g:0586⟧ : ⟦g:00ab⟧ :: ⟦g:019e⟧ : ⟦g:003e⟧ (prop. VI.2);

but ⟦g:0586⟧ = ⟦g:000d⟧ ;

∴ ⟦g:000d⟧ : ⟦g:00ab⟧ :: ⟦g:019e⟧ : ⟦g:003e⟧ (prop. V.7).

 Part II.

Let the same construction remain,

and ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:0586⟧ :: ⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:019e⟧ (prop. VI.2);

but ⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:019e⟧ :: ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:000d⟧ (hyp.)

∴ ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:0586⟧ :: ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:000d⟧ (prop. V.11).

and ∴ ⟦g:0586⟧ = ⟦g:000d⟧ (prop. V.9),

and ∴ ⟦g:0588⟧ = ⟦g:058a⟧ (prop. I.5);

but since ⟦g:0030⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0585⟧ ; ⟦g:0589⟧ = ⟦g:058a⟧ ,

and ⟦g:0587⟧ = ⟦g:0588⟧ (prop. I.29);

∴ ⟦g:058a⟧ = ⟦g:0588⟧ , and ⟦g:0587⟧ = ⟦g:0589⟧ ,

and ∴ ⟦g:0030⟧ bisects ⟦g:058b⟧.

Q. E. D.
