Proposition I.16 (Theorem)

[Figure abq: The diagram for Proposition I.16, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *a side of a triangle (⟦g:00ce⟧) is produced, the external angle (⟦g:00cf⟧) is greater than either of the internal remote angles (⟦g:00d0⟧ or ⟦g:00d1⟧).*

Make ⟦g:0050⟧ = ⟦g:00d2⟧ (prop. I.10);

Draw ⟦g:00a0⟧ and produce it until ⟦g:00d3⟧ = ⟦g:00a0⟧ ;

draw ⟦g:00d4⟧.

In ⟦g:00d5⟧ and ⟦g:00d6⟧;

⟦g:0050⟧ = ⟦g:00d2⟧ , ⟦g:00d7⟧ = ⟦g:00d8⟧ and ⟦g:00a0⟧ = ⟦g:00d3⟧ (const. and prop. I.15),

∴ ⟦g:00d0⟧ = ⟦g:00d9⟧ (prop. I.4),

∴ ⟦g:00cf⟧ > ⟦g:00d0⟧ .

In like manner it can be shown, that if ⟦g:00da⟧ be produced, ⟦g:00db⟧ > ⟦g:00d1⟧

and therefore ⟦g:00cf⟧ which is = ⟦g:00db⟧ is > ⟦g:00d1⟧ .

Q. E. D.
