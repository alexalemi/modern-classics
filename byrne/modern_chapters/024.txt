Proposition I.19 (Theorem)

[Figure abt: The diagram for Proposition I.19, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *in any triangle ⟦g:00ea⟧ one angle ⟦g:00eb⟧ be greater than another ⟦g:00ec⟧ the side ⟦g:00ed⟧ which is opposite to the greater angle, is greater than the side ⟦g:00ee⟧ opposite the less.*

If ⟦g:00ed⟧ be not greater than ⟦g:00ee⟧ then must

⟦g:00ed⟧ = or < ⟦g:00ee⟧ .

If ⟦g:00ed⟧ = ⟦g:00ee⟧ then

⟦g:00eb⟧ = ⟦g:00ec⟧ (prop. I.5);

which is contrary to the hypothesis.

⟦g:00ed⟧ is not less than ⟦g:00ee⟧; for if it were,

⟦g:00eb⟧ < ⟦g:00ec⟧ (prop. I.18)

which is contrary to the hypothesis:

∴ ⟦g:00ed⟧ > ⟦g:00ee⟧ .

Q. E. D.
