Proposition I.41 (Theorem)

[Figure acq: The diagram for Proposition I.41, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *a parallelogram ⟦g:01bf⟧ and a triangle ⟦g:01c0⟧ are upon the same base ⟦g:003e⟧ and between the same parallels ⟦g:01c1⟧ and ⟦g:003e⟧, the parallelogram is double the triangle.*

Draw ⟦g:00f1⟧ the diagonal.

Then ⟦g:01c2⟧ = ⟦g:01c0⟧ (prop. I.37)

⟦g:01bf⟧ = twice ⟦g:01c2⟧ (prop. I.34)

∴ ⟦g:01bf⟧ = twice ⟦g:01c0⟧ .

Q. E. D.
