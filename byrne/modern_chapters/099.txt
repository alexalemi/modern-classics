Proposition III.30 (Problem)

[Figure afi: The diagram for Proposition III.30, in Byrne’s colours.]

To *bisect a given arc ⟦g:03fe⟧.*

Draw ⟦g:03ff⟧;

make ⟦g:00aa⟧ = ⟦g:01c6⟧ ,

draw ⟦g:0400⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:03ff⟧ , and it bisects the arc.

Draw ⟦g:00b6⟧ and ⟦g:0330⟧.

⟦g:00aa⟧ = ⟦g:01c6⟧ (const.)

⟦g:0400⟧ is common,

and ⟦g:0401⟧ = ⟦g:0402⟧ (const.)

∴ ⟦g:00b6⟧ = ⟦g:0330⟧ (prop. I.4)

⟦g:0403⟧ = ⟦g:0404⟧ (prop. III.28),

and therefore the given arc is bisected.

Q. E. D.
