Proposition VI.33 (Theorem)

[Figure ahz: The diagram for Proposition VI.33, in Byrne’s colours.]

In *equal circles (⟦g:02e5⟧, ⟦g:06ae⟧), angles, whether at the centre or circumference, are in the same ratio to one another as the arcs on which they stand (* ⟦g:06af⟧ : ⟦g:06b0⟧ :: ⟦g:06b1⟧ : ⟦g:06b2⟧ *); so also are sectors.*

Take in the circumference of ⟦g:02e5⟧ any number of arcs ⟦g:06b3⟧, ⟦g:06b4⟧, &c. each = ⟦g:06b1⟧ , and also in the circumference of ⟦g:06ae⟧ take any number of arcs ⟦g:06b5⟧, ⟦g:06b6⟧, &c. each = ⟦g:06b2⟧ , draw the radii to the extremities of the equal arcs.

The since the arcs ⟦g:06b1⟧, ⟦g:06b3⟧, ⟦g:06b4⟧, &c. are all equal, the angles ⟦g:06af⟧, ⟦g:06b7⟧, ⟦g:06b8⟧, &c. are also equal (prop. III.27); ∴ ⟦g:06b9⟧ is the same multiple of ⟦g:06af⟧ which the arc ⟦g:06ba⟧ is of ⟦g:06b1⟧; and in the same manner ⟦g:06bb⟧ is the same multiple of ⟦g:06b0⟧, which the arc ⟦g:06bc⟧ is of the arc ⟦g:06b2⟧.

Then it is evident (prop. III.27),

if ⟦g:06b9⟧ (or if m times ⟦g:06af⟧) >, =, < ⟦g:06bb⟧ (or n times ⟦g:06b0⟧)

then ⟦g:06ba⟧(or m times ⟦g:06b1⟧) >, =, < ⟦g:06bc⟧ (or n times ⟦g:06b2⟧);

∴ ⟦g:06af⟧ : ⟦g:06b0⟧ :: ⟦g:06b1⟧ : ⟦g:06b2⟧ (def. V.5), or the angles at the centre are as the arcs on which they stand; but the angles at the circumference being halves of the angles at the centre (prop. III.20) are in the same ratio (prop. V.15), and therefore are as the arcs on which they stand.

It is evident, that sectors in equal circles, and on equal arcs are equal (prop. I.4, III.24 and III.27 and def. III.9). Hence, if the sectors be substituted for the angles in the above demonstration, the second part of the proportion will be established, that is, in equal circles the sectors have the same ratio to one another as the arcs on which they stand.

Q. E. D.
