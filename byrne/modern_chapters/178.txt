Proposition VI.18 (Problem)

[Figure ahk: The diagram for Proposition VI.18, in Byrne’s colours.]

On *a given straight line (⟦g:01d4⟧) to construct a rectilinear figure similar to a given one (⟦g:0610⟧) and similarly placed.*

Resolve the given figure into triangles by drawing the lines ⟦g:02f3⟧ and ⟦g:0611⟧.

At the extremities of ⟦g:01d4⟧ make

⟦g:0612⟧ = ⟦g:0613⟧ and ⟦g:0614⟧ = ⟦g:0615⟧ ;

again at the extremities of ⟦g:017e⟧ make

⟦g:0616⟧ = ⟦g:0617⟧ and ⟦g:0618⟧ = ⟦g:0619⟧ ;

in like manner make

⟦g:061a⟧ = ⟦g:061b⟧ and ⟦g:061c⟧ = ⟦g:061d⟧ .

Then ⟦g:0610⟧ = ⟦g:061e⟧ .

It is evident from the construction and (prop. I.32) that the figures are equiangular; and since the triangles ⟦g:061f⟧ and ⟦g:0620⟧ are equiangular;

then by (prop. VI.4), ⟦g:01d4⟧ : ⟦g:00f0⟧ :: ⟦g:00a4⟧ : ⟦g:0621⟧

and ⟦g:00f0⟧ : ⟦g:017e⟧ :: ⟦g:0621⟧ : ⟦g:02f3⟧

Again, because ⟦g:0622⟧ and ⟦g:0623⟧ are equiangular,

⟦g:017e⟧ : ⟦g:0304⟧ :: ⟦g:02f3⟧ : ⟦g:000d⟧

∴ ex æquali,

⟦g:00f0⟧ : ⟦g:0304⟧ :: ⟦g:0621⟧ : ⟦g:000d⟧ (prop. V.22)

In like manner it may be shown that the remaining sides of the two figures are proportional.

∴ by (prop. VI.1)

⟦g:061e⟧ is similar to ⟦g:0610⟧ and similarly situated; and on the given line ⟦g:01d4⟧.

Q. E. D.
