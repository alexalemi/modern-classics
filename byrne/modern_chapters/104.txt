Proposition III.35 (Theorem)

If *two chords* ⟬{⟦g:042b⟧‖⟦g:042c⟧}⟭ *in a circle intersect each other, the rectangle contained by the segments of the one is equal to the rectangle contained by the segments of the other.*

 Figure I.

[Figure afp: The diagram for Proposition III.35, in Byrne’s colours.]

If the given right lines pass through the centre, they are bisected in the point of intersection, hence the rectangles under their segments are squares of their halves, and therefore equal.
