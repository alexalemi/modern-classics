Proposition VI.2 (Theorem)

[Figure agu: The diagram for Proposition VI.2, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *a straight line ⟦g:0472⟧ be drawn parallel to any side ⟦g:057e⟧ of a triangle, it shall cut the other sides, or those sides produced, into proportional segmentsAnd if any straight line ⟦g:0472⟧ divide the sides of a triangle, or those sides produced, into proportional segments, it is parallel to the remaining side ⟦g:057e⟧.*

⟦g:019d⟧ : ⟦g:0316⟧ :: ⟦g:057f⟧ : ⟦g:0330⟧ .

 Part II.
