Proposition VI.16 (Theorem)

[Figure ahi: The diagram for Proposition VI.16, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *four straight lines be proportional (* ⟦g:00bb⟧ : ⟦g:0086⟧ :: ⟦g:017f⟧ : ⟦g:01b2⟧ *) the rectangle (* ⟦g:00bb⟧ × ⟦g:01b2⟧ *) contained by the extremes, is equal to the rectangle (* ⟦g:0086⟧ × ⟦g:017f⟧ *) contained by the means.And if the rectangle contained by the extremes be equal to the rectangle contained by the means, the four straight lines are proportional.*

 Part I.

From the extremities of ⟦g:00bb⟧ and ⟦g:0086⟧ draw ⟦g:0174⟧ and ⟦g:017e⟧ ⊥ to them and = ⟦g:01b2⟧ and ⟦g:017f⟧ respectively:

complete the parallelograms ⟦g:060c⟧ and ⟦g:060d⟧.

and since,

⟦g:00bb⟧ : ⟦g:0086⟧ :: ⟦g:017f⟧ : ⟦g:01b2⟧ (hyp.)

∴ ⟦g:00bb⟧ : ⟦g:0086⟧ :: ⟦g:017e⟧ : ⟦g:0174⟧ (const.)

∴ ⟦g:060c⟧ = ⟦g:060d⟧ (prop. VI.14),

that is, the rectangle contained be the extremes, equal to the rectangle contained be the means.

 Part II.

Let the same construction remain;

∵ ⟦g:01b2⟧ = ⟦g:0174⟧ , ⟦g:060c⟧ = ⟦g:060d⟧

and ⟦g:017e⟧ = ⟦g:017f⟧ .

∴ ⟦g:00bb⟧ : ⟦g:0086⟧ :: ⟦g:017e⟧ : ⟦g:0174⟧ (prop. VI.14)

But ⟦g:017e⟧ = ⟦g:017f⟧ ,

and ⟦g:0174⟧ = ⟦g:01b2⟧ (const.)

∴ ⟦g:00bb⟧ : ⟦g:0086⟧ :: ⟦g:017f⟧ : ⟦g:01b2⟧ (prop. V.7).

Q. E. D.
