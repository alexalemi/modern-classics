Proposition I.15 (Theorem)

[Figure abp: The diagram for Proposition I.15, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *two right lines (⟦g:004f⟧ and ⟦g:00a3⟧) intersect one another, the vertical angles ⟦g:00c9⟧ and ⟦g:00ca⟧, ⟦g:00cb⟧ and ⟦g:00cc⟧ are equal.*

⟦g:00c9⟧ + ⟦g:00cb⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧

⟦g:00ca⟧ + ⟦g:00cb⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧

∴ ⟦g:00c9⟧ = ⟦g:00ca⟧ .

In the same manner it may be shown that

⟦g:00cb⟧ = ⟦g:00cc⟧ .

Q. E. D.
