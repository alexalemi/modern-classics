Proposition VI.19 (Theorem)

[Figure ahl: The diagram for Proposition VI.19, in Byrne’s colours.]

Similar *triangles (⟦g:0625⟧ and ⟦g:0626⟧) are to one another in the duplicate ratio of their homologous sides.*

Let ⟦g:0627⟧ and ⟦g:0628⟧ be equal angles, and ⟦g:0629⟧ and ⟦g:037e⟧ homologous sides of the similar triangles ⟦g:0625⟧ and ⟦g:0626⟧ and on ⟦g:0629⟧ the greater of these lines take ⟦g:062a⟧ a third proportional, so that

⟦g:0629⟧ : ⟦g:037e⟧ :: ⟦g:037e⟧ : ⟦g:062a⟧ ;

draw ⟦g:033a⟧.

⟦g:0629⟧ : ⟦g:000d⟧ :: ⟦g:037e⟧ : ⟦g:00ab⟧ (prop. VI.4);

∴ ⟦g:0629⟧ : ⟦g:037e⟧ :: ⟦g:000d⟧ : ⟦g:00ab⟧ (prop. V.16),

but ⟦g:0629⟧ : ⟦g:037e⟧ :: ⟦g:037e⟧ : ⟦g:062a⟧ (const.),

∴ ⟦g:037e⟧ : ⟦g:062a⟧ :: ⟦g:000d⟧ : ⟦g:00ab⟧

consequently ⟦g:0625⟧ = ⟦g:062b⟧ for they have the sides about the equal angles ⟦g:0627⟧ and ⟦g:0628⟧ reciprocally proportional (prop. VI.15);

∴ ⟦g:0626⟧ : ⟦g:0625⟧ :: ⟦g:0626⟧ : ⟦g:062b⟧ (prop. V.7);

but ⟦g:0626⟧ : ⟦g:062b⟧ :: ⟦g:0629⟧ : ⟦g:062a⟧ (prop. VI.1),

∴ ⟦g:0626⟧ : ⟦g:0625⟧ :: ⟦g:0629⟧ : ⟦g:062a⟧ ,

that is to say, the triangles are to one another in the duplicate ratio of their homologous sides ⟦g:037e⟧ and ⟦g:0629⟧ (def. V.11).

Q. E. D.
