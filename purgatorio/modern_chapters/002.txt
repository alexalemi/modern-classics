Canto Three

	Although that sudden flight
	had scattered them across the plain,
	turned to the mountain where justice probes us,

	I drew close to my faithful companion.
	And how could I have run on without him?
	Who would have brought me up the mountain?

	He seemed to me to be gnawing at himself.
	Oh dignified and clean conscience,
	how bitterly a small fault bites you!

	When his feet gave up the haste
	that takes the dignity out of any act,
	my mind, which until then had been narrowed,

	widened its attention, as if hungry,
	and I set my face towards the hill
	that rises highest out of the water towards heaven.

	The sun, flaming red behind us,
	was broken in front of me by my own shape,
	because his rays came to rest on me.

	I turned to one side, afraid
	that I had been left alone, when I saw
	the ground darkened in front of me only,

	and my comfort, turning right round to me,
	began: "Why do you still distrust?
	Do you not believe I am with you and guiding you?

	It is evening already over there, where the body
	is buried inside which I used to cast a shadow.
	Naples has it; it was taken from Brindisi.

	Now, if nothing casts a shadow in front of me,
	do not wonder at it more than at the heavens,
	where one does not block the light of another.

	To suffer torments, heat and frost,
	the Power disposes bodies like this,
	and how it does it, it does not want revealed to us.

	Anyone is mad who hopes our reason
	can travel the endless road
	that one substance in three persons takes.

	Be content, human race, with the that;
	for if you had been able to see everything,
	there was no need for Mary to give birth,

	and you have seen men longing without fruit
	whose longing would otherwise have been quieted,
	which is given them eternally as their grief:

	I mean Aristotle, and Plato,
	and many others." And here he bent his head
	and said no more, and stayed troubled.

	Meanwhile we came to the foot of the mountain.
	There we found the rock so sheer
	that quick legs would have been no use on it.

	The most deserted, the most broken landslide
	between Lerici and Turbia is a stairway,
	compared with that, easy and open.

	"Now who knows which way the slope goes down,"
	said my master, stopping his step,
	"so that a man without wings can climb?"

	And while he kept his face lowered,
	examining in his mind what road to take,
	and I was looking up around the rock,

	on the left hand there appeared to me a crowd
	of souls, moving their feet towards us,
	and not seeming to, they came so slowly.

	"Master," I said, "lift your eyes:
	here are people who will give us advice,
	if you cannot get it out of yourself."

	He looked then, and with an easier air
	answered: "Let us go to them, since they come slowly;
	and you, sweet son, hold your hope firm."

	Those people were still as far off,
	after we had gone a thousand paces, I mean,
	as a good thrower could send a stone by hand,

	when they all pressed against the hard rocks
	of the high bank, and stood still and close together
	as a man stands to stare when he is in doubt.

	"You who have ended well, spirits already chosen,"
	Virgil began, "by that peace
	which I believe you are all waiting for,

	tell us where the mountain lies less steep,
	so that going up is possible;
	for the more a man knows, the more he hates losing time."

	As sheep come out of the fold
	by ones and twos and threes, and the rest
	stand timidly, with eye and muzzle to the ground,

	and what the first one does the others do,
	crowding up behind her if she stops,
	simple and quiet, and not knowing why;

	so I saw the head of that fortunate flock
	begin to move and come forward then,
	modest in the face and dignified in walking.

	When those in front saw the light
	broken on the ground at my right side,
	so that my shadow lay from me to the cliff,

	they stopped, and drew themselves back a little,
	and all the others coming on behind,
	not knowing why, did the same.

	"Without your asking, I admit to you
	that this is a human body you see,
	which is why the sunlight is split on the ground.

	Do not be astonished, but believe
	that it is not without power come from heaven
	that he seeks to get over this wall."

	So my master; and those worthy people
	said: "Turn back, then, and go on ahead of us,"
	making the sign with the backs of their hands.

	And one of them began: "Whoever
	you are, turn your face as you go:
	consider whether you ever saw me over there."

	I turned towards him and looked at him hard:
	he was fair and handsome and of noble bearing,
	but a blow had split one of his eyebrows.

	When I had humbly denied
	ever having seen him, he said: "Now look";
	and he showed me a wound at the top of his chest.

	Then, smiling, he said: "I am Manfred,
	grandson of the Empress Constance;
	so I beg you, when you go back,

	to go to my beautiful daughter, mother
	of the honour of Sicily and of Aragon,
	and tell her the truth, if anything else is said.

	After my body had been broken
	by two mortal wounds, I gave myself up,
	weeping, to the one who forgives willingly.

	My sins were horrible;
	but infinite goodness has such wide arms
	that it takes in whatever turns back to it.

	If the shepherd of Cosenza, who was set
	on hunting me down by Clement then,
	had read this page of God properly,

	the bones of my body would still be
	at the head of the bridge near Benevento,
	under the guard of the heavy cairn.

	Now the rain washes them and the wind moves them,
	outside the kingdom, almost along the Verde,
	where he carried them with the candles put out.

	No curse of theirs so loses a man
	that eternal love cannot come back to him,
	as long as hope has anything green in flower.

	It is true that whoever dies in defiance
	of Holy Church, even if he repents at the end,
	must stay outside this bank

	thirty times for every season he spent
	in his presumption, unless such a decree
	is shortened by good prayers.

	See now whether you can make me glad,
	telling my good Constance how you have seen me,
	and about this ban as well;

	for here we gain a great deal by those over there."
