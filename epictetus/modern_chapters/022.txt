Chapter 21: Against Those Who Want to Be Admired

When a man holds the position in life that properly belongs to him, he does not stand about gaping after things outside it. Man, what is it you want to happen to you? I am satisfied if I desire and avoid in line with nature, if I move toward a thing and away from it as I am naturally formed to do, and likewise in my intentions and my plans and my assent.

Then why do you strut in front of us as though you had swallowed a spit?

"What I have always wanted is that the people who meet me should admire me, and the people following me should exclaim: what a great philosopher."

And who are these people you want to be admired by? Are they not the very people you are in the habit of calling mad? Well then — do you want to be admired by madmen?
