DECISIONS LOG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

--- FORM: 4,720 lines in 34 cantos, tercet for tercet, unrhymed. The
argument is in prep.py's docstring and in text_analysis.txt. check.py
compares the tercet count, the lines within each tercet and the total
per canto EXACTLY against chapters/, and requires every line to keep
its tab — one lost tab turns a tercet into a paragraph and the ratio
does not move.

--- RATIO 1.19, and it belongs where the FR/IT prose novels do not.
Italian verse is far more compressed than English (32,706 -> 38,787
words), so verify runs at --min-ratio 1.05 --max-ratio 1.35. That is
well below ovid/'s 1.4-2.4 for Latin hexameter and well above the
0.9-1.5 of the Verne novels. Under 1.05 in any file would mean the
tercets had been squeezed.

--- V: GALEOTTO IS RENDERED FOR SENSE. "Galeotto fu 'l libro e chi lo
scrisse" names Gallehault, the go-between of the Lancelot romance the
lovers are reading. Keeping the name preserves a proper noun and
destroys the accusation, because no modern reader knows him; the line
before names Lancelot, so the context is already there. Rendered "The
book was our go-between, and so was the man who wrote it." Pinned.

--- XXXIII: UGOLINO'S LAST LINE KEEPS ITS AMBIGUITY. "Poscia, più che
'l dolor, poté 'l digiuno" can mean that starvation finished what grief
had not, or that he ate his children. Dante will not say. Any rendering
that decides is wrong in either direction, so the pin is written to
hold the ambiguity open: "Then fasting had more power than grief."

--- XXXIV: LUCIFER'S THIRD FACE. The left face is "such as those who
come from where the Nile flows down". This is DANTE'S OWN PERIPHRASIS
for a colour, in a three-coloured description that is the infernal
parody of the Trinity, and the third colour is structurally
load-bearing. Kept exactly as he writes it — no more and no less than
the Italian says — and no note. Not the mill/burke case: there a slur
NOUN stood in for a relationship and the claim survived the swap;
here there is no slur and no claim, only Dante naming a colour by
pointing at a place. Compare the grimm ruling on "The White Bride and
the Black One", where the colour is likewise the author's subject.

--- THE VERNE RULE, THROUGHOUT, WITH NO NOTE ANYWHERE IN THE VOLUME.
Canto Four damns the virtuous pagans, Virgil included, for lacking
baptism, and Virgil says so about himself. Canto Nineteen puts a Pope
head-down in a hole and blames the Donation of Constantine for the
corruption of the Church. Canto Twenty-Eight shows Muhammad and Ali
split open, in the most gruesome passage in the poem, and it is
translated in full, with gravity, and neither softened nor
sensationalised. Canto Twenty-Three has Caiaphas crucified on the
ground and calls his council "a bad seed for the Jews". Canto
Thirty-Four has Brutus and Cassius in Satan's mouths beside Judas,
which is Dante's politics, not an oversight. All of it stands as his.

--- XXI-XXIII ARE COMEDY AND MUST BE PLAYED STRAIGHT. Ten named devils
with a leader who signals the march by farting; a grafter who cons them
into letting him dive; two of them falling into the pitch in a rage and
having to be fished out. The register is slapstick with real cruelty
underneath it and must not be flattened into "epic". Equally, XXXII to
XXXIV must not be warmed up: the lowest circle goes flat, cold and
quiet, and Ugolino's canto works because nothing in it is raised.

--- ENJAMBMENT ACROSS A TERCET BOUNDARY IS KEPT WHERE DANTE HAS IT
(I.13-15, VIII.43 into the last line, XXVII.45 into the last line).
Tidying a sentence back inside its tercet is the commonest way to
smooth this poem into something slower than it is.

--- THE PROJECT'S MARKUP RULES ARE TIGHT HERE AND WERE WATCHED FOR: no
all-caps line anywhere (assemble.py would set it as a heading), no
asterisks or underscores (the Italian carries no emphasis at all, so
any marker would be an invention), and every line tab-indented so that
both renderers set it as verse. check.py tests all three.
