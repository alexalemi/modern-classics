Gustave Doré's engraving *I Found Myself Within a Forest Dark* (1861), the first plate of his Inferno. A single small figure in a long robe stands in a break of light at the foot of enormous trunks, looking back over his shoulder, with tangled undergrowth closing in around him and darkness behind. Doré's plates are the pictures most readers have of Dante's Hell, and this one is the poem's first sentence: a man in the middle of his life, lost in a wood.
