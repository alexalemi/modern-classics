Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Byrne's own figure for Proposition I.47: a right-angled triangle with a square on each side, the square on the hypotenuse divided by the line from the right angle into two parts, each equal to one of the other squares. The colours are Byrne's own red, yellow, blue and black, which he used in place of letters, and the line at the top is from his title page.
