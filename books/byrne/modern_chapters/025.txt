Proposition I.20 (Theorem)

[Figure abu: The diagram for Proposition I.20, in Byrne’s colours.]

Any *two sides ⟦g:00f0⟧ and ⟦g:00f1⟧ of a triangle ⟦g:00f2⟧ taken together are greater than the third side (⟦g:00f3⟧).*

Produce ⟦g:00f0⟧, and

make ⟦g:00f4⟧ = ⟦g:00f1⟧ (prop. I.3);

draw ⟦g:00f5⟧.

Then ∵ ⟦g:00f4⟧ = ⟦g:00f1⟧ (const.),

⟦g:00f6⟧ = ⟦g:00f7⟧ (prop. I.5)

∴ ⟦g:00f8⟧ > ⟦g:00f7⟧ (ax. I.9)

∴ ⟦g:00f0⟧ + ⟦g:00f4⟧ > ⟦g:00f3⟧ (prop. I.19)

and ∴ ⟦g:00f0⟧ + ⟦g:00f1⟧ > ⟦g:00f3⟧ .

Q. E. D.
