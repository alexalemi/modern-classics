Proposition IV.10 (Problem)

[Figure agk: The diagram for Proposition IV.10, in Byrne’s colours.]

To *construct an isosceles triangle, in which each of the angles at the base shall be double of the vertical angle.*

Take any straight line ⟦g:04bc⟧

and divide it so that ⟦g:04bc⟧ × ⟦g:04bd⟧ = ⟦g:04be⟧ ² (prop. II.11)

With ⟦g:04bc⟧ as radius, describe ⟦g:04bf⟧ and place in it from the extremity of the radius, ⟦g:04c0⟧ = ⟦g:04be⟧ (prop. IV.1);

draw ⟦g:04c1⟧.

Then ⟦g:04c2⟧ is the required triangle.

For, draw ⟦g:04c3⟧ and describe ⟦g:04c4⟧ about ⟦g:04c5⟧ (prop. IV.5)

Since ⟦g:04bc⟧ × ⟦g:04bd⟧ = ⟦g:04be⟧ ² = ⟦g:04c0⟧ ²,

∴ ⟦g:04c0⟧ is tangent to ⟦g:04c4⟧ (prop. III.37)

∴ ⟦g:04c6⟧ = ⟦g:04c7⟧ (prop. III.32),

add ⟦g:04c8⟧ to each, ∴ ⟦g:04c6⟧ + ⟦g:04c8⟧ = ⟦g:04c7⟧ + ⟦g:04c8⟧ ;

but ⟦g:04c6⟧ + ⟦g:04c8⟧ or ⟦g:04c9⟧ = ⟦g:04ca⟧ (prop. I.5):

since ⟦g:04c1⟧ = ⟦g:04bc⟧ (prop. I.5)

consequently ⟦g:04ca⟧ = ⟦g:04c7⟧ + ⟦g:04c8⟧ = ⟦g:04cb⟧ (prop. I.32)

∴ ⟦g:04c3⟧ = ⟦g:04c0⟧ (prop. I.6)

∴ ⟦g:04c0⟧ = ⟦g:04be⟧ = ⟦g:04c3⟧ (const.)

∴ ⟦g:04c7⟧ = ⟦g:04c8⟧ (prop. I.5)

∴ ⟦g:04ca⟧ = ⟦g:04c9⟧ = ⟦g:04cb⟧ = ⟦g:04c7⟧ + ⟦g:04c8⟧ = twice ⟦g:04c7⟧ ;

and consequently each angle at the base is double of the vertical angle.

Q. E. D.
