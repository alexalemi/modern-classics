Proposition IV.12 (Problem)

[Figure agm: The diagram for Proposition IV.12, in Byrne’s colours.]

To *describe an equilateral and equiangular pentagon about a given circle ⟦g:04d8⟧.*

Draw five tangents through the vertices of the angles of any regular pentagon inscribed in the given circle ⟦g:04d8⟧ (prop. III.17).

These five tangents will form the required pentagon.

Draw ⟬{⟦g:04d9⟧‖⟦g:04da⟧‖⟦g:04db⟧‖⟦g:04dc⟧‖⟦g:04dd⟧}⟭.

In ⟦g:04de⟧ and ⟦g:04df⟧

⟦g:04e0⟧ = ⟦g:04e1⟧ (prop. I.47)

⟦g:04db⟧ = ⟦g:04d9⟧ , and ⟦g:04da⟧ common;

∴ ⟦g:04e2⟧ = ⟦g:04e3⟧ and ∴ ⟦g:04e4⟧ = ⟦g:04e5⟧ (prop. I.8)

∴ ⟦g:04e6⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e3⟧ , and ⟦g:04e7⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e5⟧ .

In the same manner it can be demonstrated that

⟦g:04e8⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e9⟧ , and ⟦g:04ea⟧ = twice ⟦g:04eb⟧ ;

but ⟦g:04e7⟧ = ⟦g:04ea⟧ (prop. III.27),

∴ their halves ⟦g:04e5⟧ = ⟦g:04eb⟧ , also ⟦g:04ec⟧ = ⟦g:04ed⟧ ,

and ⟦g:04db⟧ common;

∴ ⟦g:04e3⟧ = ⟦g:04e9⟧ and ⟦g:04e1⟧ = ⟦g:04ee⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:04ef⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e1⟧ .

In the same manner it can be demonstrated that ⟦g:04f0⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e0⟧ ,

but ⟦g:04e0⟧ = ⟦g:04e1⟧

∴ ⟦g:04f0⟧ = ⟦g:04ef⟧ ;

In the same manner it can be demonstrated that the other sides are equal, and therefore the pentagon is equilateral, it is also equiangular, for

⟦g:04e8⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e9⟧ and ⟦g:04e6⟧ = twice ⟦g:04e3⟧ ,

and therefore ⟦g:04e3⟧ = ⟦g:04e9⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:04e8⟧ = ⟦g:04e6⟧ ;

in the same manner it can be demonstrated that the other angles of the described pentagon are equal.

Q. E. D.
