Proposition VI.21 (Theorem)

[Figure ahn: The diagram for Proposition VI.21, in Byrne’s colours.]

Rectilinear *figures (⟦g:0643⟧ and ⟦g:0644⟧) which are similar to the same figure (⟦g:0645⟧) are similar also to each other.*

Since ⟦g:0643⟧ and ⟦g:0645⟧ are similar, they are equiangular, and have the sides about the equal angles proportional (def. VI.1); and since the figures ⟦g:0644⟧ and ⟦g:0645⟧ are also similar, they are equiangular, and have the sides about the equal angles proportional; therefore ⟦g:0643⟧ and ⟦g:0644⟧ are also equiangular, and have the sides about the equal angles proportional (prop. V.11), and are therefore similar.

Q. E. D.
