Proposition VI.D (Theorem)

[Figure aid: The diagram for Proposition VI.D, in Byrne’s colours.]

The *rectangle contained by the diagonals of a quadrilateral figure inscribed in a circle, is equal to both the rectangles contained by its opposite sides.*

Let ⟦g:06ea⟧ be any quadrilateral figure inscribed in ⟦g:06eb⟧;

and draw ⟦g:06ec⟧ and ⟦g:037e⟧;

then ⟦g:06ec⟧ × ⟦g:037e⟧ = ⟦g:004b⟧ × ⟦g:004a⟧ + ⟦g:000d⟧ × ⟦g:06ed⟧ .

Make ⟦g:06ee⟧ = ⟦g:06ef⟧ (prop. I.23),

∴ ⟦g:06f0⟧ = ⟦g:06f1⟧ ; and ⟦g:06f2⟧ = ⟦g:06f3⟧ (prop. III.21);

∴ ⟦g:000d⟧ : ⟦g:01db⟧ :: ⟦g:037e⟧ : ⟦g:06ed⟧ (prop. VI.4);

and ∴ ⟦g:01db⟧ × ⟦g:037e⟧ = ⟦g:000d⟧ × ⟦g:06ed⟧ (prop. VI.16);

again, ∵ ⟦g:06ee⟧ = ⟦g:06ef⟧ (const.),

and ⟦g:06f4⟧ = ⟦g:06f5⟧ (prop. III.21);

∴ ⟦g:004b⟧ : ⟦g:032a⟧ :: ⟦g:037e⟧ : ⟦g:004a⟧ (prop. VI.4);

and ∴ ⟦g:032a⟧ × ⟦g:037e⟧ = ⟦g:004b⟧ × ⟦g:004a⟧ (prop. VI.16);

but, from above, ⟦g:01db⟧ × ⟦g:037e⟧ = ⟦g:000d⟧ × ⟦g:06ed⟧ ;

∴ ⟦g:06ec⟧ × ⟦g:037e⟧ = ⟦g:004b⟧ × ⟦g:004a⟧ + ⟦g:000d⟧ × ⟦g:06ed⟧ (prop. II.1).

Q. E. D.
