Proposition III.3 (Theorem)

[Figure adz: The diagram for Proposition III.3, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *a straight line (⟦g:0042⟧) drawn through the centre of a circle ⟦g:02ee⟧ bisects a chord (⟦g:02ef⟧) which does not pass through the centre, it is perpendicular to it; or, if perpendicular to it, it bisects it.*

Draw ⟦g:02f0⟧ and ⟦g:005a⟧ to the centre of the circle.

In ⟦g:02f1⟧ and ⟦g:02f2⟧

⟦g:02f0⟧ = ⟦g:005a⟧ , ⟦g:0042⟧ common,

and ⟦g:00a0⟧ = ⟦g:02f3⟧ ∴ ⟦g:02f4⟧ = ⟦g:02f5⟧ (prop. I.8)

and ∴ ⟦g:0042⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:02ef⟧ (def. I.10)

Again let ⟦g:0042⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:02ef⟧

Then in ⟦g:02f1⟧ and ⟦g:02f2⟧

⟦g:02f6⟧ = ⟦g:02f7⟧ (prop. I.5)

⟦g:02f4⟧ = ⟦g:02f5⟧ (hyp.)

and ⟦g:02f0⟧ = ⟦g:005a⟧

∴ ⟦g:00a0⟧ = ⟦g:02f3⟧ (prop. I.26)

and ∴ ⟦g:0042⟧ bisects ⟦g:02ef⟧.

Q. E. D.
