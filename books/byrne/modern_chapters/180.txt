Proposition VI.20 (Theorem)

[Figure ahm: The diagram for Proposition VI.20, in Byrne’s colours.]

Similar *polygons may be divided into the same number of similar triangles, each similar pair of which are proportional to the polygons; and the polygons are to each other in the duplicate ratio of their homologous sides.*

Draw ⟦g:0174⟧ and ⟦g:01b2⟧, and ⟦g:062d⟧ and ⟦g:062e⟧, resolving the polygons into triangles. Then because the polygons are similar, ⟦g:062f⟧ = ⟦g:0630⟧ , and ⟦g:010c⟧ : ⟦g:01da⟧ :: ⟦g:0631⟧ : ⟦g:0632⟧

∴ ⟦g:0633⟧ and ⟦g:0634⟧ are similar,

and ⟦g:0635⟧ = ⟦g:0636⟧ (prop. VI.6);

but ⟦g:0637⟧ = ⟦g:0638⟧ because they are angles of similar polygons; therefore the remainders ⟦g:0639⟧ and ⟦g:063a⟧ are equal;

hence ⟦g:01b2⟧ : ⟦g:01da⟧ :: ⟦g:062e⟧ : ⟦g:0632⟧ ,

on account of the similar triangles,

and ⟦g:01da⟧ : ⟦g:00bb⟧ :: ⟦g:0632⟧ : ⟦g:063b⟧ ,

on account of the similar polygons,

∴ ⟦g:01b2⟧ : ⟦g:00bb⟧ :: ⟦g:062e⟧ : ⟦g:063b⟧ ,

ex æquali (prop. V.22), and as these proportional sides contain equal angles, the triangles ⟦g:063c⟧ and ⟦g:063d⟧ are similar (prop. VI.6).

In like manner it may be shown that the triangles ⟦g:063e⟧ and ⟦g:063f⟧ are similar.

But ⟦g:0633⟧ is to ⟦g:0634⟧ in the duplicate ratio of ⟦g:01b2⟧ to ⟦g:062e⟧ (prop. VI.19),

and ⟦g:063c⟧ is to ⟦g:063d⟧ in like manner, in the duplicate ratio of ⟦g:01b2⟧ to ⟦g:062e⟧;

∴ ⟦g:0633⟧ : ⟦g:0634⟧ :: ⟦g:063c⟧ : ⟦g:063d⟧ (prop. V.11).

Again ⟦g:063c⟧ is to ⟦g:063d⟧ in the duplicate ratio of ⟦g:0174⟧ to ⟦g:062d⟧, and ⟦g:063e⟧ is to ⟦g:063f⟧ in the duplicate ratio of ⟦g:0174⟧ to ⟦g:062d⟧.

⟬⟦g:0633⟧ : ⟦g:0634⟧ :: ⟦g:063c⟧ : ⟦g:063d⟧‖:: ⟦g:063e⟧ : ⟦g:063f⟧⟭;

and as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so is the sum of all the antecedents to the sum of all the consequents; that is to say, the similar triangles have to one another the same ratio as the polygons (prop. V.12).

But ⟦g:0633⟧ is to ⟦g:0634⟧ in the duplicate ratio of ⟦g:010c⟧ to ⟦g:0631⟧;

∴ ⟦g:0640⟧ is to ⟦g:0641⟧ in the duplicate ratio of ⟦g:010c⟧ to ⟦g:0631⟧.

Q. E. D.
