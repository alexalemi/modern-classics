Proposition VI.32 (Theorem)

[Figure ahy: The diagram for Proposition VI.32, in Byrne’s colours.]

If *two triangles (⟦g:06a5⟧ and ⟦g:06a6⟧), have two sides proportional (* ⟦g:00ed⟧ : ⟦g:0411⟧ :: ⟦g:031c⟧ : ⟦g:00d3⟧ *), and be so placed at an angle that the homologous sides are parallel, the remaining sides (⟦g:00a5⟧ and ⟦g:0611⟧) form one right line.*

Since ⟦g:00ed⟧ ∥ ⟦g:031c⟧ ,

⟦g:06a7⟧ = ⟦g:06a8⟧ (prop. I.29);

and also since ⟦g:0411⟧ ∥ ⟦g:00d3⟧ ,

⟦g:06a8⟧ = ⟦g:06a9⟧ (prop. I.29);

∴ ⟦g:06a7⟧ = ⟦g:06a9⟧ ;

and since ⟦g:00ed⟧ : ⟦g:0411⟧ :: ⟦g:031c⟧ : ⟦g:00d3⟧ (hyp.),

the triangles are equiangular (prop. VI.6);

∴ ⟦g:06aa⟧ = ⟦g:06ab⟧ ;

but ⟦g:06a7⟧ = ⟦g:06a8⟧ ;

∴ ⟦g:06ac⟧ + ⟦g:06a8⟧ + ⟦g:06ab⟧ = ⟦g:06ac⟧ + ⟦g:06a7⟧ + ⟦g:06aa⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ (prop. I.32),

and ∴ ⟦g:00a5⟧ and ⟦g:0611⟧ lie in the same straight line (prop. I.14).

Q. E. D.
