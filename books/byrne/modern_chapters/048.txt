Proposition I.43 (Theorem)

[Figure acs: The diagram for Proposition I.43, in Byrne’s colours.]

The *complements ⟦g:01cd⟧ and ⟦g:01ce⟧ of the parallelograms which are about the diagonal of a parallelogram are equal.*

⟦g:01cf⟧ = ⟦g:01d0⟧ (prop. I.34)

and ⟦g:01d1⟧ = ⟦g:01d2⟧ (prop. I.34)

∴ ⟦g:01ce⟧ = ⟦g:01cd⟧ (ax. I.3).
