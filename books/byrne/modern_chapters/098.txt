Proposition III.29 (Theorem)

[Figure afh: The diagram for Proposition III.29, in Byrne’s colours.]

In *equal circles ⟦g:0355⟧ and ⟦g:03f5⟧ the chords ⟦g:0008⟧ and ⟦g:037d⟧ which subtend equal arcs are equal.*

If the equal arcs be semicircles the proposition is evident. But if not,

let ⟦g:0161⟧, ⟦g:03f6⟧ and ⟦g:004b⟧, ⟦g:0125⟧ be drawn to the centres;

∵ ⟦g:03f9⟧ = ⟦g:03fa⟧ (hyp.)

and ⟦g:03f7⟧ = ⟦g:03f8⟧ (prop. III.27);

but ⟦g:0161⟧ and ⟦g:03f6⟧ = ⟦g:004b⟧ and ⟦g:0125⟧

∴ ⟦g:0008⟧ = ⟦g:037d⟧ (prop. I.4);

but these are the chords subtending the equal arcs.

Q. E. D.
