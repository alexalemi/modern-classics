Proposition VI.15 (Theorem)

[Figure ahh: The diagram for Proposition VI.15, in Byrne’s colours.]

Equal *triangles, which have one angle in each equal (* ⟦g:0606⟧ = ⟦g:0607⟧ *), have the sides about the equal angles reciprocally proportional(* ⟦g:0050⟧ : ⟦g:00a3⟧ :: ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:019d⟧ *)And two triangles which have an angle of the one equal to an angle of the other, and the sides about the equal angles reciprocally proportional, are equal.*

 I.

Let the triangles be so placed that the equal angles ⟦g:0606⟧ and ⟦g:0607⟧ may be vertically opposite, that is to say, so that ⟦g:0050⟧ and ⟦g:00a3⟧ may be in the same straight line. Whence also ⟦g:00ab⟧ and ⟦g:019d⟧ must be in the same straight line (prop. I.14).

Draw ⟦g:01b2⟧, then

⟬⟦g:0050⟧ : ⟦g:00a3⟧ :: ⟦g:0608⟧ : ⟦g:0609⟧ (prop. VI.1)‖:: ⟦g:060a⟧ : ⟦g:0609⟧ (prop. V.7)‖:: ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:019d⟧ (prop. VI.1)⟭

∴ ⟦g:0050⟧ : ⟦g:00a3⟧ :: ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:019d⟧ (prop. V.11).

 II.

Let the same construction remain, and

⟦g:0608⟧ : ⟦g:0609⟧ :: ⟦g:0050⟧ : ⟦g:00a3⟧ (prop. VI.1)

and ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:019d⟧ :: ⟦g:060a⟧ : ⟦g:0609⟧ (prop. VI.1)

but ⟦g:0050⟧ : ⟦g:00a3⟧ :: ⟦g:00ab⟧ : ⟦g:019d⟧ , (hyp.)

∴ ⟦g:0608⟧ : ⟦g:0609⟧ :: ⟦g:060a⟧ : ⟦g:0609⟧ (prop. V.11);

∴ ⟦g:0608⟧ = ⟦g:060a⟧ (prop. V.9).

Q. E. D.
