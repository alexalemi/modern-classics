Proposition I.13 (Theorem)

[Figure abn: The diagram for Proposition I.13, in Byrne’s colours.]

When *a straight line (⟦g:00bb⟧) standing upon another straight line (⟦g:00bc⟧) makes angles with it; they are either two right angles or together equal to two right angles.*

If ⟦g:00bb⟧ be ⊥ to ⟦g:00bc⟧ then,

⟦g:00bd⟧ and ⟦g:00be⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ (def. I.10),

but if ⟦g:00bb⟧ be not ⊥ to ⟦g:00bc⟧,

draw ⟦g:00bf⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:00bc⟧ (prop. I.11);

⟦g:00b9⟧ + ⟦g:00c0⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ (const.),

⟦g:00b9⟧ = ⟦g:00c0⟧ = ⟦g:00c1⟧ + ⟦g:00be⟧

∴ ⟦g:00b9⟧ + ⟦g:00c0⟧ = ⟦g:00b9⟧ + ⟦g:00c1⟧ + ⟦g:00be⟧ (ax. I.2)

= ⟦g:00bd⟧ + ⟦g:00be⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ .

Q. E. D.
