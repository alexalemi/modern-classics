Proposition V.20 (Theorem)

If *there be three magnitudes, and other three, which taken two and two, have the same ratio; then, if the first be greater than the third, the fourth shall be greater than the sixth; and if equal, equal; and if less, less.*

Let ⟦g:0545⟧, ⟦g:056f⟧, ⟦g:052d⟧, the first three magnitudes,

and ⟦g:0529⟧, ⟦g:0523⟧, ⟦g:0521⟧, be the other three,

such that ⟦g:0545⟧ : ⟦g:056f⟧ :: ⟦g:0529⟧ : ⟦g:0523⟧ , and ⟦g:056f⟧ : ⟦g:052d⟧ :: ⟦g:0523⟧ : ⟦g:0521⟧ .

Then, if ⟦g:0545⟧ >, =, or < ⟦g:052d⟧ ,

then will ⟦g:0529⟧ >, =, or < ⟦g:0521⟧ .

From the hypothesis, by alternando, we have

⟦g:0545⟧ : ⟦g:0529⟧ :: ⟦g:056f⟧ : ⟦g:0523⟧ ,

and ⟦g:056f⟧ : ⟦g:0523⟧ :: ⟦g:052d⟧ : ⟦g:0521⟧

∴ ⟦g:0545⟧ : ⟦g:0529⟧ :: ⟦g:052d⟧ : ⟦g:0521⟧ (prop. V.11);

∴ if ⟦g:0545⟧ >, =, or < ⟦g:052d⟧ ,

then will ⟦g:0529⟧ >, =, or < ⟦g:0521⟧ (prop. V.14).
