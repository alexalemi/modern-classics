Proposition I.36 (Theorem)

[Figure acl: The diagram for Proposition I.36, in Byrne’s colours.]

Parallelograms *(⟦g:019b⟧ and ⟦g:019c⟧) on equal bases, and between the same parallels, are equal.*

Draw ⟦g:019d⟧ and ⟦g:019e⟧

⟦g:0174⟧ = ⟦g:0138⟧ = ⟦g:00a0⟧ by (prop. I.34 and hyp.);

∴ ⟦g:0174⟧ = and ∥ ⟦g:00a0⟧ ;

∴ ⟦g:019d⟧ = and ∥ ⟦g:019e⟧ (prop. I.33)

And therefore ⟦g:019f⟧ is a parallelogram:

but ⟦g:019b⟧ = ⟦g:019f⟧ = ⟦g:019c⟧ (prop. I.35)

∴ ⟦g:019b⟧ = ⟦g:019c⟧ (ax. I.1).

Q. E. D.
