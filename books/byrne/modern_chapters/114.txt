About *a given circle ⟦g:049e⟧ to circumscribe a square.*

Draw two diameters of the given circle perpendicular to each other, and through their extremities draw ⟦g:00c3⟧, ⟦g:0046⟧, ⟦g:0042⟧ and ⟦g:0045⟧ tangents to the circle;

and ⟦g:049f⟧ is a square.

⟦g:04a0⟧ = ⟦g:02e2⟧ a right angle, (prop. III.18)

also ⟦g:04a1⟧ = ⟦g:02e2⟧ (const.),

∴ ⟦g:00c3⟧ ∥ ⟦g:03e3⟧ ; in the same manner it can be demonstrated that ⟦g:0042⟧ ∥ ⟦g:03e3⟧ , and also that ⟦g:0046⟧ and ⟦g:0045⟧ ∥ ⟦g:03e4⟧ ;

∴ ⟦g:049f⟧ is a parallelogram,

and ∵ ⟦g:04a0⟧ = ⟦g:04a2⟧ = ⟦g:04a3⟧ = ⟦g:04a4⟧ = ⟦g:04a5⟧ they are all right angles (prop. I.34);

it is also evident that ⟦g:00c3⟧, ⟦g:0046⟧, ⟦g:0042⟧ and ⟦g:0045⟧ are equal.

∴ ⟦g:049f⟧ is a square.

Q. E. D.
