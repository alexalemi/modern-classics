Proposition IV.16 (Problem)

[Figure agq]

To *inscribe an equilateral and equiangular quindecagon in a given circle.*

Let ⟦g:00b7⟧ and ⟦g:037e⟧ be the sides of an equilateral pentagon inscribed in the given circle, and ⟦g:0119⟧ the side of an inscribed equilateral triangle.

⟬The arc subtended‖by ⟦g:00b7⟧ and ⟦g:037e⟧⟭} = ⟪2‖5⟫ = ⟪6‖15⟫ ⟬{of the whole‖circumference}⟭

⟬The arc subtended‖by ⟦g:0119⟧⟭} = ⟪1‖3⟫ = ⟪5‖15⟫ ⟬{of the whole‖circumference}⟭

Their difference = ⟪1‖15⟫

∴ the arc subtended by ⟦g:0519⟧ = ⟪1‖15⟫ difference of the whole circumference.

Hence if straight lines equal to ⟦g:0519⟧ be placed in the circle (prop. IV.1), an equilateral and equiangular quindecagon will be thus inscribed in the circle.

Q. E. D.
