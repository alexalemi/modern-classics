Proposition III.14 (Theorem)

[Figure aeo]

Equal *straight lines* (⟬⟦g:035f⟧‖⟦g:0360⟧⟭) *inscribed in a circle are equally distant from the centre; and also, straight lines equally distant from the centre are equal.*

From the centre of ⟦g:0361⟧ draw ⟦g:0362⟧ ⊥ to ⟦g:035f⟧ and ⟦g:0363⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:0360⟧ , join ⟦g:0364⟧ and ⟦g:0365⟧.

Then ⟦g:0366⟧ = half ⟦g:0360⟧ (prop. III.3)

and ⟦g:0367⟧ = ⟪¹‖²⟫ ⟦g:035f⟧ (prop. III.3),

since ⟦g:0360⟧ = ⟦g:035f⟧ (hyp.)

∴ ⟦g:0366⟧ = ⟦g:0367⟧ ,

and ⟦g:0364⟧ = ⟦g:0365⟧ (def. I.15)

∴ ⟦g:0364⟧ ² = ⟦g:0365⟧ ²;

but since ⟦g:0368⟧ is a right angle

⟦g:0364⟧ ² = ⟦g:0362⟧ ² + ⟦g:0367⟧ ² (prop. I.47)

and ⟦g:0365⟧ ² = ⟦g:0363⟧ ² + ⟦g:0366⟧ ² for the same reason,

∴ ⟦g:0362⟧ ² + ⟦g:0367⟧ ² = ⟦g:0363⟧ ² + ⟦g:0366⟧ ²

∴ ⟦g:0362⟧ ² = ⟦g:0363⟧ ²

∴ ⟦g:0362⟧ = ⟦g:0363⟧

Also, if the lines ⟦g:035f⟧ and ⟦g:0360⟧ be equally distant from the centre; that is to say, if the perpendiculars ⟦g:0362⟧ and ⟦g:0363⟧ be given equal, then ⟦g:035f⟧ = ⟦g:0360⟧ .

For, as in the preceding case,

⟦g:0363⟧ ² + ⟦g:0366⟧ ² = ⟦g:0367⟧ ² + ⟦g:0362⟧ ²

but ⟦g:0363⟧ ² = ⟦g:0362⟧ ²

∴ ⟦g:0366⟧ = ⟦g:0367⟧ , and the doubles of these ⟦g:035f⟧ and ⟦g:0360⟧ are also equal.

Q. E. D.
