Proposition VI.9 (Problem)

[Figure ahb]

From *a given straight line (⟦g:05cb⟧) to cut off any required part.*

From either extremity of the given line draw ⟦g:05cc⟧ making any angle with ⟦g:05cb⟧;

and produce ⟦g:05cc⟧ till the whole produced line ⟦g:05cd⟧ contains ⟦g:05ce⟧ as often as ⟦g:05cb⟧ contains the required part.

Draw ⟦g:05cf⟧,

and draw ⟦g:05d0⟧ ∥ ⟦g:05cf⟧ .

⟦g:05d1⟧ is the required part of ⟦g:05cb⟧.

For since ⟦g:05d0⟧ ∥ ⟦g:05cf⟧

⟦g:05d1⟧ : ⟦g:05d2⟧ :: ⟦g:05ce⟧ : ⟦g:05d3⟧ (prop. VI.2),

and by composition (prop. V.18);

⟦g:05cb⟧ : ⟦g:05d1⟧ :: ⟦g:05cd⟧ : ⟦g:05ce⟧ ;

but ⟦g:05cd⟧ contains ⟦g:05ce⟧ as often as ⟦g:05cb⟧ contains the required part (const.);

∴ ⟦g:05d1⟧ is the required part.

Q. E. D.
