If *from any point (⟦g:00fa⟧) within a triangle ⟦g:00fb⟧ straight lines be drawn to the extremities of one side (⟦g:00fc⟧), these lines must be together less than the other two sides, but must contain a greater angle.*

Produce ⟦g:00fd⟧,

⟦g:00fe⟧ + ⟦g:00ff⟧ > ⟦g:0100⟧ (prop. I.20),

⟦g:00fe⟧ + ⟦g:0102⟧ > ⟦g:0100⟧ + ⟦g:0101⟧ (ax. I.4)

⟦g:0100⟧ + ⟦g:0101⟧ > ⟦g:00fd⟧ + ⟦g:0103⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:00fe⟧ + ⟦g:0102⟧ > ⟦g:00fd⟧ + ⟦g:0103⟧ ,

which was to be proved.

Again ⟦g:0104⟧ > ⟦g:0105⟧ (prop. I.16),

and also ⟦g:0106⟧ > ⟦g:0104⟧ (prop. I.16),
