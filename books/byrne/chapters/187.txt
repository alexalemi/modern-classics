Proposition VI.27 (Theorem)

[Figure aht]

Of *all the rectangles contained by the segments of a given straight line, the greatest is the square which is described on half the line.*

Let ⟦g:0671⟧ be the given line,

⟦g:0672⟧ and ⟦g:0673⟧ unequal segments,

and ⟦g:0674⟧ and ⟦g:0675⟧ equal segments;

then ⟦g:0676⟧ > ⟦g:0677⟧ .

For it has been demonstrated already (prop. II.5), that the square of half the line is equal to the rectangle contained by any unequal segments together with the square of the part intermediate between the middle point and the point of unequal section. The square described on half the line exceeds therefore the rectangle contained by any unequal segments of the line.

Q. E. D.
