If *three striaght lines be proportional (* ⟦g:00a0⟧ : ⟦g:01c8⟧ :: ⟦g:01c8⟧ : ⟦g:0357⟧ *) the rectangle under the extremes is equal to the square of the mean.And if the rectangle under the extremes be equal to the square of the mean, the three straight lines are proportional.*

(prop. VI.16).

therefore, if the three straight lines are proportional, the rectangle contained be the extremes is equal to the square of the mean.

 Part II.

Assume ⟦g:00e6⟧ = ⟦g:01c8⟧ ,
