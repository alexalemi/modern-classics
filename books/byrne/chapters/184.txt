Proposition VI.24 (Theorem)

[Figure ahq]

In *any parallelogram (⟦g:0654⟧) the parallelograms (⟦g:0655⟧ and ⟦g:0656⟧) which are about the diagonal are similar to the whole, and to each other.*

As ⟦g:0654⟧ and ⟦g:0656⟧ have a common angle they are equiangular;

but ∵ ⟦g:004f⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0657⟧

⟦g:0658⟧ and ⟦g:0659⟧ are similar (prop. VI.4),

∴ ⟦g:0465⟧ : ⟦g:004f⟧ :: ⟦g:065a⟧ : ⟦g:0657⟧ ;

and the remaining opposite sides are equal to those,

∴ ⟦g:0656⟧ and ⟦g:0654⟧ have the sides about the equal angles proportional, and are therefore similar.

In the same manner it can be demonstrated that the parallelograms ⟦g:0654⟧ and ⟦g:0655⟧ are similar.

Since, therefore, each of the parallelograms ⟦g:0656⟧ and ⟦g:0655⟧ is similar to ⟦g:0654⟧, they are similar to each other.

Q. E. D.
