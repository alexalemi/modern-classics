Proposition III.24 (Theorem)

[Figure afc]

Similar *segments ⟦g:03d2⟧ and ⟦g:03d3⟧, of circles upon equal straight lines (⟦g:01d4⟧ and ⟦g:007f⟧) are each equal to the other.*

For, if ⟦g:03d4⟧ be so applied to ⟦g:03d5⟧,

that ⟦g:007f⟧ may fall on ⟦g:01d4⟧,

the extremities of ⟦g:007f⟧ may be on the extremities ⟦g:01d4⟧

and ⟦g:03d6⟧ at the same side as ⟦g:03d7⟧;

∵ ⟦g:007f⟧ = ⟦g:01d4⟧ ,

⟦g:007f⟧ must wholly coincide with ⟦g:01d4⟧;

and the similar segments being then upon the same straight line and at the same side of it, must also coincide (prop. III.23), and are therefore equal.

Q. E. D.
