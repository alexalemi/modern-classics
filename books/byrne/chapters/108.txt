Proposition IV.1 (Problem)

[Figure agb]

In *a given circle ⟦g:0462⟧ to place a straight line, equal to a given straight line (⟦g:008d⟧), not greater than the diameter of the circle.*

Draw ⟦g:0463⟧, the diameter of ⟦g:0462⟧;

and if ⟦g:0463⟧ = ⟦g:008d⟧ , then the problem is solved.

But if ⟦g:0463⟧ be not equal to ⟦g:008d⟧,

⟦g:0463⟧ > ⟦g:008d⟧ (hyp.);

make ⟦g:039c⟧ = ⟦g:008d⟧ (prop. I.3)

with ⟦g:039c⟧ as radius,

describe ⟦g:0464⟧, cutting ⟦g:0462⟧,

and draw ⟦g:0465⟧, which is the line required.

For ⟦g:0465⟧ = ⟦g:039c⟧ = ⟦g:008d⟧ (prop. I.15 and const.).

Q. E. D.
