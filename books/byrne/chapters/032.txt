Proposition I.27 (Theorem)

[Figure acc]

If *a straight line (⟦g:0150⟧) meeting two other straight lines (⟦g:00a0⟧ and ⟦g:0138⟧) makes with them the alternate angles (⟦g:0151⟧ and ⟦g:0152⟧; ⟦g:0153⟧ and ⟦g:0154⟧) equal, these two straight lines are parallel.*

If ⟦g:00a0⟧ be not parallel to ⟦g:0138⟧ they shall meet when produced.

If it be possible, let those lines be not parallel, but meet when produced; then the external angle ⟦g:0152⟧ is greater than ⟦g:0151⟧ (prop. I.16), but they are also equal (hyp.), which is absurd: in the same manner it may be shown that they cannot meet on the other side; ∴ they are parallel.

Q. E. D.
