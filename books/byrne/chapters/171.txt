Proposition VI.11 (Problem)

[Figure ahd]

To *find a third proportional to two given straight lines (⟦g:05e3⟧ and ⟦g:05e4⟧).*

At either extremity of the given line ⟦g:05e4⟧ draw ⟦g:05e5⟧ making an angle;

take ⟦g:05e6⟧ = ⟦g:05e3⟧ , and draw ⟦g:05e7⟧;

make ⟦g:05e8⟧ = ⟦g:05e3⟧ ,

and draw ⟦g:05e9⟧ ∥ ⟦g:05e7⟧ ; (prop. I.31)

⟦g:05ea⟧ is the third proportional to ⟦g:05e4⟧ and ⟦g:05e3⟧.

For since ⟦g:05e7⟧ ∥ ⟦g:05e9⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:05e4⟧ : ⟦g:05e8⟧ :: ⟦g:05e6⟧ : ⟦g:05ea⟧ (prop. VI.2);

but ⟦g:05e8⟧ = ⟦g:05e6⟧ = ⟦g:05e3⟧ (const.);

∴ ⟦g:05e4⟧ : ⟦g:05e3⟧ :: ⟦g:05e3⟧ : ⟦g:05ea⟧ (prop. V.7).

Q. E. D.
