Proposition III.20 (Theorem)

The *angle at the centre of a circle is double the angle at the circumference, when they have the same part of the circumference for their base.*

[Figure aev]

Let the centre of the circle be on ⟦g:03a0⟧

a side of ⟦g:03a1⟧.

∵ ⟦g:0026⟧ = ⟦g:00c4⟧ ,

But ⟦g:03a3⟧ = ⟦g:03a1⟧ + ⟦g:03a2⟧ ,

or ⟦g:03a3⟧ = twice ⟦g:03a1⟧ (prop. I.32).
