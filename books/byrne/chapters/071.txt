Proposition III.2 (Theorem)

[Figure ady]

Astraight *line (⟦g:02e4⟧) joining two points in the circumference of a circle ⟦g:02e5⟧, lies wholly within the circle.*

Find the centre of ⟦g:02e5⟧ (prop. III.1);

from the centre draw ⟦g:02e6⟧ to any point in ⟦g:02e4⟧, meeting the circumference from the centre;

draw ⟦g:02e7⟧ and ⟦g:02e8⟧.

Then ⟦g:02e9⟧ = ⟦g:02ea⟧ (prop. I.5)

but ⟦g:02eb⟧ > ⟦g:02e9⟧ or > ⟦g:02ea⟧ (prop. I.16)

∴ ⟦g:02e7⟧ > ⟦g:02e6⟧ (prop. I.19)

but ⟦g:02e7⟧ = ⟦g:02ec⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:02ec⟧ > ⟦g:02e6⟧ ;

∴ ⟦g:02e6⟧ < ⟦g:02ec⟧ ;

∴ every point in ⟦g:02e4⟧ lies within the circle.

Q. E. D.
