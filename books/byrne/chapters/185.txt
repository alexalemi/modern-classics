Proposition VI.25 (Problem)

[Figure ahr]

To *describe a rectilinear figure, which shall be similar to a given rectilinear figure (⟦g:065c⟧), and equal to another (⟦g:065d⟧).*

Upon ⟦g:0045⟧ describe ⟦g:065e⟧ = ⟦g:065c⟧ ,

and upon ⟦g:00a0⟧ describe ⟦g:065f⟧ = ⟦g:065d⟧ ,

and having ⟦g:0660⟧ = ⟦g:0661⟧ (prop. I.45),

and then ⟦g:0045⟧ and ⟦g:0662⟧ will lie in the same straight line (prop. I.14 and I.29).

Between ⟦g:0045⟧ and ⟦g:0662⟧ find a mean proportional ⟦g:039b⟧ (prop. VI.13),

and upon ⟦g:039b⟧ describe ⟦g:0663⟧, similar to ⟦g:065c⟧, and similarly situated.

Then ⟦g:0663⟧ = ⟦g:065d⟧ .

For since ⟦g:065c⟧ and ⟦g:0663⟧ are similar, and

⟦g:0045⟧ : ⟦g:039b⟧ :: ⟦g:039b⟧ : ⟦g:0662⟧ (const.),

⟦g:065c⟧ : ⟦g:0663⟧ :: ⟦g:0045⟧ : ⟦g:0662⟧ (prop. VI.20);

but ⟦g:065e⟧ : ⟦g:065f⟧ :: ⟦g:0045⟧ : ⟦g:0662⟧ (prop. VI.1);

∴ ⟦g:065c⟧ : ⟦g:0663⟧ :: ⟦g:065e⟧ : ⟦g:065f⟧ (prop. V.11);

but ⟦g:065c⟧ = ⟦g:065e⟧ (const.),

and ∴ ⟦g:0663⟧ = ⟦g:065f⟧ (prop. V.14);

and ⟦g:065f⟧ = ⟦g:065d⟧ (const.);

consequently, ⟦g:0663⟧ which is similar to ⟦g:065c⟧ is also = ⟦g:065d⟧ .

Q. E. D.
