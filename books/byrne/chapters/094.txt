Asegment *of a circle being given, to describe the circle of which it is the segment.*

From any point in the segment draw ⟦g:0086⟧ and ⟦g:0174⟧,

bisect them, and from the points of bisection

draw ⟦g:00f5⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:0086⟧

and ⟦g:0067⟧ ⊥ ⟦g:0174⟧

where they meet is the centre of the circle.

Because ⟦g:0086⟧ terminated in the circle is bisected perpendicularly by ⟦g:00f5⟧, it passes through the centre (prop. III.1), likewise ⟦g:0067⟧ passes through the centre, therefore the centre is in the intersection of these perpendiculars.
