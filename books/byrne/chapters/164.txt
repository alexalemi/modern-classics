Proposition VI.4 (Theorem)

[Figure agw]

In *equiangular triangles (⟦g:058d⟧ and ⟦g:058e⟧) the sides about the equal angles are proportional, and the sides which are opposite to the equal angles are homologous.*

Let the equiangular triangles be so placed that two sides ⟦g:058f⟧, ⟦g:0590⟧ opposite to equal angles ⟦g:0591⟧ and ⟦g:0592⟧ may be conterminous, and in the same straight line; and that the triangles lying at the same side of that straight line; and that the triangles lying at the same side of that straight line, may have the equal angles not conterminous, i. e. ⟦g:0593⟧ opposite to ⟦g:0594⟧, and ⟦g:0595⟧ to ⟦g:0596⟧.

Draw ⟦g:033a⟧ and ⟦g:04d1⟧.

Then, ∵ ⟦g:0595⟧ = ⟦g:0596⟧ , ⟦g:0031⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0597⟧ (prop. I.28);

and for a like reason ⟦g:0371⟧ ∥ ⟦g:0598⟧ ,

∴ ⟦g:0599⟧ is a parallelogram.

But ⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:0370⟧ :: ⟦g:04d1⟧ : ⟦g:0511⟧ (prop. VI.2);

and since ⟦g:04d1⟧ = ⟦g:0031⟧ (prop. I.34),

⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:0370⟧ :: ⟦g:0031⟧ : ⟦g:0511⟧ ;

and by alternation ⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:0031⟧ :: ⟦g:0370⟧ : ⟦g:0511⟧ (prop. V.16).

In like manner it may be shown, that

⟦g:005a⟧ : ⟦g:0371⟧ :: ⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:0370⟧ ;

and by alternation, that

⟦g:005a⟧ : ⟦g:003e⟧ :: ⟦g:0371⟧ : ⟦g:0370⟧ ;

but it has been already proved that

⟦g:003e⟧ : ⟦g:0031⟧ :: ⟦g:0370⟧ : ⟦g:0511⟧

and therefore, ex æquali,

⟦g:005a⟧ : ⟦g:0031⟧ :: ⟦g:0371⟧ : ⟦g:0511⟧ (prop. V.22),

therefore the sides about the equal angles are proportional, and those which are opposite to the equal angles are homologous.

Q. E. D.
