Proposition VI.31 (Theorem)

[Figure ahx]

If *any similar rectilinear figures be similarly described on the sides of a right angled triangle (⟦g:069b⟧), the figure described on the side (⟦g:069c⟧) subtending the right angle is equal to the sum of the figures on the other sides.*

From the right angle draw ⟦g:069d⟧ perpendicular to ⟦g:069c⟧;

then ⟦g:069c⟧ : ⟦g:069e⟧ :: ⟦g:069e⟧ : ⟦g:069f⟧ (prop. VI.8).

∴ ⟦g:06a0⟧ : ⟦g:06a1⟧ :: ⟦g:069c⟧ : ⟦g:069f⟧ (prop. VI.20).

but ⟦g:06a0⟧ : ⟦g:06a2⟧ :: ⟦g:069c⟧ : ⟦g:06a3⟧ (prop. VI.20).

Hence ⟦g:069f⟧ + ⟦g:06a3⟧ : ⟦g:069c⟧ :: ⟦g:06a2⟧ + ⟦g:06a1⟧ : ⟦g:06a0⟧ ;

but ⟦g:069f⟧ + ⟦g:06a3⟧ = ⟦g:069c⟧ ;

and ∴ ⟦g:06a2⟧ + ⟦g:06a1⟧ = ⟦g:06a0⟧ .

Q. E. D.
