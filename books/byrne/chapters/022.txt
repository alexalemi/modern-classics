Proposition I.17 (Theorem)

[Figure abr]

Any *two angles of a triangle ⟦g:00dd⟧ are together less than two right angles.*

Produce ⟦g:00bf⟧, then will

⟦g:00de⟧ + ⟦g:00df⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ .

But ⟦g:00df⟧ > ⟦g:00e0⟧ (prop. I.16)

∴ ⟦g:00de⟧ + ⟦g:00e0⟧ < ⟦g:0023⟧ ,

and in the same manner it may be shown that any other two angles of the triangle taken together are less than two right angles.

Q. E. D.
