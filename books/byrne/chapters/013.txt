Proposition I.8 (Theorem)

[Figure abi]

If *two triangles have two sides of the one respectively equal to two sides of the other (* ⟦g:008d⟧ = ⟦g:008e⟧ *and* ⟦g:0046⟧ = ⟦g:008f⟧ *) and also their bases (* ⟦g:0090⟧ = ⟦g:0091⟧ *), equal; then the angles (⟦g:0092⟧ and ⟦g:0093⟧) contained by their equal sides are also equal.*

If the equal bases ⟦g:0090⟧ and ⟦g:0091⟧ be conceived to be placed one upon the other, so that the triangles shall lie at the same side of them, and that the equal sides ⟦g:0046⟧ and ⟦g:008f⟧, ⟦g:008d⟧ and ⟦g:008e⟧ be conterminous, the vertex of the one must fall on the vertex of the other; for to suppose them not coincident would contradict the last proposition.

Therefore sides ⟦g:0046⟧ and ⟦g:008d⟧, being coincident with ⟦g:008f⟧ and ⟦g:008e⟧, ∴ ⟦g:0094⟧ = ⟦g:0095⟧ .

Q. E. D.
