Proposition VI.23 (Theorem)

[Figure ahp]

Equiangular *parallelograms (⟦g:064d⟧ and ⟦g:064e⟧) are to one another in a ratio compounded of the ratios of their sides.*

Let two of the sides ⟦g:0138⟧ and ⟦g:00bb⟧ about the equal angles be placed so that they may form one straight line.

Since ⟦g:064f⟧ + ⟦g:0650⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ ,

and ⟦g:0651⟧ = ⟦g:064f⟧ (hyp.),

⟦g:0651⟧ + ⟦g:0650⟧ = ⟦g:0023⟧ ,

and ∴ ⟦g:0046⟧ and ⟦g:004a⟧ form one straight line (prop. I.14);

complete ⟦g:0652⟧.

Since ⟦g:064d⟧ : ⟦g:0652⟧ :: ⟦g:0138⟧ : ⟦g:00bb⟧ (prop. VI.1),

and ⟦g:0652⟧ : ⟦g:064e⟧ :: ⟦g:004a⟧ : ⟦g:0046⟧ (prop. VI.1),

⟦g:064d⟧ has to ⟦g:064e⟧ a ratio compounded of the ratios of ⟦g:0138⟧ to ⟦g:00bb⟧, and of ⟦g:004a⟧ to ⟦g:0046⟧.

Q. E. D.
