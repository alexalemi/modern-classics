Proposition I.1 (Problem)

[Figure abb]

On *a given finite straight line (⟦g:0042⟧) to describe an equilateral triangle.*

Describe ⟦g:0043⟧ and ⟦g:0044⟧ (post. I.3);

draw ⟦g:0045⟧ and ⟦g:0046⟧ (post. I.1).

Then will ⟦g:0047⟧ be equilateral.

For ⟦g:0042⟧ = ⟦g:0045⟧ (def. I.15);

and ⟦g:0042⟧ = ⟦g:0046⟧ (def. I.15);

∴ ⟦g:0045⟧ = ⟦g:0046⟧ (ax. I.1);

and therefore ⟦g:0047⟧ is the equilateral triangle required.

Q. E. D.
