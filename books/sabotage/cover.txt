Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, from the manual's advice for sabotaging meetings and offices. A piece of business passes from desk to desk "through channels," then goes round a committee table, and is handed back to where it started. The manual also asks for committees "as large as possible—never less than five," so the table has six chairs. The line at the top is its first instruction.
