Adolph Menzel's *The Iron Rolling Mill* (1875). In a dark shed under a tangle of shafts and belts, a crew of workmen haul a white-hot bar through the rollers with long tongs, sparks spraying across the floor and a great flywheel turning in the background. It is a picture of industrial production at full pressure, and production of exactly this kind is what the manual set out to slow down, one dull tool and wrong switch at a time.
