Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A radius turns inside a circle, and the height of its tip, carried along the line, draws the sine wave beside it: the picture behind Whitehead's chapters on periodicity and trigonometry, where he shows how one simple function describes everything in nature that repeats. The line at the top is from his fifth chapter, on the symbolism of mathematics.
