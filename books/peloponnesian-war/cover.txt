Philipp von Foltz's *Pericles' Funeral Oration* (1852), from a photograph of the painting made in the 1870s. Pericles stands on the speaker's platform in a crested helmet, one arm thrown out toward the city, while the Athenians crowd the steps below him and the Acropolis rises behind. It is the speech Thucydides reports at the end of the war's first year, over the men who had died in it. Foltz painted the Parthenon whole and new, as Pericles had built it, and in the next chapter of the book the plague comes into that same crowded city.
