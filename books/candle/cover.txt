Alexander Blaikley's picture of Faraday lecturing at the Royal Institution on 27 December 1855. He stands at the curved lecture bench, one arm out to the room, with flasks and glassware on the table and children in the front rows, below tiers of bonnets and frock coats. It is one of his Christmas courses for young people, in the same theatre and at the same bench where he gave the lectures in this book five years later.
