[PLATE 0.02 0.64 0.85 0.99 | photo | The beaver is nature's hydraulic engineer, habitually cutting down streamside trees, building dams, and living in the ponds so created. Near headwaters, it may be a useful agent for soil and water conservation. Downstream, in farming and irrigation districts, its activities may cause damage. Some States now regularly trap and transfer beaver to areas where their activities are useful. In the stream shown below, beavers built a series of dams, raised the water table, and turned semi-arid land into a good hay-producing meadow.]
