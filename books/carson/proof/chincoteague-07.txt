# chincoteague 07: map

[PLATE 0.00 0.03 1.00 0.93 | map | SUMMER BREEDING RANGE; WINTERING RANGE; CHINCOTEAGUE]

There are several reasons why refuges are particularly needed within the Atlantic flyway. Although this flyway has enormous nesting areas its wintering grounds are very small and lie in a densely settled part of the United States. The Atlantic flyway was affected by the general decline that reduced the populations of ducks and geese to a very low point early in the 1930’s. It has made less progress toward recovery than most other areas. Since 1944 its waterfowl have again been declining sharply.
