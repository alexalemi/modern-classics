John James Audubon's *Canada Goose* (1834), a hand-colored engraving with aquatint made by Robert Havell after Audubon's drawing for *The Birds of America*. One goose stands on a rock among the reeds with its head thrown back, calling, and a second sits low in the grass behind it. Canada geese winter at Mattamuskeet and pass along every flyway in these booklets, and they are among the birds the refuges were set aside to protect.
