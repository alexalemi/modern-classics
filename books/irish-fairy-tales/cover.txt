Arthur Rackham's frontispiece for this book, the 1920 Macmillan edition, captioned "In a forked glen into which he slipped at night-fall he was surrounded by giant toads." A young warrior in a gold helmet and blue cloak, shield up and spear ready, stands among grey toads as big as he is, their wide mouths turned toward him, under knotted trees whose roots run down like water. Rackham drew sixteen colour plates for Stephens' stories, and they are all inside this edition.
