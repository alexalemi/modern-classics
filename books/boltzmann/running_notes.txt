BOLTZMANN -- RUNNING NOTES (updated by the orchestrator between parts)

- Title and heading: "A German Professor's Journey into Eldorado".
- The trip: June 1905, Vienna -> Leipzig (academy cartel meeting on the
  Encyclopedia of Mathematical Sciences) -> Bremen -> New York -> California
  (Berkeley summer school, Mrs. Phoebe Hearst, Stanford), return via
  Chicago and New York on the Kaiser Wilhelm II.

AFTER PART 1 (voice set):
- Voice: chatty, self-mocking, contractions; the Viennese stomach is the
  running gag; direct address ("Dear reader!") and parenthetical asides stay.
- Food: Geselchtes / Selchfleisch mit Knödel = "smoked pork with dumplings"
  (keep this wording if it recurs); Gollasch mit Nockerl = "goulash with
  little dumplings".
- Kartell = "Academy Cartel"; Enzyklopädie = "Encyclopedia of Mathematical
  Sciences", then "the encyclopedia"; Klein's "mathematical public
  convenience" (the toilet pun).
- He quotes Schiller constantly in honour of the Schiller centenary (1905);
  translate the quotations in quotation marks, verse as TAB lines. "Before
  the Pestalutz does it, I'll do it myself" (Wallensteins Tod) stays as is.
- English words he uses on purpose stay English (washing rooms, office,
  Blackboard, Chalk, lobstersalad); his broken English ("When lunch will be
  served?", "Lönch, lanch") verbatim.
- Ships: out on the Kronprinz Wilhelm ("a Hohenzollern prince carried me on
  his back"); back on the Kaiser Wilhelm II -- echo the joke if he makes it.
- Berkeley: Cloyne Court on Euclid Avenue ("nothing non-Euclidean"); sung
  menus. Miss May O'Callaghan, his Vienna English teacher.
- FORWARD REFERENCE: the Lick Observatory and its millionaire idealist
  ("more about that later") -- Part 2 or 3 must deliver it; idealism is a
  thread (Klein's, Lick's, Bishop Berkeley's "in a different sense").
- Part 1 ends mid-Berkeley, inside the Cloyne Court description: Part 2
  continues straight on, no re-introduction.

SENSITIVE-CONTENT RULING, REVISED (Alex, 2026-09-28): the railway-soot joke
(a future scholar might think Black people became black from working as
railway staff) is CUT, together with its punchline (the porter greeting
the soot-blackened Boltzmann as "Colleague", Part 3). Kept: the heat, the
soot, "got completely black". Not the author's subject; nothing else
depends on it (the Carroll/Aladdin precedent). "A Black servant" at the
Hearst hacienda (Part 2) is plain description and stays.
