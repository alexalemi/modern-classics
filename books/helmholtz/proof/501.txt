The notation used by Mr. Colin ¶ Brown, as shewn in fig. 67, is different from that used above. The column of Fifths, so far as it is there shewn, is

	`*D*♭, *A*♭, *E*♭, *B*♭, *F*, *C*, *G*, *D*,
	*A'*, *E'*, *B'*, *F'*♯

The rest of his notation need not be specified, because it does not appear in fig. 67.

¶ By the duodenary arrangement it is seen that the only duodenation contemplated was by Fifths up and down from the duodene of *B₁*♭ to that of *D₁*♯. But it was not viewed in this light. It was rather considered as a series of major scales modulating into the dominant or subdominant, and also into the relative minor. The tonic minor was always taken one comma flatter than the tonic
