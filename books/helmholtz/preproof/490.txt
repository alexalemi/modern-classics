Abbreviate the names ' major triad ' and ' minor triad ' into their first syllables, ma., mi. The seven tones in each trichordal reduced to a single octave constitute an harmonic scale, that is a scale in which each note belongs to a triad in the scale. We may begin the scale with any one of the notes of any one of the 3 generating triads. These notes may be numbered in order of sharpness when reduced to the same octave, as 4, 6, 1 in the subdominant, 1, 3, 5 in the tonic, and o, 7, 2 in the dominant.
