For a pianoforte, the discontinuity in the motion of the string is diminished by covering the hammer with an elastic pad. This sensibly diminishes the force of ¶ the higher upper partials, because the motion is no longer conveyed to a single point, but is imparted to a sensible length of string, and this too, not in an indivisible moment of time, as would be the case for a blow with a perfectly hard body. On the contrary, the elastic jjad yields to the blow at first, and then recovers itself, so that while the hammer is in contact with the string, the motion is capable of extending over a considerable length.
