Up to this point we have considered only the relations of the tones in a piece of ¶ music with its tonic, and of its chords with its tonic chord. On these relations depends the connection of the parts of a mass of tone into one coherent whole. But besides this the succession of the tones and chords must be regulated by natural relations.
