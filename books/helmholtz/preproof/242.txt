This is, however, far from being the case. The minor triad is very decidedly less harmonious than the major triad, in consequence of the combinational tones, which must consequently be here taken into consideration. In treating of the relative harmoniousness of the consonant intervals we have seen that combinational tones may produce beats when two intervals are compounded, even when each interval separately produced no beats at all, or at least none distinctly audible (pp. 2006-2046).

Hence we must determine the combinational tones of the major and minor triads.
