RULINGS FROM THE PILOT (these override anything above that disagrees)
1. ¶ IN EITHER MARGIN (right margin on odd pages) is the same mark: "¶ " at
   the start of the text of the printed line it stands beside. Beside a
   displayed formula: before the \[ . Beside a set-off line: after the TAB.
   Beside a figure or music cut: at the start of the NEXT text after it.
2. ITALIC AND WORD CHARACTERS. The renderer only sees *...* as italic when
   the opening * does not follow a letter or digit. So:
   - page-quarter letters glued to page numbers are ROMAN: "16d", "215d'",
     "p. 220c" (never "16*d*");
   - a note name with a superscript prefix puts the prefix INSIDE the
     italics: "*¹¹a'*♭", "*⁷b*♭"; sharps/flats may stay outside;
   - italic note names otherwise as printed: "*c'*", "*CE₁G*".
3. FOOTNOTE MARKS: an asterisk mark is written ⁎ (U+204E), in the body and
   at the start of the footnote ("Footnote: ⁎ ..."); † ‡ § ‖ as printed.
   A bare * is only ever part of *italic*.
4. Small-capital A.D. / B.C. are written "A.D." / "B.C." with no markup.
   Small capitals opening a paragraph ("In", "The"): plain, no markers.
5. The printer's space before ; : ? ! is closed up ("tone:", "said;").
6. A music passage's printed title: if it carries no footnote mark, it is
   the plate's printed label (and not in the text); with a mark, it stays
   in the text and the label is empty.
7. Stacked figured-bass numbers: \(\substack{6\\3}\). Equation lines
   numbered on the left: "1) \quad ..." inside the display.
8. BOX EDGES: find a cut's edges from dark-pixel row/column profiles
   (PIL), then confirm by cropping; eyeballing clips double bars and
   hairlines.
9. Each agent keeps its scratch files in its own subfolder.
10. A ¶ BESIDE THE SECOND HALF OF A WORD broken at a line end goes before
   the WHOLE joined word: "general ¶ tunefulness", never "tuneful-¶ ness".
11. ELLIS'S GRAVE-ACCENT PREFIX on a note (the skhismic notes, from about
   p. 274 on) is a plain backtick before the italic note: `*E₁*. It
   survives the italic rule; a modifier-letter grave does not. The
   Pythagorean comma sign is ‿; notes with a stroke above or below (the
   old barred notation) take a combining macron or low line (c̄, A̲).
