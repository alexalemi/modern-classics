Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. One string is shown vibrating six ways at once: as a whole, in halves, in thirds, and so on, each with its still points, or nodes, marked. These are the upper partial tones that sound together in every musical note, and Helmholtz's book is built on them: on how the ear hears them, and on why some combinations of notes sound smooth and others rough.
