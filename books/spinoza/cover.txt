Johannes Vermeer's *The Astronomer* (1668). A scholar in a loose blue robe leans from his table to turn a celestial globe, an open book under his other hand, with the light coming in through a leaded window beside him. Vermeer was born the same year as Spinoza, and painted this in the Dutch Republic while Spinoza was writing the *Ethics*: a man alone with his instruments, working something out by himself.
