Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Ball's own figure. The seven stars of the Great Bear stand in a night of fainter ones, and from the two Pointers, alpha and beta, a dotted line runs up to the Pole Star. It is the first step in the closing chapter, "How to Name the Stars", where Ball teaches his young readers to find their way round the sky; the line at the top is his.
