Claude Monet's *Woman with a Parasol — Madame Monet and Her Son* (1875). Camille Monet stands on a grassy rise against a blue summer sky, her white dress and veil blown by the wind, holding a green parasol; their small son stands a little way off in the grass. Russell counts affection, the family and attention turned outward among the causes of happiness, and this is a picture of an ordinary afternoon of exactly that kind.
