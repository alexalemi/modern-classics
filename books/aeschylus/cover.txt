John Collier's *Clytemnestra* (1882). She stands in a bronze-studded doorway, one hand holding back a patterned curtain and the other resting on the haft of a long axe, its blade red, her white robe stained down the front. Collier painted the moment in the *Agamemnon* when she comes out of the palace to stand over her husband's body and say that she did it, and is not sorry.
