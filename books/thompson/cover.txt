Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A beam of white light passes through a prism and fans out into its colours, red to violet, and beyond each end lie the rays the eye cannot see, the infra-red and the ultra-violet. Thompson gives a lecture to each of those invisible parts of the spectrum, and a last one to the newest kind of invisible light, Röntgen's.
