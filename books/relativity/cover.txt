Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A train runs along an embankment, and lightning strikes the rails at A and B; M is the point midway between them on the embankment, M′ the passenger midway along the train. It is Einstein's own example, from the section on the relativity of simultaneity: two flashes that are simultaneous for the embankment are not simultaneous for the train.
