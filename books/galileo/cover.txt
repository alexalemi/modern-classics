Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. The Sun sits at the centre, the planets move in circles about it, the Moon circles the Earth, and four moons circle Jupiter: the Copernican system as Simplicio draws it, step by step at Salviati's prompting, in the Third Day, with the moons of Jupiter that Galileo had found with his telescope more than twenty years before.
