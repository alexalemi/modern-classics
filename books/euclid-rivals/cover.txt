Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. Two parallel lines, AB and CD, are cut by a third line EF at G and H, and the alternate angles AGH and GHD are shaded: the figure of Euclid's theorem on parallels, quoted on the cover in the book's own words. Euclid's treatment of parallels is the ground on which Carroll's long defence of him against the modern textbooks is fought.
