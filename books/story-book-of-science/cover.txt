Jean-Henri Fabre's own watercolour of *Pleurotus phosphoreus* (1878). A cluster of orange-capped mushrooms with pale gills and dark stems rises out of a patch of earth. Fabre painted hundreds of studies of fungi, and this one is a poisonous kind. In the book Uncle Paul spends a Sunday telling the village that no rule of colour, smell or shape will tell the safe mushrooms from the dangerous ones.
