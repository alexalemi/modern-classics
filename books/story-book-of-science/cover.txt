Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A garden spider's web hangs from a single strong thread: spokes run out from the centre, the spiral is laid round them from spoke to spoke, and the spider waits in the middle. Uncle Paul tells his nephews and niece how she builds it in the chapter called "The Spider's Web", beginning with the bridge thread, and the line at the top is how he begins.
