Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Turgot's division of society into three classes: the cultivators, the landowners and the artisans. Money passes round them in a ring, from the cultivators to the landowners as rent, from the landowners to the artisans for their goods, and back to the cultivators for food. Turgot himself compares this circulation to the circulation of the blood, and the line at the top is his.
