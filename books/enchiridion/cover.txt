The frontispiece of Edward Ivie's Latin verse *Enchiridion* (Oxford, 1715), a copperplate engraving by Michael Burghers after William Sonmans. Epictetus sits at a table with an open book and a pen, looking up, with his crutch propped against his knee for the lame leg. The Greek line below is the old epitaph that begins "I was Epictetus, a slave, and lame in body." It is the man the introduction describes, drawn as an English press imagined him three centuries ago.
