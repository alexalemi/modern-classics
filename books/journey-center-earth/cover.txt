Édouard Riou's engraving from the illustrated edition Hetzel published in 1867, three years after the novel. A raft with three men clinging to it rides high on the face of an enormous wave, while blocks of stone fly through a sky full of light above them. Riou's plates show what Verne asks the reader to believe: three travellers on a raft, far under the earth, at the mercy of forces nobody had measured.
