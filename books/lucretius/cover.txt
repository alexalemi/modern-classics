Joseph Wright of Derby's *Vesuvius in Eruption, with a View over the Islands in the Bay of Naples* (about 1776). The volcano's cone glows red with lava under a column of fire and a black plume of smoke, sparks scattered across the night sky, and two small figures stand on the dark ground in the foreground. Lucretius gives the last book of his poem to things that frighten people, Etna's fires among them, and explains each one by atoms and void.
