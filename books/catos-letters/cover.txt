A hand-coloured engraving after Edward Matthew Ward's painting *The South Sea Bubble, a Scene in 'Change Alley in 1720* (1847). A crowd packs the lane outside Garraway's coffee house, faces fill the windows above, and a placard on the wall reads "South Sea Stock at 1000 Per Ct." In front, a man in a yellow coat stands among women in silk gowns while a dog noses past. Exchange Alley was where the shares changed hands, and the crash that followed is what set Trenchard and Gordon writing.
