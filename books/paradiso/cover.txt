Gustave Doré's engraving of the Empyrean (1868), for Canto 31. Two small figures, Dante and his guide, stand on a rock at the foot of a vast white rose made of angels, ring inside ring, turning around a point of light at the center. It is the place the whole poem has been climbing toward. Doré's plates are on the covers of all three volumes of the Comedy in this collection.
