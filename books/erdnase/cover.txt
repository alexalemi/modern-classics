Caravaggio's *The Cardsharps* (c. 1594). A young cheat in a striped doublet and plumed hat leans over a card table, looking across it at his mark, while behind his back his hand draws extra cards from his belt. At the left edge his older accomplice stands with a gloved hand raised. Four centuries before Erdnase, it is the card table artifice his first half teaches, caught at the moment it is done.
