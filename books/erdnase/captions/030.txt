cm	The left hand holds the deck face down with the thumb along one side; the lower portion has been released and lies tilted in the palm while the thumb still supports the upper portion.
cn	The first finger has pushed the fallen portion up against the left thumb, and the upper portion is tilting past its upturned side, ready to drop into the palm beneath it.
co	The Longitudinal Shift prepared: the right hand covers the deck from above at the ends, while the left little finger is pressed in between the corners of the two packets at the inner end.
cp	The shift in action: the right fingers press the lower packet in and down against the left fingers, while the left little finger and thumb draw the upper packet away toward the performer.
cq	The Open Shift prepared: the left hand holds the deck by its sides, the little finger holding the break, while the right second finger and thumb seize the ends near the right corners.
cr	The right thumb and second finger draw the lower packet to the right and upwards while the right first finger presses the upper packet down against the left thumb.
cs	The S. W. E. Shift position: the deck lies crosswise in the left hand, thumb at one end, and the little finger holds a break at the lower end, unseen from the front.
ct	The right hand seizes the sides of the deck close to the lower corners with the second and third fingertips and thumb, leaving most of the deck in view.
cu	The packets reversing: the right hand holds the lower end of the deck, thumb along the side, while fingertips show at either end as the two packets tilt apart.
cv	The right hand lies over the deck while the left hand holds it by the sides; the selected card has been slid diagonally and its corner protrudes at the side of the deck.
cw	The palm-shift: the left hand turns over and inwards with the selected card palmed in it, while the right hand slides the deck out and away to the right.
cx	Alternatively the left hand, with the card palmed, takes the deck by its corner between the first finger and thumb, and the right hand is left free.
