db	The Top Change: as the hands meet, the left thumb pushes the top card over the side and the right first and second fingertips clip it, leaving the right hand's card on top.
dc	The Bottom Change: the right hand's card goes under the deck to the left fingers, while the right thumb and first finger carry off the top card the left thumb has pushed over.
dd	The Palm Change: the left thumb and fingers seize the two cards held as one, a diamond card in front, drawing one off as the other is pressed up into the right palm.
de	The Double-Palm Change: the left hand holds the face-up packet from the table by its sides between thumb and fingertips, as the right hand comes flat over it to palm it.
