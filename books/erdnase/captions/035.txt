df	The First Method: the left hand holds the deck while the right palm presses against the selected card and draws it down past the wrong card, which is pushed up against the left first finger.
dg	The Second Method: the right fingertips lie against the end of the deck while the left hand draws the deck down, leaving the selected card to be pressed into the right palm.
dh	The Fourth Method: the left hand holds the deck on its side, first and little fingers at the ends, while the flat right hand covers its face and the left thumb pushes up the back card.
di	The Sixth Method: the left hand holds the deck by the ends, face to the company, and the little fingertips of both hands meet at the lower corner, clipping the pushed-over card between them.
dj	The right hand turns over and palms the lower card as it swings out at the upper end, while the left hand keeps the face of the deck in view.
