cy	The Back Palm begins: the right hand holds a card face up, the two of clubs, between the thumb and the tips of the second and third fingers, first and little fingers at the sides.
cz	The second and third fingers have curled under the card, and the thumb has pushed it outward until its inner corners reach the first and little fingers, which hold it.
da	The fingers straightened, seen from the back: the two of clubs lies along the back of the right hand, its corners clipped between the first and second and between the third and little fingertips.
