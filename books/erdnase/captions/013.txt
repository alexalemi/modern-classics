av	The hold for bottom dealing: the deck held by its corners between the left second finger and the palm below the thumb, the thumb on top pointing toward the second finger tip.
aw	The top card pushed over by the left thumb, and beneath it the bottom card pushed out by the finger tip, the two cards overhanging the side together so that the upper hides the under.
ax	The right hand advancing apparently to take off the top card, its thumb and fingers meeting the overhanging cards at the side of the deck in the left hand.
ay	The bottom card being dealt: the right thumb and second finger draw it out from under the deck while the left thumb holds back the top card.
