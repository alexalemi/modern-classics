The drawing for the frontispiece of *Leviathan* (1651), in ink, from the manuscript copy Hobbes offered to Charles II; it is usually credited to Abraham Bosse. A crowned giant rises over hills and a town with a twin-spired church, holding a bishop's crozier in his left hand, and his body is made of human faces. In the printed frontispiece the little figures turn toward the giant's head; here they look out at us. The giant is the commonwealth: one person made of many.
