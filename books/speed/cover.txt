Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Speed's Diagram III. A card frame with a rectangular opening is crossed by threads in equal squares, and through it a cone and a ball are seen as shapes on a flat surface. Speed tells the student to make one from stiff cardboard and hold it upright between the eye and the object, one eye closed, so that appearances can be copied as a flat subject; the line at the top is his caption.
