Jan Steen's *The Drawing Lesson* (1665). In a painter's studio an artist bends over the table to correct a drawing while a young woman beside him watches closely and a boy looks on from behind. The walls are hung with plaster casts of a face, a head and a foot, and a small figure stands on the table: the casts students learned to draw from. Speed's book is a drawing lesson of the same kind, written two and a half centuries later by a painter who taught it.
