Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after the one figure in the book. Species A to L stand along the foot; each horizontal line is a thousand generations or more; most lines of descent stop, one species, F, goes on unchanged, and two branch again and again into new varieties and species. Darwin calls it a diagram that will help with "this rather complex subject", and it is the whole theory in one picture.
