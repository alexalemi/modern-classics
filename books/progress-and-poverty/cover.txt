Gustave Doré's engraving *Over London—by Rail* (1872), from *London: A Pilgrimage*. Seen through the arch of a railway viaduct, rows of brick houses run back to back into the distance, each with its tiny walled yard full of people, washing and barrels, under a sky of chimney smoke and another viaduct. It is George's question in one picture: the railway, the newest of all the new powers of production, runs straight over the poor.
