Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after the law of rent on which George builds his argument. Eight pieces of land stand in order of how much they yield. A line is drawn at the least productive land still in use, and everything a better piece produces above that line goes to rent; wages and interest get only what lies below it. The line at the top is his statement of the law.
