Jules Férat's engraving for the original 1875 Hetzel edition of the novel, from the first pages of the story. The balloon has come down at the edge of the sea, its envelope sagging and its netting trailing in the water, and the castaways struggle out of the tangle under a sky full of seabirds. It is the moment they arrive on the island with almost nothing, which is where everything they go on to build begins.
