ab	The endpapers, drawn in pale green line across two pages: a cottage among clouds and trees, a little procession of dancing figures on a winding path, and a bird perched high on a bare branch.
ac	Alice stands on her own in a plain frame, hands behind her back, in a patterned pinafore dress, striped stockings and buttoned shoes, looking straight out at the reader.
ad	The decorated title page, framed by a climbing rose-tree, with playing-card gardeners among the branches and the roses, the White Rabbit hurrying along the bottom, and a small cat's face beneath the title.
ae	The White Rabbit, in a coat and waistcoat, dashes along a bank towards the dark mouth of the rabbit-hole under a twisted hedge.
af	Alice, stretched to an enormous height, stands with her arms held away from her sides, her neck and legs drawn impossibly long.
ag	Alice swims through the dark water beside the Mouse, with a crowd of birds and beasts paddling around them — a duck, a stork, a crab, a crow and other odd creatures.
ah	The Dodo stands in the middle of a crowd of wet birds and beasts on the shore, with Alice sitting among them and the Mouse in the foreground.
ai	The Mouse sits up on its haunches, its long tail curled along the ground, while Alice sits cross-legged facing it, listening with her head bowed.
aj	The White Rabbit, with an umbrella in his hand, crouches under a tall withered tree and calls out angrily to Alice, who stops short at the left.
ak	The Caterpillar, a long blue creature with a man's face, sits on top of a huge mushroom smoking a hookah, while Alice stands below looking up at him, her hands behind her back.
al	Alice, shrunk so suddenly that her chin has struck her foot, crouches with her hair streaming out around her.
am	Alice's head and long neck rise up among the tops of the trees, where the Pigeon flutters beside her, beating its wings.
an	The Frog-Footman, in livery and a curled wig, sits on the step outside the Duchess's door, while Alice stands by the brick wall and points towards the door.
ao	In the Duchess's smoke-filled kitchen, plates, a clock and pans fly through the air; the Duchess sits nursing the baby, the cook throws things, the Cheshire Cat grins on the hearth and Alice bends forward at the left.
ap	Alice stands among tall grasses holding the baby, which has a pig's snout and ears, and looks down at it with worry.
aq	The Cheshire Cat lies stretched along a bough of a tree, its striped body half hidden in the twigs, grinning down.
ar	Alice stands small at the foot of a great twisted old tree, looking up at the branches where the Cheshire Cat has vanished.
as	Alice sits in a large armchair at the far end of the long tea-table, while the Hatter in his tall hat and the March Hare sit close together at the other end among the teacups and the teapot.
at	Beside the rose-tree, the Knave bows low, carrying the King's crown on a crimson cushion, while the gardeners lie face down beneath their painted-card backs and the Queen and King look on; paint-pots stand on the grass.
au	Alice holds a flamingo tucked under her arm as her croquet-mallet, its long neck and head hanging down towards the ground.
av	The Cheshire Cat's head appears alone in a curl of cloud, eyes shut and grinning broadly.
aw	The executioner, a thin playing card with a club on his front and a hood over his eyes, stands leaning on his axe.
ax	On the croquet-ground the Queen of Hearts stands in her robes beside a young rose-tree, with the King and other cards gathered to one side; the White Rabbit and Alice are small in the distance, and a hedgehog crawls across the ground.
ay	The Gryphon lies asleep in the sun at the foot of a gnarled tree, its wings folded and its long tail curled across the ground.
az	Alice sits on a rock by the sea between the Mock Turtle, who has a calf's head on a turtle's body, and the Gryphon, whose golden wings are spread behind her.
ba	The Mock Turtle stands up on its hind legs on the edge of a rock, head thrown back and flippers raised, as if in the middle of a song.
bb	In the court, the King sits in his wig with his crown on top beside the Queen of Hearts, the White Rabbit holds a scroll, the jurors crowd into the box, and a courtier in a red cap leans in from the right.
