Arthur Rackham's *A Mad Tea-Party*, one of his colour plates for this 1907 edition. The Hatter leans across a long table set with far too many cups, the March Hare beside him, the Dormouse squashed between them as an elbow-rest, and Alice sits at the far end in a tall armchair looking unimpressed. It is Chapter VII, and the picture is in its place inside the book.
