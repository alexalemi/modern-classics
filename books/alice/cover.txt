John Tenniel's last picture for this book (1865), engraved on wood by the Dalziel brothers. At the end of the trial Alice, grown to her full size, tells the court that they are nothing but a pack of cards, and the whole pack rises into the air and comes flying down on her. She throws up an arm to beat them off while the creatures of the court scatter round her feet. A moment later she wakes on the riverbank with her head in her sister's lap.
