Proem

[Figure aa: The front cover in red cloth, lettered in gold, with a colour picture of Alice sitting on the seashore between the Mock Turtle and the Gryphon.]

[Figure ab: The endpapers, drawn in pale green line across two pages: a cottage among clouds and trees, a little procession of dancing figures on a winding path, and a bird perched high on a bare branch.]

[Figure ac: Alice — Alice stands on her own in a plain frame, hands behind her back, in a patterned pinafore dress, striped stockings and buttoned shoes, looking straight out at the reader.]

[Figure ad: The decorated title page, framed by a climbing rose-tree, with playing-card gardeners among the branches and the roses, the White Rabbit hurrying along the bottom, and a small cat's face beneath the title.]

	*'Tis two score years since Carroll's art,*
	    *With topsy-turvy magic,*
	  *Sent Alice wondering through a part*
	    *Half-comic and half-tragic.*

	*Enchanting Alice! Black-and-white*
	    *Has made your deeds perennial;*
	  *And naught save "Chaos and old Night"*
	    *Can part you now from Tenniel;*

	*But still you are a Type, and based*
	    *In Truth, like Lear and Hamlet;*
	  *And Types may be re-draped to taste*
	    *In cloth-of-gold or camlet.*

	*Here comes afresh Costumier, then;*
	    *That Taste may gain a wrinkle*
	  *From him who drew with such deft pen*
	    *The rags of Rip Van Winkle!*

Austin Dobson.
