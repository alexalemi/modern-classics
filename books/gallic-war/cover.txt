Lionel Royer's *Vercingetorix Throws Down His Arms at the Feet of Julius Caesar* (1899), here from a black-and-white reproduction. The Gaulish leader sits a white horse in armour, his arms flung back, with his weapons on the ground in front of him and Roman standards beyond. Caesar reports the surrender after Alesia in a few flat words near the end of Book Seven: Vercingetorix was surrendered, and the weapons were laid down. Royer gave it a whole canvas.
