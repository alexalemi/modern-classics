the lizard you see in the fire is a salamander
gave me a great box on the ear
the greatest emperors and the greatest kings of the world practise it
a bath of purest molten gold
always demanding the fire for her and for her mother
I have known you these many years for the greatest goldsmith
so that in a moment my mould filled
Adieu, mon ami
Benvenuto truly deserves it
he is much more terrible than you say
