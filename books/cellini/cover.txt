Niklaus Manuel's *Saint Eligius in His Workshop* (1515). Eligius, the patron saint of goldsmiths, sits in a red gown with a hammer, working a gilt cup, beside two craftsmen at the bench; on the shelf behind them stand a chalice, beakers and a covered cup, and the tools lie on the table. It is a goldsmith's shop of Cellini's own boyhood, and the trade he was trained in and never stopped boasting of.
