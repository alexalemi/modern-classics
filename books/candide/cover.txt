Jean-Honoré Fragonard's *The Swing* (1767). A young woman in pink silk flies out on a swing in a dim garden, kicking her slipper into the air toward a young man lying in the roses below, while an older man in the shadows works the ropes. It is the world of the baron's castle, where Candide kisses Cunégonde behind a screen and is kicked out of it at the end of the first chapter.
