Whence, if the axioms of the formalized system of arithmetic are consistent, neither the formula G nor its negation is demonstrable. In short, if the axioms are consistent, G is formally *undecidable*—in the precise technical sense that neither G nor its contradictory can be formally deduced from the axioms.
