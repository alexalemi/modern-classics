Claude Lorrain's *Seaport with the Embarkation of the Queen of Sheba* (1648). The queen of the title is cropped out. What remains is the harbour: porters carry a chest and bales of goods down to a rowing boat, merchant ships ride at anchor beneath a round tower, and the low sun lights the whole basin. Smith argues that a harbour and cheap carriage by water are where the division of labour first grows, and this is the traffic he means.
