61. FIGWORT FAMILY. Order SCROPHULARIACEÆ.

Herbs with a 2-lipped or more or less irregular monopetalous corolla, and 4 stamens in pairs (2 long and 2 short), or only 2 perfect stamens; rarely all 5 present: style 1: the ovary 2-celled and making a many-seeded pod (few-seeded in some Speedwells and Cow-wheat). Flowers often showy. Two lobes always belong to the upper lip, three to the lower.
