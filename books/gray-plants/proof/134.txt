8. POPPY FAMILY. Order PAPAVERACEÆ.

Herbs with a milk-white, yellow, or reddish juice (colorless in Eschscholtzia), which is bitter or acrid and poisonous, alternate leaves, and flowers remarkable for having only 2 (rarely 3) sepals, which fall when the blossom opens, but 4 (or in one case 8 or 12) petals, which fall early. Stamens many, on the receptacle. Pistil one, compound, but almost always one-celled, many-seeded, the seeds borne on the walls or on projections from them (parietal). Eschscholtzia is remarkable for its calyx shaped like a pointed cap or a candle-extinguisher. — In most cases we have only one species of each genus.
