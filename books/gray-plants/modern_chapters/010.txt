4. Moonseed Family. Order Menispermaceæ

Woody climbers, with alternate leaves and small diœcious flowers (as shown in Fig. 167, 168); the sepals and petals each 4 or 6 and both of the same color, and a few one-seeded pistils, becoming small drupes in fruit, with a moon-shaped or kidney-shaped stone. We have two genera of one species each, the first common at the North, the second at the South.

1. Stamens 12 to 20: pistils 2 to 4. Flowers white: leaves rounded and angled shield-shaped. Fruit blue-black, (*Menispérmum*) Moonsekd.

2. Stamens 6, one before each petal. Flowers greenish: leaves heart-shaped. (*Cócculus*) Cocculus.
