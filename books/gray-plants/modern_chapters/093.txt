87. Sweet-Gale Family. Order Myricaceæ

Shrubs (generally low), with fragrant alternate leaves; and with catkins much as in the Birch family, but short and with only one naked blossom under each scale; the ovary forming a little nut or dry drupe.

	Flowers monœcious: fertile catkins round and bur-like: fruit a smooth little nut. Leaves lance-linear, pinnatifid. Fern-like, whence the common name, · · · (*Comptònia*) Sweet-Fern.
	Flowers diœcious: scales of the fertile catkins falling off, and leaving only the small round fruits, which are incrusted with wax, and so appear like drupes. Leaves entire or serrate, · · · (*Myrìca*).
	  One species in wet grounds, N., with wedge-lanceolate pale leaves, · · · (*M. Gale*) Sweet-Gale.
	  One on the sea-coast with lance-oblong, shining leaves, and waxy fruit, · · · (*M. cerífera*) Bayberry.
