64. Borrage Family. Order Borraginaceæ

Herbs with alternate entire leaves, not aromatic, commonly rough: the flowers regular, with a 5-leaved calyx, 5-lobed corolla, 5 stamens on the tube, one style, and a 4-lobed ovary, making 4 akenes. Flowers generally in one-sided raceme-like clusters, coiled up at the tip, and unfolding as the blossoms expand. Innocent mucilaginous and slightly bitter plants, the roots of some species yielding a red dye.
