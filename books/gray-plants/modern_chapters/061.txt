55. Ebony Family. Order Ebenaceæ

Of this small family, we have only one species, a tree, which deserves notice, viz.: —

Persimmon. *Diospýros.*

Tree with alternate thickish leaves; in their axils some trees bear clustered staminate flowers, with a 4-cleft corolla and about 16 stamens; others single and larger perfect flowers, with a 4-lobed corolla and 8 stamens. Calyx 4-cleft, rather large, thickish. Corolla pale yellow. Pistil one, with 4 styles: the ovary ripening into a plum-like fruit, which is very astringent when green, but sweet and yellow and eatable after frosts, containing 8 large and bony flat seeds.  *D. Virginiàna.*
