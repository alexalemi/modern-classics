5. Barberry Family. Order Berberidaceæ

Readily distinguished (with a single exception) by having the sepals and petals in fours, sixes, or eights (not in fives), and with just the same number of stamens as petals, *one before each petal* (on the receptacle), the anthers opening by an uplifted valve or door on each side. Pistil only one. Harmless, except the May-Apple (also called Mandrake), which has rather poisonous roots, although the fruit is innocent and eatable.
