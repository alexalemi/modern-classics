81. Mezereum Family. Order Thymeleaceæ

Shrubs, with very tough and acrid bark; entire generally alternate leaves; and perfect flowers, with a tubular calyx colored like a corolla, bearing 8 or 10 stamens, free from the simple pistil. Ovary one-celled, one-ovuled, making a berry in fruit. — We have one wild plant of the family; *Daphne Mezereum* is a hardy low shrub in gardens, and *D. odora* in houses. Flowers appearing earlier than the leaves.
