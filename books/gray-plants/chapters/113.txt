Series II: Flowerless or Cryptogamous Plants

Plants destitute of flowers, and propagated by spores instead of seeds. See Part I., Paragr. 165, 308, 312–314.

Class III. — Acrogens

This class includes the Ferns, the Horsetails, and the Club-Mosses.

Class IV. — Anophytes

This class includes the Mosses and the Liverworts.

Class V. — Thallophytes

Includes the Lichens, the Algæ or Seaweeds, and the Fungi or Mushrooms.
