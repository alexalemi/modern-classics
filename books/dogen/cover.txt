Sesshū Tōyō's *Huike Offering His Arm to Bodhidharma* (1496). Bodhidharma sits in a white robe facing the wall of a cave, his eyes turned aside. Behind him, the monk Huike stands holding out his own severed arm, the offering that proved he was serious enough to be taught. Dōgen cites this exact scene twice in these two texts: the nine years facing a wall in the Fukanzazengi, and Huike's arm at Shaoshi in the Gakudō Yōjinshū.
