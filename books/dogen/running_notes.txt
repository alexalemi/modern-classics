READABILITY -- ALEX'S CORRECTION OF 2026-09-02, AND THE RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
"I found it hard to read. Remember it is meant to be readable ... as if
your life's work but also excellent story teller and writer."
He was right, and the diagnosis is worth writing down because it is the
failure mode of every from-the-original book in this collection when
there is no crib to lean on:
  - THE CHINESE SENTENCE WAS THE UNIT. Kanbun parallelism ("where it is
    seen, knowing is lost; when it is got, it goes beyond mind") came
    across as parallelism, and English readers do not read that way.
  - EVERY ALLUSION CROSSED AS A BARE PROPER NOUN. Maoqiang and Xishi,
    the Duke of She, a stone of Yan, "four and seven in the west",
    "the hero of the patriarchs' seat", the sixteen-foot golden body,
    Jiashan's cook laughing. Each is a story Dogen's readers knew and
    ours do not. A footnote is the wink the Verne rule forbids, so the
    scholar-storyteller's move is to FOLD THE STORY INTO THE SENTENCE:
    "the Duke of She, who loved dragons -- he had them carved on his
    pillars and painted on his walls, and when a real dragon heard of
    it and came to visit, he ran away."
  - TECHNICAL TERMS STAYED TERMS where the term was not load-bearing:
    "dharma-gate", "the two vehicles", "hearers and solitary buddhas",
    "skilful means", "the eight difficult conditions". Now "teaching",
    "the lesser Buddhist schools", "the wisest disciples", "the eight
    conditions where the Buddha's teaching cannot be heard". The LOCKED
    terms stay locked (beyond thinking, original face, name and gain,
    true teacher, the thought of awakening): those are the machine.
  - PARADOX GOT NO FOOTHOLD. "Be obstructed by the way" is now "let the
    way get in your way, let it stop you, and it is clear where you are
    standing" -- the same claim, with a handhold.
THE RULE, for this book and for the Kenzeiki after it: every sentence
must be one a bright fourteen-year-old could read aloud and follow.
Faithful to the meaning, every clause of the source present, nothing
summarised -- and the reader never made to look anything up. Where
Dogen counts on a story the reader knows, tell the story in the
sentence. Where he uses a term, use the plain word unless the term is
one the book turns on.
THE BAND HELD: 000 went 1.25 -> 1.55 words per character with the
unpacking, 001 1.18 -> 1.21, both inside 1.20-1.65. A retelling that
explains its allusions inline is longer than a crib, and that is the
right direction; a file that got SHORTER in this pass would be the one
to worry about.
The first drafts are kept in legacy_v1/ for comparison.

DECISIONS LOG
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- 2026-09-01 THE SOURCE WAS VERIFIED, NOT ASSUMED. The Wikisource
  transcription was read against the NDL page images of the 1909
  Eiheiji edition for the Gakudō Yōjinshū's first four sections; they
  agree character for character, colophon included. Two witnesses that
  share no code -- which is the only substitute available for the
  missing crib.
- 2026-09-01 A WORD RATIO CANNOT FIRE ON THIS BOOK and is not shipped
  as if it could. Characters-to-words instead, band 1.20-1.65 DERIVED
  from the finished files (measured 1.48 and 1.39). I predicted 2.0-3.0
  before measuring; that band would have failed every correct file.
- 2026-09-01 A PRESENCE TEST CANNOT SEE A DROPPED DUPLICATE. The
  locked-vocabulary check was written as presence and passed when one
  of seven occurrences of 名利 was reworded. Rebuilt as an exact count
  for the fixed compounds (the hume rule), and proved by reintroducing
  the defect.
- 2026-09-01 A PHRASE TEST MUST SEE WHAT THE RENDERER SEES. The number
  check fired on "nine years" because my hard-wrapping had split it
  across a line break -- correct prose, wrong check. All phrase tests
  now run on whitespace-normalised text, as assemble.py joins a
  paragraph's lines before rendering (the epictetus rule).
- 2026-09-02 THE VOLUME IS EXTENDED WITH THE TENZO KYOKUN (1237), from
  the same 1909 Eiheiji edition, NDL volume 3 (pid 823141), printed
  pages 13-24. It belongs here and is not merely convenient: the book
  was two sets of instructions -- how to sit and how to study -- and
  this is the third, how to cook, which Dogen treats as the same
  practice and not a support for it.
  IT ALSO CLOSES A LOOP. The Tenzo Kyokun first surfaced in this
  session inside a 補 note in the Kenzeiki, where the rule required
  dropping it. Here it is the text itself.
- 2026-09-02 THE RATIO CHECK CAUGHT A REAL OMISSION, which is what it
  is for. 002 came in at 0.99 against a band of 1.20-1.65. Diagnosed
  rather than waved through: two passages translated in full were
  measured separately and came out at 1.36 and 0.92, so the text is not
  uniformly denser -- but the diagnosis also turned up a passage I had
  genuinely dropped, the count of the assembly and the rice-grain
  riddle on Luling rice and Guishan's water buffalo, skipped because I
  could not read the column at page resolution. Refetched at high zoom
  through the IIIF region API and translated; that moved the file to
  1.00. ONLY THEN was the remaining gap accepted, as a signed allowance
  in check.RATIO_EXPECT with the argument written out. A band widened
  before the omission was found would have hidden it.
- 2026-09-02 ANOTHER NUMERAL-INSIDE-A-NUMERAL BUG, same family as the
  "nine years" line-break one. 六年 is "six years" in the Fukanzazengi
  but every occurrence in the Tenzo Kyokun is the tail of 嘉定十六年, a
  date. The count now refuses a match preceded by another numeral.
