RUNNING NOTES — five-rings (consistency ledger)

One translator (the session model) did all five scrolls in order, Earth
first to set the voice, so this ledger records what was locked rather than
what was passed between batches. text_analysis.txt has the reasoning.

LOCKED VOCABULARY (caps spent; glossed once at first use, never again)
  兵法 strategy (glossed Earth, "heihō, the way of fighting")
  一分の兵法 / 大分の兵法 strategy on the small scale / on the large scale
  拍子 rhythm (glossed Earth "the timing by which things happen")
  構 guard (glossed Water "A guard, or kamae, is the way you hold your sword")
     五方の構 the five guards: upper, middle, lower, right side, left side
     有構無構 the guard that is no guard
  表/おもて the five forms (first form ... fifth form)
  太刀 long sword; 刀/脇差 named once (tachi/katana; katana/wakizashi), then
     long sword / short sword
  二天一流 Niten Ichiryū, the Two Heavens as One (preface; Water opening)
  二刀一流 Nitō Ichiryū, the Two Swords as One (Earth, then the English only)
  観の目 / 見の目 the eye that perceives (kan) / the eye that looks (ken);
     "Perceive strongly and look weakly." Glossed Water; reused in Wind
     and Void without gloss.
  先 the initiative; 懸の先 initiative of attack, 待の先 initiative of
     waiting, 体々の先 initiative of both together
  空 the void (glossed Earth "kū, which means emptiness"; again in Void)
  鍛/錬 forge / temper
  縁 "a step toward cutting him" (有構無構); "chance" in 縁のあたり
  いつく "fix / fixing in place": "A fixed hand is a dead hand."

REFRAINS (last sentence of the article, never inflated)
  能々吟味有べし        Examine this well.
  鍛錬有べし            Train at this (well).
  工夫有べし            Think this through. / Work this out for yourself.
  能々分別すべし        Think carefully about this.
  稽古有べし            Practice this well.
  口傳                  This is taught by word of mouth.

WORDPLAY REBUILT ON THE ENGLISH (the comic-voice rule)
  枕をおさふる: Musashi says hold down the う of うつ (strike), か of かかる,
  と of とぶ, き of きる. Rendered as the first letters of the English
  verbs: the "s" of "strike", "a" of "attack", "j" of "jump", "c" of "cut".

TITLES OF ARTICLES — English names, Japanese kept inside the text only where
the name is a term or untranslatable: munen musō, shūkō (short-armed monkey),
shikkō (lacquer and glue), katsu-totsu, jikitsū. 鼠頭午首 is printed with 午
(horse) in 1909, which is the OCR or a misprint for 牛 (ox): rendered "Rat's
Head, Ox's Neck". まぎるゝ (Fire) is "Weaving Through" (the text explains it
as つゞらをり, a winding path). まふるゝ is "Blending In".

UNITS AND DATES
  間: 20 ken "about forty yards"; 2-3 ken "twelve or eighteen feet".
  寸: 5 or 3 sun "six inches or four". 里: 40-50 ri "a hundred miles or
  more"; 2-3 ri "five or seven miles".
  Kan'ei 20 = 1643; Shōhō 2.5.12 = June 1645; Kanbun 7 = 1667; 寅の一点
  "about four in the morning". Noh pieces Oimatsu ("The Old Pine") and
  Takasago named with a word of context.

COLOPHONS — kept at the end of each scroll, one paragraph; the Void's
lacks the 1667 line in this print and so does the translation.
