Antoni Brodowski's *Oedipus and Antigone* (1828). The blind old king, in a red cloak and leaning on a rough staff, walks with his eyes closed and his hand on his daughter's shoulder, while she glances aside, watchful, as she leads him. It is how *Oedipus at Colonus*, the last play Sophocles wrote, finds the two of them: the exile arriving at the edge of Athens with Antigone as his eyes.
