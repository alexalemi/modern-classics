cinema, cinematograph, kin-. The cin- forms are obviously more handy for words destined to constant popular use, & should be accepted heartily. circumstance. The objection to under the cc., & insistence that in the cc. is the only right form, because what is round us is not over us, is puerile. To point out that round applies as much to vertical as to horizontal relations, & that a threatening sky is a c. no less than a threatening bulldog (Under the circumstances I decided not to venture), might lay one open to the suspicion of answering fools according to their folly. A more polite reply is that ‘the cc.’ means the state of affairs, & may naturally be conceived as exercising the pressure under which one acts.
