Carl Spitzweg's *The Bookworm* (c. 1850). An old man in a black coat stands at the top of a library ladder, reading a book held close to his face, with another in his other hand, a third under his arm and a fourth clamped between his knees. The sign over the shelves reads *Metaphysik*. Fowler wrote for readers who look things up, and this one has forgotten to come down.
