judicial, judicious. The first has to do with judges & lawcourts & legal judgements, the second with the mental faculty of judgement. Judicial murder is murder perpetrated by means of a legal trial; judicious murder is murder that is well calculated to serve the murderer’s interests. The distinction is clear enough, except that judicial has one usc that brings it near judicious; this use is impartial or such as might be expected of a judge or a lawcourt, applied to such wo as view, conduct, care, lin onda to which judicious is also applicable in the sense of wise or sagacious or prudent.
