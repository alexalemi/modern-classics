incognito. The inflexions are of no great importance, being now little used, but should be done right if at all. Of the personal noun incognito, incognita, incogniti, are the masculine, feminine, & plural, = man, woman, people, of concealed identity. The abstract noun, = anonymity &c., is incognito only, with possible plural incognitos (never dropping their incogniios, or usually incognito). The adverb or predicative adjective (travelling 1.) is usually -to irrespective of read & number; if declined, it is like the personal noun.
