AUGUSTINE — RUNNING CONSISTENCY LEDGER
======================================
Read text_analysis.txt first. This file is the accumulated set of locked
decisions; it is updated as files are completed. Anything locked here is
spent — use it and do not re-gloss it.


THE PRONOUN RULE (locked before file 000, applies to all 36 files)

  God              -> You / Your / Yours / Yourself, CAPITALISED
                      He / Him / His capitalised only where a sentence
                      contains both God and a man and the reference
                      would otherwise be ambiguous. Not reflexively.
  the reader, men  -> you / your, lower case
  Christ           -> He/Him capitalised, same rule

Never "Thou" anywhere, in any register, including inside quoted
Scripture. The whole point of the decision is that the book has ONE
voice, and re-archaising the quotations would put a frame around them
that Augustine did not put there.

NEAR-MISSES REGISTERED, so a variant is caught the way the Nights'

NOT "Too late I loved You" (the settled English is "Late have I loved
You"); NOT "beauty ever ancient, ever new" (that is Sheed's, and it
re-archaises); NOT "Take it and read", NOT "Pick it up and read".

FALSE FRIENDS in Pusey, all four confirmed present in this text and all
four are the silent kind — they read as modern words and mean something
else: presently (= at once), conversation (= way of life), want (=
lack), prevent (= go before), sensible (= perceptible by the senses).


REGISTER BY BOOK

  1-9    memoir. Fast, funny, self-interrupting, told at speed.
  10     memory. Hinge book; still first-person but now analytic.
  11-13  argument. Time, creation, Genesis. Handle as hume/ is handled:
         clear the apparatus, keep the joints visible. He prays in the
         middle of the argument — keep that, it is not decoration.

KEEP THE RHETORICAL FIGURES. The piled antitheses ("most hidden, yet
most present") are professional equipment and unstacking them guts the
passage. This reverses the project's usual instruction and is the single
easiest way to lose 10% of the word count without noticing.


DECISIONS LOG

(files 000-004, Books One to Three)

THE VERNE RULE, applied and NOT flagged, in Book Three: Augustine's
condemnation of the men of Sodom, which he treats as an offence against
nature to be detested and punished everywhere and always. Rendered
plainly and in full — neither sharpened nor softened, and with no note.
It is his argument, made as part of his subject (the distinction
between what offends God and what merely offends local custom), and a
note would be the wink the rule forbids. Same handling as bunyan/'s
Giant Pope and quixote/'s Ricote.

"believe much of it on the word of mere women" (Book One): Pusey has
"weak females" for Augustine's diminutive MULIERCULIS, which is
dismissive in the Latin too. Kept dismissive. This is his attitude, not
the translator's addition, so unlike Pusey's "Thou" it stands.

THE "WRECKERS" (Book Three): Pusey's "Subverters" for EVERSORES, the
student gang whose name was the badge of style at Carthage. Rendered
"Wreckers" so that Augustine's pun survives — he says they were wrecked
themselves first. A transliteration would lose the whole point of the
paragraph.

PIN ADDED AT FILE 004: "it is not possible that the son of these tears
should perish". Written from the source, not from memory.
