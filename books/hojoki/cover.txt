Kikuchi Yōsai's portrait of Kamo no Chōmei, a woodblock print from his *Zenken Kojitsu*, a nineteenth-century book of portraits and lives of famous Japanese. Drawn in loose ink lines, Chōmei sits in a monk's robe with his shaven head tipped back and his eyes on the sky. It is the hermit of the last pages, an old man who has given up everything but his robe and his thoughts, and is still asking questions of himself.
