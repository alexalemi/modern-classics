aa	The Bellman at the masthead, ringing his bell, above a sail lettered with the title, the whole inside a border of knotted rope.
ab	The crew aboard ship: a man in a ruff and night-cap grips the rail above, and below, seen through the hatch, others crowd the cabin, one of them weighing with a pair of scales.
ac	The Butcher, a round-faced boy in a cap, kneels on the deck with a ruler, while the Beaver sits upright at a little table working at the lace on its pillow.
ad	A chart with nothing on it at all: an empty ruled frame marked North, South, East and West, with the Equator, the Poles, the Zenith and the rest named round its edges, and a scale of miles.
ae	The Baker’s uncle, bald and wild-eyed, sits up in bed and lifts a warning hand, while the young Baker kneels at the bedside with his face hidden; through the window a ship lies at the quay.
af	The crew set out on the hunt, carrying forks, thimbles and other odd gear, with the Beaver at their feet and a young woman in a loose gown walking among them.
ag	The Butcher sits on a rock in a rocky gorge with a book on his knee, working his sum, the Beaver beside him, while bats, birds and strange small creatures crowd round them.
ah	The Barrister lies asleep at the foot of the picture, and above him, in his dream, a court sits: a figure in wig and gown holds up a scroll of charges while judges and jurymen look on.
ai	The Banker, black in the face, sits wailing in a carved chair, while the Bellman rings his bell at his ear and the Beaver, in a ruff, looks on.
aj	A dark tangle of rock and undergrowth in which a great shadowy face can just be made out, with the Bellman’s bell at the top corner and a hand reaching in from below.
ak	A bell-buoy riding a heavy sea under a stormy sky with gulls, lettered round its sides “It was a Boojum”.
