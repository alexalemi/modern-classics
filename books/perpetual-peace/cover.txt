Gottlieb Doebler's portrait of Immanuel Kant (1791), painted four years before this book, when Kant was sixty-seven. He sits in a brown coat with a lace jabot and cuffs, his powdered hair rolled at the sides, turning to look past the painter. The oval frame is part of the painting. It shows the philosopher at the age he wrote Toward Perpetual Peace, in the city he hardly ever left.
