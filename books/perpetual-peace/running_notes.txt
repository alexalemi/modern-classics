LOCKED VOCABULARY
Recht = right; Staatsrecht = civil right (ius civitatis); Völkerrecht =
the right of nations (ius gentium); Weltbürgerrecht = cosmopolitan right
(ius cosmopoliticum). Völkerbund = league of nations; Föderalism freier
Staaten = federation of free states; Friedensbund = league of peace
(foedus pacificum) vs Friedensvertrag = peace treaty (pactum pacis);
Völkerstaat = state of nations (civitas gentium); Weltrepublik = world
republic; the "negative substitute". Form of sovereignty (forma imperii:
autocracy, aristocracy, democracy) vs form of government (forma
regiminis: republican or despotic). Republicanism = separating executive
from legislative power. Democracy "in the proper sense of the word" is
"necessarily a despotism". Hospitality = right to visit, NOT right to be
a guest. Erlaubnisgesetz = permissive law; leges strictae / latae kept.
moral politician / political moralist; despotizing moralists /
moralizing politicians (Appendix One). Staatsklugheit = political
prudence; Staatsweisheit = political wisdom; Klugheitslehre = doctrine
of prudence. Publicität = publicity. Vorsehung = Providence (capital P).
Maxim, a priori, transcendental kept; "transcendental" glossed once (007).
