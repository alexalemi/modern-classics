TOWARD PERPETUAL PEACE -- TEXT ANALYSIS
Immanuel Kant, Zum ewigen Frieden. Ein philosophischer Entwurf
(Königsberg: Nicolovius, 1795; "neue vermehrte Auflage" 1796)

THE TEXT
The 1795 first edition (Gutenberg #46873) with the one thing the 1796 second
edition added: the Second Supplement, "A Secret Article for Perpetual
Peace" (from the Akademie-Ausgabe VIII 368-369, which prints 1796). A
word-level diff of the whole AA against #46873 found nothing else
substantive -- spelling, a dozen single words, and the corrected definition
of freedom in note 4 (applied). 16,700 German words. Kant's sixteen
footnotes are part of the argument (the threefold division of right, the
definition of freedom and equality, hereditary nobility, permissive laws,
Providence, the hunting life, the Arctic driftwood, the Tibet/Konx Ompax
excursus, "difference of religions") and are kept in full, each as a
"Footnote:" paragraph after its citing paragraph.

WHAT IT IS
A short treatise cast as a mock peace treaty: six Preliminary Articles
(prohibitions that clear the ground), three Definitive Articles (the
positive conditions), two Supplements (a "guarantee" clause and a "secret
article"), and two Appendices on morality and politics. The treaty
format is a joke Kant keeps up all the way through: the preface is a
lawyer's saving clause, the Secret Article is a contradiction he
enjoys. The tone is dry, ironic, often sarcastic about statesmen,
lawyers and the "sorry comforters" of international law (Grotius,
Pufendorf, Vattel). Underneath is a rigorous argument from right.

STYLE AND DIFFICULTY
Kant's difficulty is his own: long periodic sentences with parentheses
inside parentheses, technical vocabulary from his practical philosophy,
Latin legal tags, and a habit of building the point at the end of the
sentence. Strategy: split at his own joints (semicolons, dashes, the
"denn"/"mithin"/"folglich" hinges), keep every clause and every
parenthesis, keep the logical connectives explicit ("so", "therefore",
"for"), and put the verb near the subject. Keep his sarcasm sharp and his
jokes as jokes. Never smooth the argument into a summary.

LOCKED VOCABULARY (the distinctions the argument turns on)
- Recht = right (not "law"); Gesetz = law. Staatsrecht = the right of a
  state / civil right; Völkerrecht = the right of nations (international
  right); Weltbürgerrecht = cosmopolitan right. Ius civitatis / gentium /
  cosmopoliticum kept in parentheses where Kant gives them.
- Republican vs. democratic. Form of sovereignty (forma imperii: who
  rules -- autocracy, aristocracy, democracy) vs. form of government
  (forma regiminis: how -- republican or despotic). "Republican" means
  separation of the executive from the legislative power and
  representation; DEMOCRACY in Kant's sense (direct rule by all) is
  "necessarily a despotism". Render that as his claim, unblurred, and
  never use "republic" to mean "democracy" or vice versa. Autokratie =
  autocracy.
- Völkerbund = a league (federation) of nations / of peoples; Föderalism
  freier Staaten = a federation of free states; Völkerstaat = a state of
  nations (a world state, which he rejects); Weltrepublik = world
  republic; the "negative surrogate" = a league that wards off war.
- Hospitalität (Wirthbarkeit) = hospitality: the right of a stranger not
  to be treated as an enemy -- a right to VISIT (Besuchsrecht), not a
  right to be a guest (Gastrecht).
- Naturzustand = the state of nature; Friedenszustand must be
  "established" (gestiftet).
- Präliminarartikel / Definitivartikel = Preliminary / Definitive
  Article. Zusatz = Supplement. Anhang = Appendix.
- Erlaubnisgesetz = permissive law; leges strictae / latae = strict /
  broad laws.
- moralischer Politiker = the moral politician; politischer Moralist =
  the political moralist (the villain of Appendix I). Never swap.
- Staatsklugheit = political prudence; Staatsweisheit = political
  wisdom; Klugheitslehre = doctrine of prudence.
- Publicität = publicity; the transcendental formula of public right:
  "All actions relating to the right of other human beings are wrong if
  their maxim is incompatible with publicity."
- Natur as artist (natura daedala rerum), Vorsehung = Providence,
  Garantie/Gewähr = guarantee.
- Maxime = maxim (glossed once as a personal rule of action).
- a priori, transcendental: kept, glossed once in plain words.

ALLUSIONS TO FOLD INTO THE SENTENCE (never a note of ours)
Eleven skittles at one throw (bowling all the pins at once -- the
theorist's harmless fantasy); Jesuit casuistry (mental reservation);
Augustus's "ad calendas graecas" (never); the Bulgarian prince and the
smith's tongs; Mallet du Pan and Pope's couplet; Frederick the Great
("the first servant of the state"); Grotius, Pufendorf, Vattel; Virgil's
"furor impius" (Aeneid I); Lucretius' "natura daedala rerum"; Seneca's
"fata volentem ducunt"; the Ostyaks and Samoyeds; Bouterwek's lines;
"fac et excusa", "si fecisti nega", "divide et impera"; Garve's essay;
"fiat iustitia"; 
The long Tibet note (Konx Ompax, Georgius' Alphabetum
Tibetanum, Fischer, the Eleusinian mysteries) is Kant's own learned
excursus: keep it whole, plainly.

SENSITIVE CONTENT
A canonical work of political philosophy; translate as scholarly
modernization, his argument as his. The Third Definitive Article's attack
on European colonialism ("the inhospitable conduct of the civilized
states", the East India companies, the "sugar islands, the seat of the
cruelest and most calculated slavery") is kept at full strength. The
hunting-life note and the Arctic-peoples passage reflect 1795 ethnography
(Ostyaks, Samoyeds, "Eskimos" -- Kant's word is "Eskimos"; keep his names
as period names, no slurs are involved). Kant's view that a people has no
right of rebellion (Appendix II) is his and is rendered as his. No cuts.

TARGET
A bright fourteen-year-old should be able to read any sentence aloud and
follow it. American spelling. Ratio expected ~1.25-1.4 English words per
German word; MEASURED 1.15 (files 1.13-1.21). Smith's 1.3 is Victorian
padding, not extra content: every Latin term and all 16 notes are present,
and the parenthesis count drops only where Kant's asides were unbracketed
into sentences (001 42 -> 35, 003 55 -> 47).
