Jean Siméon Chardin's *The Kitchen Maid* (1738). A woman in a white cap and apron sits in a plain kitchen with a knife in one hand and a turnip in the other. More turnips lie on the floor, the peeled ones wait in a bowl of water, and copper pans lean against the table. It is the everyday work of a kitchen, which is what Farmer's book set out to make reliable.
