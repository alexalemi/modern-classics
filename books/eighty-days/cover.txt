Léon Benett's engraving for the first illustrated edition (1873), published by Hetzel. A sledge rigged with a sail runs across a frozen plain under a dark sky. Fogg sits upright in his top hat, Aouda is wrapped in rugs beside him, and the others crowd behind while the owner holds the tiller. Having lost their train at Fort Kearney in Nebraska, they reach Omaha by this wind-driven sledge, one more improvised connection in a race against the timetable.
