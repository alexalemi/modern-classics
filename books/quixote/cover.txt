Gustave Doré's frontispiece to *Don Quixote* (1863), an engraving cut on the wood by H. Pisan, whose name is at the foot of the plate. The old gentleman sits in his chair with a book open on his knee and a sword raised in his other hand, and out of the air around him pour the contents of his library: knights on horseback, a dragon, ladies in distress, a giant's severed head on the floor. Two mice play among the fallen books. It is the first chapter, the one in which the reading turns his brain.
