Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Howard's own diagram No. 1, "The Three Magnets." Two horseshoe magnets, Town and Country, pull from above at a block marked "The People," with Howard's question beneath it: where will they go? The third magnet, Town-Country, pulls from below. Howard's text says it plainly: each city may be regarded as a magnet, each person as a needle.
