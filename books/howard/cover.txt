John Constable's *Hampstead Heath Looking Towards Harrow* (1821). A family walks a sandy path under three tall pines, and beyond them the country falls away in green and blue toward the horizon under a big grey sky. Constable painted the heath in the years he lived at Hampstead, then a village on a hill a few miles out of London: open country within reach of the city, which is what Howard's town-country was meant to offer everyone.
