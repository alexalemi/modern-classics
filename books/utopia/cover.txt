Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Raphael Hythloday's description of the island in Book Two. It is shaped like a crescent moon, with the sea coming in between the horns through a strait eleven miles wide to a great sheltered bay, and fifty-four cities stand on it, with the capital, Amaurot, in the middle. The line at the top is More's.
