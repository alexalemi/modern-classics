Ambrosius Holbein's woodcut map *The Island of Utopia* (1518), made for an early edition of More's book. The crescent island is crowded with walled towns, with the capital, Amaurot, at the top and the source and mouth of the river Anyder labelled on either side. In the foreground a ship lies at anchor, and a bearded man marked Hythlodaeus points across the water for a listener. The traveller is telling the story, which is how More's Book Two works.
