ca	A dark shore: at left a figure with a radiant head throws up both arms, while a huge serpent coils over bodies lying on the ground.
cb	A spiral stair winds up through a starry night toward a light at the top, with robed figures climbing it and others gathered at its foot.
cc	A monstrous figure with a scaly, fish-like head and a gaping fanged mouth, its body bound in scaly coils, under bat-like wings and a roof of jagged spikes.
cd	A long-bearded old man crouches over an open book before two tall stone tablets, under a dark vaulted arch.
ce	A row of hooded, long-robed figures stands on a sloping rock floor inside a cavern, its mouth opening onto a dark sky.
cf	A great bat-winged creature spreads across a stormy sky above heaving waves, with a rayed sun at its left and a crescent moon at its right.
cg	Two naked figures locked together in a dark hollow ringed with spiky shapes, one folded over the other and gripping its head.
ch	Inside a rocky cave beside a gnarled tree, a group of women in long gowns stand together, several raising their arms above their heads.
ci	An old man with an immense flowing beard sits enthroned among rocks, his arms spread wide along the stone on either side.
cj	A figure lies stretched along a rock above dark water, while another figure crouches on the cliff high above.
ck	A sea serpent's coils rise out of rough water in three great arches, the nearest framing a pale disc.
cl	A bearded old figure leans over a huge open book whose pages are covered in rough script.
cm	Against a black sky, a crouching figure inside a great disc reaches down with a pair of compasses whose legs spread in two thin rays toward the ground.
cn	A bearded, long-haired old man sits squarely facing out, hands on his knees, with dark shapes fanning out behind his head; the picture is screened in a heavy grid.
co	A tall robed figure stands beside a great coiled, ribbed form, the picture screened in a fine grid.
cp	A low, grid-screened scene of bowed figures and a bearded face at the left, half lost in the dark of a cave.
cq	A muscular man runs or leaps across a hillside with a cloak streaming behind him, his hands clutching his head, beside a black disc.
cr	A naked woman stands on a coiled serpent in a dark wood, her head turned aside, the serpent's body winding around her.
cs	A powerful naked man strides out from between two stone tablets inscribed with writing, his hair and beard streaming.
ct	A nude figure hunched on a rock bends over a scroll on the ground, measuring a figure drawn on it with a pair of compasses.
cu	A narrow band of figures swept along in rolling flames, their arms raised and bodies twisted together.
cv	A man with arms spread wide rises above a dark landscape, light breaking around his head and dark wing-like shapes opening behind him.
cw	A ring of figures dances in a woodland clearing, arms raised and hands joined, around a slender pole hung with garlands.
