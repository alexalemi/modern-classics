William Blake's *Behemoth and Leviathan* (1825), plate 15 of his engravings for the Book of Job. God leans down from a cloud between two angels and points below, past Job and his wife and friends, to a circle holding the two monsters: Behemoth on land and Leviathan coiled in the sea. Hobbes took the name for his state from Job's Leviathan, "king over all the children of pride", and Perlman took his twenty-seven plates from Blake.
