# Pins written FROM the finished chapters (the epictetus rule), for the
# things this edition must not lose. Each is a line a reader of the
# book will remember, or a document line whose exact wording carries
# the evidence.

# Chapter One: the voice in the air, and the funeral.
this child has had no equal in five hundred years
he sat and watched the smoke go up from the incense

# Chapter Two: the question that drove him off Hiei.
why did the buddhas of the past, the present and the future ever have to make up their minds to seek awakening

# Chapter Three: the youngest man in the room.
Then I, who was the most junior of them all, said

# Chapter Five: the seating dispute, which is the first thing he did in China.
Brothers are brothers, and the natural order between them is not to be confused.

# Chapter Seven: Rujing's open door, and the awakening.
This old monk will overlook your want of ceremony exactly as a father overlooks his own child's.
body and mind have dropped away, and I have come
take in one man, or half a man, and do not let my school be cut off

# Chapter Eight: the poem everybody remembers.
the sound of night rain over the quiet lodging at Fukakusa

# Chapter Nine: what he was building, in his own words.
the practice of the way not interrupted for a moment, day or night
It is rank grass and no monastery yet.

# Chapter Thirteen: the homecoming verse, and Genmyo.
my love for the mountain is deeper than it was at first
this gladness of Genmyo's was filthy

# Chapter Fourteen: the purple robe, and the bell nobody else heard.
will laugh at this one old man
all say they heard nothing

# Chapter Fifteen: Ejo's regret.
that we shall never see the draft of the hundred essays

# Chapter Sixteen: the death verse, exactly.
Living, I plunge into the Yellow Springs.
He threw down the brush and passed away.
