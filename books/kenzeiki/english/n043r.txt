One day the old man Rujing was rebuking a monk who had fallen asleep
sitting. He said: in zazen body and mind must drop away. What is the
good of just sitting there sleeping?

The Founder, sitting beside him, was suddenly and greatly awakened.

He went straight up to the abbot's room and burned incense.

Rujing asked: what is the incense for?

The Founder said: body and mind have dropped away, and I have come.

Rujing said: body and mind dropped away. Dropped away body and mind.

The Founder said: this is only a passing knack. Do not put your seal on
me carelessly.

Rujing said: I am not putting my seal on you carelessly.

The Founder said: and what is it that is not careless sealing?

Rujing said: dropped away body and mind.

The Founder bowed.

Guangping, the attendant from Fuzhou, was standing beside them, and
said: that a man from another country should get this far is truly no
small matter.

The Founder withdrew with respect.
