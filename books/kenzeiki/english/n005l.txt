One. Somebody has found fault with this undertaking, saying: what an
extreme piece of idle amusement. We cannot think that is right. The
deeds of the sages and worthies of former ages — everything that
writing cannot reach — have always been supplied by pictures. Are the
mandalas of the Diamond and Womb realms, or the paintings of the
nirvana at Kusinagara, to be slandered as idle amusement too? Writing
and painting alike turn back a contrary current; they are provisions
made to meet the occasion. Would those upstream make sport of a
breaking of rules like this one?
