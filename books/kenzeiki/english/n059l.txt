A board survives with fifteen lines cut into it, and it is now at
Myokakuji in Echizen. The brush strokes are unmistakably the Founder's
own. It reads:

	Respectfully inviting the deities of ten places as protectors of
	Myokakuji.

	First, Kumano. Second, Nissho. Third, Kinpusen. Fourth, Kamo.
	Fifth, Hachiman. Sixth, Gion. Seventh, Sekizan. Eighth, Hiyoshi.
