	The first day of spring, the fifth year of Kangen, the year of

There is another such sheet in the abbot's room at Tendenji at
Tenma in Naniwa, seventy-eight characters:

	Homage and refuge to the Buddha, the dharma and the sangha; to
	the seven buddhas and the twenty-eight ancestors; to the six
	ancestors of the eastern land; to the founders of this house
	and the masters and disciples of the Soto line. May temples be
	founded, the way handed on and the work taught, gods and men
	brought on, and all be peaceful and at rest. May the
	cloud-robed gather in crowds, may long life and fortune
	flourish, may everything go as it should and the dharma be
	spread in plenty — great good fortune, great good fortune,
	great good fortune. The turn of the year towards the light —
	great good fortune, great good fortune, great good fortune,
	great good fortune.

	Kigen [cipher]

The first year of Hoji — the era-name was changed on the
twenty-eighth day of the third month.

His going down to Kamakura on the third day of the eighth month of
that year was at the request of the lord of Saimyoji, whose dharma
name was Doso.
