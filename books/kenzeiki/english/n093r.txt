The monk sent with the deed of gift was the head monk Genmyo. He was
delighted at the grant of the fief and went about telling the assembly
of it. When the Founder heard of it, he said that this gladness of his
was filthy, and expelled him from the temple then and there; and it is
handed down that he had the very platform Genmyo had sat zazen on cut
out and taken away. Nothing like it had been known before.

As for that Genmyo: about a hundred and thirty years after the
Founder's death, a wandering monk met him on Mount Hakone in Izu. I am
Genmyo, he said, who was head monk of Eiheiji in Echizen — and he told
the pilgrim stories of the Founder's lifetime, leaning on a bamboo
staff and standing there as he talked. The pilgrim afterwards came to
Eiheiji and told them of it.
