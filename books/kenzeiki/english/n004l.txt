One. Things like Ejo's taking the whisk we have for the present left
unillustrated, because a picture of it would be confused with the
founder's own ceremonies. As for the rest, where a picture would add
little we have here and there left it out, for fear of clutter. Do not
charge us with carelessness.

One. Kenzei wrote in the plain syllabary for the sake of lay believers,
and his intention in it was deep and earnest. That we should now be
adding pictures on top of it is no better than the ugly woman of the
village trying to make good her looks by copying another's frown.
