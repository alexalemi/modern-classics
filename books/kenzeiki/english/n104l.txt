	After that his illness grew heavier and heavier by degrees, and
	so this was the teaching he left as his command. It is our
	misfortune, and the thing I most regret, that we shall never
	see the draft of the hundred fascicles. Anyone who loves and
	longs for our late master must copy this fascicle out and keep
	it safe. This is the Buddha's last command, and it is our late
	master's last testament.

	Now, on the fourteenth day of the seventh month of the seventh
	year of Kencho, the year of the hare, I have had Gien write it
	out, and the copying is finished; and I have collated it
	against the original at the same time. Recorded by Ejo.

The colophon of the original reads: written from the end of the fourth
year of Kencho to the sixth day of the first month of the fifth year.
Eiheiji.

From old times, it is said, those who have looked into this fascicle
have been moved to tears, and an abbot expounding it has wept aloud.
If in the last age this testament is kept, the manner of our school
will be fanned into flame for ever, the line will flow on, and it will
not fall away.

Since the fascicle is long, only the names of the eight are set down
here.
