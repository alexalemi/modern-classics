the midday meal the honourable meal; you may say the hour of the meal,
but never simply the hour. When you send word about the rice, say that
the rice is to be washed clean and served, and never that it is to be
rinsed. Say that the honourable soup is to be made and served, and
never simply soup. Say that the honourable broth is to be made and
served. Say of the gruel that it has been brought to the boil for them.
When you are serving, never blow with your breath on the rice or the
greens or on anything else. Even if your sleeve is dry, do not let it
touch the food. If your hand has touched your face and you have not yet
washed it, do not lay it on the bowls of gruel or meal, or on the gruel
or meal itself. From the picking over of the rice and greens, through
to the making up of the rice and broth and the serving of them — if at
any point in the work you have scratched an itch anywhere on your body,
