Chapter Six: Wandering, and the Plum Blossom

In the first year of Baoqing, the year of the rooster, the Founder was
lodging at Tiantong and coming up to twenty-six. Wuji had approved him
several times over, but he would not accept it of himself.

He left Tiantong and went to see Zheweng Ruyan at Jingshan.

Ruyan asked: when did you get here?
The Founder said: the fourth month of last year.
Ruyan said: you came along with the crowd, just so.
The Founder said: since I came along with the crowd, just so — then
what is it that does not come along with the crowd?
Ruyan said: sit down a while and drink your tea.

He went on to Cuiyan in Taizhou, met Zhuo, and asked at once: what is
the Buddha?
Zhuo said: the one inside the hall.
The Founder said: if he is the one inside the hall, how is it that he
fills the sands of the Ganges?
Zhuo said: he fills the sands of the Ganges.
The Founder said: you have fallen into your own words.

He travelled to Ayuwang again, and seeing the round moon of Nagarjuna
drawn on a stone plinth there, he put Chenggui the guest-prefect to the
test.

He went on to the Wannian monastery at Pingtian, met the abbot Yuanzi,
and was allowed to look at his succession document and bow to it.
Afterwards Yuanzi had a dream about him, and thought a great deal of
him as a vessel for the dharma. There was a second dream as well, and
the Founder was deeply moved by the connection between them.

— the transmission document, or not? Dogen said: how might I be allowed
to see it?

The elder then rose himself, held up the document and said: this is a
thing not shown even to a man's own kin, nor to an attendant monk of
many years' standing. That is the rule of the buddhas and ancestors.

But yesterday, when I had come out of the city and was staying in the
prefectural town, I had a dream. There was an eminent monk in it who
seemed to be Chan master Fachang of Damei, and he held up a branch of
plum blossom and said: if there should come a real man who has already
crossed the ship's rail, do not begrudge him the flower. And he gave
the plum blossom to me. Without knowing what I did, I recited in the
dream: thirty good blows for the man who has not yet crossed the ship's
rail. And this document has plum blossom in its damask, besides.

Now, meeting you, elder brother, who have already crossed the ship's
rail — this must be what Damei was teaching. It agrees with the dream.

The Founder went round from place to place and met the teachers there,
but because he found no one of surpassing capacity fit to be his
master, he set out to visit Wuji again and go back to Tiantong. On the
road he heard that Wuji had died. He grieved over it greatly, and from
then on the thought of going home began to come up in him. So he went
to call on Myozen, who was lodging at Tiantong, and climbed the
mountain a second time.

On the way there was a monk called Laogui, who said to him: of all the
masters under heaven today there is none beyond Chan master Rujing. He
has lately taken up residence at Tiantong at the emperor's request. You
should go up the mountain at once and see him.

The Founder took this for good fortune, and hurried up to Tiantong.
