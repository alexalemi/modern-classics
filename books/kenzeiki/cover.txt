The frontispiece of this book's own illustrated edition of 1806, a woodcut by Rinnō Daiken. The inscription at the top reads *The True Likeness of Zen Master Dōgen, Founder of Eihei*. He sits upright in a carved chair, in a dark robe under a pale outer robe, holding a whisk across his lap, his shoes set out on a footstool before him. It is the formal portrait of a Zen master, and it stands before all the pictures of his life that follow.
