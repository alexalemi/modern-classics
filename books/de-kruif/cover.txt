Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A round field of view, as through a lens, is crowded with tiny rods, spheres, chains and spirals: the "little animals" Antony van Leeuwenhoek found in a drop of rain water and shouted for his daughter to come and see. De Kruif begins his book with that moment, the first time anyone saw a microbe, and the line at the top is Leeuwenhoek's cry.
