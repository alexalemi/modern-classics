George Caleb Bingham's *The County Election* (1852). Voters crowd the steps of a courthouse to cast their votes with the clerks inside, under a banner reading "The Will of the People the Supreme Law." Below them men argue, drink, hand out tickets and lean on each other; two boys play a game in the dirt and a dog wanders through. Bingham was a Missouri painter who ran for office himself, and this is the American democracy Tocqueville went to see, painted from the inside.
