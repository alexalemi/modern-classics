# Every pin below was copied out of burke/chapters/ and then rendered
# into the modern form the translation is REQUIRED to use. Matching is
# case- and whitespace-insensitive but NOT punctuation-insensitive, so
# the pins avoid internal commas wherever possible.
#
# The lesson these encode, from epictetus/ and re-learned in augustine/:
# PIN FROM THE TEXT, then decide the modern form deliberately. Never
# write a pin from a recollection of how the famous quotation goes.
#
# MOST OF THESE ARE PINNED UNCHANGED, and for a reason particular to
# this book. Burke's reputation rests on about eight sentences, and
# every one of them is already modern English at the level of the word;
# what is hard about the Reflections is the 200-word periodic sentences
# AROUND them. The hume rule therefore governs with unusual force: if a
# sentence is already clear, leave it alone. A translation that
# "improved" these would destroy the only parts of the book most
# readers have ever met.

# Section Eight. The most famous sentence in the book, and the reason
# it is still read. Pinned UNCHANGED, in all three of its parts.
the age of chivalry is gone
sophisters
the glory of Europe is extinguished

# Section Eight, four paragraphs earlier: the vision at Versailles that
# the chivalry passage is the payoff to. Pinned UNCHANGED.
I saw the queen of France

# Section Eight. The phrase that made Burke hated for a century, and
# which he never retracted. It STAYS, unsoftened -- see the ledger.
swinish multitude

# Section Ten. The definition of society that is Burke's real legacy,
# and the passage conservatives and radicals have both claimed since.
# Pinned UNCHANGED across the whole triad.
a partnership in all science
# NOTE the commas. The header above prefers pins without internal
# punctuation, but this phrase is a three-item list and cannot
# lose them without the pin demanding prose that is wrong. The
# pin follows the required rendering; it does not dictate it.
those who are living, those who are dead, and those who are to be born

# Section Two. The sentence that makes him something other than a
# reactionary, and the one his opponents least expect. Pinned UNCHANGED.
A state without the means of some change is without the means of its conservation

# Section Six (file 005). The origin of one of the most quoted phrases in
# political writing, and it means something narrower than it is usually
# made to mean: the small group you actually belong to.
the little platoon

# NOT PINNED, deliberately: "revolution", "liberty" and "France", which
# cannot fail and would read as coverage without being any. The grimm
# rule.
