Hubert Robert's *The Bastille in the First Days of Its Demolition* (1789). The great round tower of the fortress stands in raking light, with a crew of workmen strung along its broken top tearing it down, and smoke and rubble below among small figures. Robert painted it in the year the Bastille fell, the year before Burke wrote, and it shows what his book is about: how quickly an old structure comes down once people decide it should.
