Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Holmes's account in the first lecture of how a rule outlives its reason. The same tablet stands four times in a row: its old content drains away and a new one fills it, and by the last the outline itself has changed shape. The line at the top is his: the old form receives a new content, and in time even the form modifies itself to fit the meaning which it has received.
