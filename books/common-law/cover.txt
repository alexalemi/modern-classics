*Court of Common Pleas, Westminster Hall* (1808), an aquatint from *The Microcosm of London* by Thomas Rowlandson, who drew the figures, and Augustus Charles Pugin, who drew the architecture. Three judges in full wigs lean on their desks under the royal arms, counsel sit below them, law books are stacked on the floor, and wigged lawyers crowd the benches in front. This was one of the old courts where the common law Holmes traces was argued and made.
