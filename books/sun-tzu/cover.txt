Shang Xi's *Guan Yu Captures General Pang De* (c. 1430), part of a Ming dynasty hanging scroll. The general Guan Yu sits under a pine in green and gold armour with his officers around him, deciding the fate of a captured enemy. Pang De was a general of Cao Cao, and Cao Cao, under the name Ts'ao Kung, is the earliest of the commentators in this book.
