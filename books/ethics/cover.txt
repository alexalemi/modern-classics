Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Aristotle's example in Book II. If ten pounds of food is too much and two too little, the mean of the thing itself is six, but the trainer will not simply order six: it is too little for Milo the wrestler and too much for a beginner. Virtue, he argues, is a mean of this second kind, relative to the person. The line at the top is his.
