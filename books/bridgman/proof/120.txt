PROBLEMS

1. ^^The^^ gas constant in the equation \(pv = RT\), has the value 0.08207 when the pressure \(p\) is expressed in atmospheres, the volume \(v\) is the volume in liters of 1 gm mol, and \(T\) is absolute Centigrade degrees. What is \(R\) when \(p\) is expressed in \(\text{dynes/cm}^2\) and \(v\) is in \(\text{cm}^3\)?

2. The thermal conductivity of copper is 0.92 cal. per \(\text{cm}^2\) per sec per 1° per cm temperature gradient. What is it in B.T.U. per hour per square foot for a temperature gradient of 1° Fahrenheit per foot? (This last is the engineering unit.)

3. If the numerical value of \(e^2/ch\) is 0.001161 in terms of the gm, cm, and sec, what is its value in terms of the ton, mile, and hour? \(e\) is the charge of the electron in E.S.U., \(c\) is the velocity of light in empty space, and \(h\) is Planck's quantum of action.

4. The thrust exerted by an air propeller varies with the number of revolutions per second and the speed of advance along the axis of revolution. Show that the critical speed of advance at which the thrust vanishes is proportional to the number of revolutions per second.

5. Show that the acceleration toward the center of a particle moving uniformly in a circle of radius \(r\) is \(\text{Const}\, v^2/r\).

6. Show that the time of transverse vibration of a heavy stretched wire is \(\text{Const} \times \text{length} \times (\text{linear density/tension})^{\frac{1}{2}}\).

7. The time of longitudinal vibration of a bar is \(\text{Const} \times \text{length} \times (\text{volume density/elastic constant})^{\frac{1}{2}}\).

8. The velocity of sound in a liquid is \(\text{Const} \times (\text{density/modulus of compressibility})^{\frac{1}{2}}\).

9. Given that the twist per unit length of a cylinder varies inversely as the elastic constant, or as the moment of the applied force, prove that it also varies inversely as the fourth power of the diameter.

10. There is a certain critical speed of rotation at which a mass of incompressible gravitating fluid becomes unstable. Prove that the angular velocity at instability is independent of the diameter and proportional to the square root of the density.

11. There is a certain size at which a solid non-rotating gravitating sphere becomes unstable under its own gravitation. Prove that the radius of instability varies directly as the square root of the elastic constant and inversely as the density.

12. Given that the velocity of advance of waves in shallow water is independent of the wave length, show that it varies directly as the square root of the depth.
