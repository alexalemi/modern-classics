Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A simple pendulum hangs from its support, a bob of mass m on a string of length l, swinging through the angle θ under gravity g. Bridgman opens his method with it, asking how the time of swing can depend on these quantities, because dimensional analysis can answer that before any physics is done; the line at the top is his own.
