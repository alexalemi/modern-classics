Chapter 9: The Royal Secret

Krishna said:

Now, because you do not find fault, I will tell you the deepest
secret of all: knowledge, together with insight. Once you know it, you
will be set free from all that is evil. This is the king of all
knowledge, the king of secrets, the supreme purifier. You can see it for
yourself, directly. It is in keeping with what is right, it is very easy
to practice, and it never perishes. People who have no faith in this
teaching, Arjuna, do not reach me. They come back to the road of the
round of birth and death.

This whole world is spread out by me, in my unmanifest form. All
beings rest in me, but I do not rest in them. And yet beings do not
rest in me either: see my divine power, my yoga! My self brings beings
into being and holds them up, but it does not rest in them. Just as
the great wind, moving everywhere, always rests in space, understand
that all beings rest in me in the same way.

At the end of an age of the world, Arjuna, all beings pass into my
nature, and at the beginning of the next age I send them out again.
Taking hold of my own nature, I send out this whole multitude of
beings again and again. They have no power over it; it is nature's
power. And these actions do not bind me, Arjuna, for I sit among
them as if I had no part in them, unattached to all these doings.
With me watching over her, nature gives birth to everything, moving
and unmoving; and that, Arjuna, is why the world goes round.

Fools look down on me when I take on a human body. They do not know
my highest state, as the great Lord of all beings. Their hopes are
empty, their actions empty, their knowledge empty, and their minds are
lost. They have taken on the deluding nature of fiends and demons.
But the great souls, Arjuna, who have taken on the divine nature,
worship me with minds that turn to nothing else, knowing me as the
source of all beings, the one who never changes. Always singing my
praises, striving, firm in their vows, bowing down to me with devotion,
always disciplined, they worship me. And others worship me by
offering the sacrifice of knowledge: as the one, and as the many, and in
countless ways, me whose face is turned in every direction.

I am the ritual, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to the
ancestors, I am the healing herb. I am the sacred chant, I am the melted
butter poured on the fire, I am the fire, and I am the offering itself.
I am the father of this world, its mother, its sustainer, its
grandfather. I am what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om,
and the Rig, Sama and Yajur Vedas. I am the goal, the support, the
lord, the witness, the home, the refuge, the friend; the beginning and
the dissolving, the ground, the treasure house, the seed that never
perishes. I give out heat. I hold back the rain and I send it down.
I am deathlessness, and I am death. I am what is, Arjuna, and what is
not.

Those who know the three Vedas, who drink the soma, the sacred
drink of the sacrifice, and are made clean of sin, worship me with
sacrifices and pray for the way to heaven. Their merit carries them to
the holy world of Indra, king of the gods, and there in heaven they
feast on the divine pleasures of the gods. When they have enjoyed
that wide world of heaven, and their merit is used up, they come back
into the world of mortals. So it is that those who follow the rules laid
down by the three Vedas, wanting what they want, get only coming and
going.

But there are people who think of me and nothing else, and worship
me with all their hearts. They are always disciplined, and for them I
myself carry the burden of getting things and keeping them. Even
those who are devoted to other gods, and sacrifice to them full of
faith, are really sacrificing to me, Arjuna, though not in the way the
rules lay down. For I am the one who enjoys every sacrifice, and I
am its lord. But they do not know me as I truly am, and so they fall
back. Those whose vows are to the gods go to the gods. Those whose
vows are to the ancestors go to the ancestors. Those who sacrifice to
the spirits go to the spirits. And those who sacrifice to me come to me.

If someone offers me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or
water, I accept it, offered with devotion from a heart that strives to
be pure. Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in
sacrifice, whatever you give, whatever austerity you practice, Arjuna,
do it as an offering to me. That way you will be freed from the
chains of action, with their good and bad fruits. With your self
disciplined by the yoga of renunciation, set free, you will come to me.

I am the same to all beings. There is no one I hate, and no one I
favor. But those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I am in
them. Even if someone who has lived a truly wicked life worships me
and turns to nothing else, he should be thought of as good, for he has
made the right decision. Soon he becomes a person of righteous
soul, and he finds lasting peace. Arjuna, you can promise it: no one who
is devoted to me is ever lost.

For those who take refuge in me, Arjuna, even those who may be born
of sinful wombs, women, vaishyas, who farm and herd and trade, and
shudras too, who labor for the other classes, even they reach the
highest goal. How much more, then, holy Brahmins, and devoted royal
seers! Since you have come into this passing and joyless world, worship
me. Keep your mind on me; be devoted to me; sacrifice to me; bow
down to me. Disciplining yourself this way, with me as your highest
goal, you will come to me.
