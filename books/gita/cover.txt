*Krishna and Arjuna in the Chariot*, an album painting by an unknown artist, c. 1830. Blue-skinned Krishna sits on the lead horse with the reins and whip, turned back to speak. Arjuna sits in the gilded chariot behind him with his palms pressed together, and the monkey on his banner is Hanuman, Arjuna's emblem in the epic. It is the moment the whole poem happens in: the charioteer talking and the archer listening.
