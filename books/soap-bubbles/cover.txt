John Everett Millais's *Bubbles* (1886). A curly-haired small boy in a green velvet suit and a lace collar sits on a stone with a bowl of soapy water in his lap and a clay pipe in his hand, looking up at a bubble floating above him in the dark. Pears' soap bought the painting and used it in its advertisements, so the children who came to hear Boys a few years later may well have known it already.
