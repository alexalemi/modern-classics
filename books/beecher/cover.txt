Lilly Martin Spencer's *Kiss Me and You'll Kiss the 'Lasses* (1856). A young woman in a green dress and pink apron turns from her kitchen table to smile at us, a spoon of molasses in one hand, with baskets of cherries and raspberries, a scale, and a tub of apples and pineapples around her. Spencer painted American kitchens and the women in them, which is the room and the work this book sets out to treat as a profession.
