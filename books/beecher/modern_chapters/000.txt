Dedication

[Figure aa: An evening in a family sitting room: an elderly man in a reclining chair holds books while a young couple and a boy show a large album to a small child. A girl hugs a doll.]

[Figure ab: The illustrated title page, with the title and authors lettered between three scenes: a mother in a rocking chair with children and a cat, the cottage by a pond, and a garden.]

*To the women of America, in whose hands rest the real destinies of the Republic, as moulded by the early training and preserved amid the maturer influences of home, this volume is affectionately inscribed.*
