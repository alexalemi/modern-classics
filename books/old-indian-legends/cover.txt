Angel De Cora's frontispiece for this book's first edition (1901), taken from its own illustrations. Under a tree at the edge of open prairie, a man bends low toward a woman and holds out both hands to her, palms down. The caption printed beneath it reads: "This was a sign of gratitude used when words failed to interpret strong emotion." De Cora signed it "A. de C." in the lower corner, and her other plates appear inside.
