Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, from the two series in Malthus's second chapter. The people are drawn as a family tree that doubles at every step, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, and beside each row the food grows by one, 1 to 6. After six steps there are thirty-two mouths for six portions. That gap, which widens forever, is the whole argument of the essay.
