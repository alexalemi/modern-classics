Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. It is Carroll's biliteral diagram, a square cut into four cells for the combinations of two attributes, with his counters on it: red for a cell that is occupied, grey for one that is empty, and a red counter sitting on the line between two cells, which says only that one of them is occupied. The premisses at the top are one of his own puzzles, which the reader is left to solve.
