A portrait of Cesare Borgia attributed to Altobello Melone (1513). He is shown in profile, bearded, in a black cap and cloak over a gold brocade doublet, holding a rolled paper, and the lettering above reads CAES. BORGIA VALENTIN. Machiavelli watched Borgia at work as an envoy, and in Chapter Seven he holds him up as the model of a prince who rose by fortune and other men's arms.
