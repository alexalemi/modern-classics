Edvard Munch's crayon study for his posthumous portrait of Friedrich Nietzsche (1906). Nietzsche stands in a dark coat with his heavy moustache and his face in profile, against rolling blue hills, a small house and a sky in bands of pale colour with a ringed sun. Nietzsche was almost unread when he wrote this book; six years after his death he was famous enough to be drawn by Munch.
