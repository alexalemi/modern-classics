On Chastity

I love the forest. It is bad to live in the cities: there are too many people there who are in heat.

Is it not better to fall into the hands of a murderer than into the dreams of a woman in heat?

And just look at these men: their eye says it — they know nothing better on earth than to lie with a woman.

There is mud at the bottom of their souls; and woe, if their mud has spirit in it as well!

If only you were at least perfect as animals! But innocence belongs to the animal.

Do I advise you to kill your senses? I advise the innocence of the senses.

Do I advise chastity? With some, chastity is a virtue, but with many it is almost a vice.

These people abstain, no doubt: but the bitch Sensuality looks out with envy from everything they do.

Even up into the heights of their virtue and right into the cold spirit, this beast follows them, and its discontent with it.

And how nicely the bitch Sensuality knows how to beg for a piece of spirit when she is refused a piece of meat!

You love tragedies and everything that breaks the heart? But I am suspicious of your bitch.

Your eyes are too cruel for my liking, and you look lustfully at those who suffer. Has your lust not merely put on a disguise and called itself pity?

And I give you this parable too: not a few who wanted to drive out their devil went into the swine themselves.

Whoever finds chastity hard should be advised against it, so that it does not become the road to hell — that is, to the mud and heat of the soul.

Do I speak of dirty things? To me that is not the worst.

It is not when the truth is dirty, but when it is shallow, that the seeker of knowledge is reluctant to step into its water.

Truly, there are people who are chaste to the core: they are gentler of heart, they laugh more gladly and more richly than you.

They laugh at chastity too, and ask: “What is chastity!

Is chastity not folly? But this folly came to us, and not we to it.

We offered this guest a roof and a heart: now he lives with us — let him stay as long as he likes!”

Thus spoke Zarathustra.
