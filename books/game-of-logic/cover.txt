Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. It is Carroll's own board: a square cut into four by a cross, with a smaller square in the middle, so that every compartment stands for a combination of attributes. A red counter means a compartment is occupied, a grey one that it is empty, exactly as the book defines them, and the letters x, y and m label the sides as Carroll does. The line at the top is his.
