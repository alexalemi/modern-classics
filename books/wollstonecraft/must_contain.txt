# Passages that must survive near-verbatim into modern_chapters/.
#
# EVERY ONE OF THESE WAS CHECKED AGAINST THE PREPARED TEXT before being
# written down, not recalled from memory (the epictetus rule). That is
# not a formality here: four pins written from memory in the first pass
# were simply not in the book -- "the divine right of husbands" is there
# but "half beings" and "toys of man" are not, and "strengthen the
# female mind" is really "Strengthen the female mind by enlarging it".
# A pin written from memory can only be satisfied by a translation that
# is wrong.
#
# MOST OF THESE ARE PINNED UNCHANGED, which is the hume/mill/burke rule
# doing its work: Wollstonecraft's famous sentences are already modern,
# and a file in which every sentence has been rewritten has been
# damaged. Where a pin does differ from the source it is recording a
# decision -- modern comma for her semicolon, her comma dropped from
# "gentle, domestic brutes" -- so that the choice cannot drift.

# --- the Dedication to Talleyrand ------------------------------------
I plead for my sex, not for myself
tyrants of every denomination
degrading the master and the abject dependent

# --- the Introduction: her own demand for plain prose, which is this
# --- project's thesis in her words, and the reason she is easy to
# --- retell and hard to improve
I shall be employed about things, not words
alluring mistresses than rational wives
the first object of laudable ambition is to obtain a character as a human being
# the ironic reclamation, and the joke only works if the word stays
grow more and more masculine

# --- Chapter Two ------------------------------------------------------
Strengthen the female mind by enlarging it, and there will be an end to blind obedience
domestic brutes
# Milton, kept VERBATIM as all quoted verse is -- and it is the passage
# the whole chapter exists to answer
God is thy law, thou mine

# --- Chapter Three: the gilt cage ------------------------------------
beauty is woman's sceptre
roaming round its gilt cage, only seeks to adorn its prison
# she italicises the first "divine right" and not the second, so the
# marker is part of the pin -- dropping it would pass must_contain and
# fail emphasis parity, which is how the mill defect was found
*divine right* of husbands, like the divine right of kings
It is time to effect a revolution in female manners

# --- Chapter Four -----------------------------------------------------
the toy of man, his rattle

# --- Chapter Five: the answer to Rousseau. Her semicolon becomes a
# --- modern comma, and the pin is written that way so it cannot drift.
I do not wish them to have power over men, but over themselves

# --- Chapter Twelve ---------------------------------------------------
Make them free, and they will quickly become wise and virtuous
