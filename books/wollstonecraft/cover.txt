Adélaïde Labille-Guiard's *Self-Portrait with Two Pupils* (1785). The painter sits at her easel in blue satin with palette and brushes, looking straight out, while two young women she is training lean over the back of her chair. It is a painting by a woman, from the decade of this book, of the thing the book argues for: women taught a serious art by someone who has mastered it, and earning their living by it.
