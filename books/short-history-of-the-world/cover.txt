Pieter Bruegel the Elder's *The Tower of Babel* (1563). A vast tower of arched storeys rises in a spiral above a harbour, half finished, with scaffolds and cranes on its upper levels, raw red brick where it is still going up and bare rock showing through in the middle, while stonemasons work blocks in the foreground. It is the old story of one people who set out to build together and were scattered into nations, and Wells ends his history by arguing that the nations must somehow become one again.
