Gustave Doré's engraving *The Boat of Souls* (1868), from his illustrations to the *Purgatorio*. An angel stands in the stern of a light boat crowded with souls in white, who have just been brought across the sea to the foot of the mountain. Dante and Virgil watch from the shore among the rushes. It is the second canto, the first morning on the shore, and the souls in the boat are singing a psalm in Latin as they land.
