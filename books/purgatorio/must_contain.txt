# Passages this edition is REQUIRED to keep, checked by verify.py.
# EVERY PIN WAS WRITTEN FROM THE PREPARED TEXT AND THE FINISHED
# TRANSLATION (the epictetus rule), never from memory of an English
# Dante -- almost every Purgatorio phrase an English reader knows is
# somebody's translation rather than Dante.

# The last line of the cantica, and the shape of all three endings.
pure and ready to rise to the stars

# La Pia, canto V: the whole of her life in one line.
Siena made me; Maremma unmade me

# Manfred, canto III, and the claim the canto exists for.
No curse of theirs so loses a man

# Canto VI, the invective that every Italian schoolchild learns.
Ah, Italy the slave, hostel of grief

# Casella's song in canto II is a canzone of Dante's own, and is
# rendered in English for the same reason Arnaut's Provençal is.
Love that speaks to me within my mind

# Canto XXIV: the sweet new style, defined by its inventor.
I am one who, when
Love breathes in me, take note

# Canto XXVI: Arnaut Daniel, in English (see running_notes.txt).
I am Arnaut, who weep and go singing

# Canto XXVII: Virgil's last words in the poem.
so I crown and mitre you over yourself

# Canto XXX: the three Virgils, Dante's one named appearance in the
# whole Comedy, and the line out of the Aeneid he never gets to say.
I know the signs of the old flame
Virgil, sweetest father
Dante, because Virgil is going away
I am indeed, I am indeed Beatrice

# Canto XXXIII: the riddle stays a riddle.
a five hundred, ten and five

# The mountain sings, and the Latin stays Latin.
In exitu Israel de Aegypto
Salve, Regina
Te lucis ante
Beati pauperes spiritu
Summae Deus clementiae
Venite, benedicti Patris mei
Benedictus qui venis
Manibus, oh, date lilia plenis
