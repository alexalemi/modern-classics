Arthur Rackham's illustration of *Little Red-Cap* from his edition of the Grimms' tales (1909). A small girl in a red hood, a basket on her arm, meets a grey wolf at the foot of a wood of tall, twisted, leafless trees. She is the one spot of colour in the picture, and almost the smallest thing in it. A child alone in a big wood, talking to something that should not talk, is the situation of a great many tales in this book.
