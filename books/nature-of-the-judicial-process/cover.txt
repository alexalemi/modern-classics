Ambrogio Lorenzetti's fresco *The Allegory of Good Government* (1339), the part showing Justice. She sits enthroned, looking up at Wisdom, who floats above her with a book and holds the beam of the scales; in one pan an angel punishes and in the other rewards. Below, Concord sits with a carpenter's plane across her knees beside a line of citizens, and Lorenzetti's signature runs along the border. Cardozo's question is what goes on inside the judge in that chair.
