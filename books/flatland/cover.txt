Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. The plane of Flatland is seen edge on, and above it, from a point to a full circle and back to a point, are the sections a Sphere shows as it rises through it: all that the Square can see of his visitor from the third dimension. The Square himself stands below. "Upward, not Northward" is what the Square carries home: the direction no one in Flatland can imagine.
