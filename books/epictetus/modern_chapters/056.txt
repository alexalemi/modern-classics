Chapter 25: That Logic Cannot Be Skipped

When one of the men present said, "Convince me that logic is necessary," he answered:

Do you want me to prove it to you?

"Yes."

Then I shall have to use a demonstrative form of argument.

This was granted.

And how will you know whether I am cheating you with my argument?

The man had nothing to say.

Do you see, said Epictetus, that you are admitting logic is necessary yourself — since without it you cannot even know this much, whether logic is necessary or not?
