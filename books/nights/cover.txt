Sophie Gengembre Anderson's *Scheherazade* (c. 1870). A young woman in a gold cap with a peacock feather, a pink veil and a necklace of gold coins looks straight out of the picture, calm, with a sprig of leaves in her hands. She is the storyteller of the frame, the one person in the book whose life depends on the next sentence, and every tale in this volume is told in her voice.
