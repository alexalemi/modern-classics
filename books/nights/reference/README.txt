Burton's own apparatus, cut out of the source by prep.py and kept here as a
CRIB ONLY -- the bunyan/Offor precedent. It is not translated and does not
appear in the book.

Why it is not part of the volume:

  95,000 words across the four volumes -- getting on for half the length of
  the selection itself. It is Victorian racial and sexual ethnography
  written by Burton in his own person, on his own hobby-horses, and it is
  not the Nights. A reader who wants Burton the anthropologist can have him;
  a reader who wants the tales should not have to walk past him to reach
  them.

Use it the way soap-bubbles/ used its annotation rule: consult a note where
a modern reader genuinely cannot follow the story without it (a term of
Muslim law, a coin, a piece of geography, an untranslated Arabic word left
standing in the text), and take the FACT, never the voice. Do not quote it.
