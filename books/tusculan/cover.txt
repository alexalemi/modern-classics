Richard Westall's *The Sword of Damocles* (1812). Damocles reclines on a gilded throne among wine cups and lyre players and has just looked up to find a sword hanging over his head by a single hair, while the tyrant Dionysius stands behind him with a hand raised. The story is told in Book Five of this book, where Cicero uses it to show what a tyrant's happiness is worth.
