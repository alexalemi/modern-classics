An engraving by Alphonse de Neuville for the Hetzel edition of 1871, shown here in a coloured copy. A man stands on the open deck of the Nautilus in the rain with a sextant to his eye, gulls wheeling over the swell, beside a housing set with two large glass ports. It is Captain Nemo taking the sun's height to fix the ship's position, the plain navigation by which Verne keeps his impossible voyage honest.
