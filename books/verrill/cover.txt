Christian Krohg's *The Net Mender* (1880). An old fisherman in a fur cap sits by a table working a net with a netting needle, a spare length of twine held in his teeth, while a woman in a blue headscarf stands behind him holding the ball it runs from. Verrill's introduction counts the knitting of nets among the things the human race could not have done without knots. The painting shows that skill as an everyday job in a cottage kitchen.
