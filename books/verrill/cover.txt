Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Verrill's Fig. 136, "Turks' heads": braids of line worked around an upright rod, each strand passing over and under the others. Verrill calls the Turk's head so handsome and ornamental that it is a great favorite, used aboard yachts and warships to make rings on stays and collars around stanchions. The line at the top is his.
