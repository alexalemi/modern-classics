Foreword

So, now that I have told you how King Arthur won that most excellent sword, Excalibur, to be his weapon of defense, I will soon tell you about several other noble and knightly adventures by which he won a most beautiful and gentle lady to be his Queen.

For though all the world knows very well the fame of that perfectly gracious lady, the Lady Guinevere, I do not think that the whole story of the adventures by which King Arthur won her favor has ever yet been told.

And since what I am about to tell contains not only the story of that affair, but also the account of a certain enchanted disguise that King Arthur put on for his own purposes, as well as several adventures of very knightly daring that he undertook, I have great hope that whoever reads what I have written will find it both a pleasant and an entertaining history.
