Foreword

Here follows a full account of how Merlin was enchanted by a certain damsel named Vivien, and of everything that had to do with it.

Here, too, you will hear how King Arthur was betrayed by his own sister, and how he would certainly have been killed if it had not been for the help of that same enchantress, Vivien, who brought about Merlin's downfall.

And you will also hear how the sheath of Excalibur was lost at that time.
