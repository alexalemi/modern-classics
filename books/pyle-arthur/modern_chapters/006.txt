Foreword

Here begins the story of certain adventures of Arthur after he had become King. In it you will hear how, with great knightly courage and skill at arms, he fought a very fierce and bloody battle with a certain Sable Knight, a knight all in black. You will also hear how, because of that battle, he won a certain Sword so famous and glorious that its fame will last as long as our language is spoken. For nothing like that sword was ever seen in all the world before that time, and nothing like it has ever been seen since; and its name was Excalibur.

So, if you care to read this story, I believe it will give you excellent entertainment, and it will without doubt lift your spirit high, because of the remarkable courage those two famous and worthy knights showed when they fought that famous battle together. You will also find great delight in reading here about the wonderful marvels of a certain country of Fairyland into which King Arthur wandered. There he found a Lake of Enchantment, and there he talked with a gentle and beautiful lady of that land, who showed him how to win the famous sword I have just told you about.

For it has given me such pleasure to write these things that at times my heart has swelled as if with pure joy, and so I have great hopes that you may find as much pleasure in them as I have already found. So I ask you to listen to what follows.
