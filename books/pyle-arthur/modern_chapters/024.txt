Foreword

Here follows the story of Sir Pelleas, whom many people called the Gentle Knight.

For Sir Pelleas was the kind of man of whom it was said that all women loved him and came to no harm by it, and that all men loved him and were much the better for it.

And so, in the end, when he won for his beloved that beautiful Lady of the Lake, who was one of the very highest damsels of Fairyland, and when he went to live as lord and master in that wonderful dwelling place which no mortal but he and Sir Lancelot of the Lake had ever seen, then all men rejoiced at his great good fortune. And yet the whole Court of King Arthur grieved that he had gone so far away from them, never to return again.

So I believe you will take pleasure in reading the history of Sir Pelleas that is written here for your instruction and delight.
