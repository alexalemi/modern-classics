Foreword

Here follows the story of Sir Gawain, and of how he showed such wonderful faithfulness to King Arthur, who was his lord, that I do not believe anyone had ever seen such faithfulness before.

For truly, though Sir Gawain was at times very rough and harsh in his manner, and though he was always so plain-spoken that his words hid the gentle nature that lay within him, still, under that pride of manner, there was a great deal of courtesy. And at times he was so gracious in his manner and so soft in his speech that many people called him the Knight of the Silver Tongue.

So here you will read how his faithfulness to King Arthur brought him such a high reward that almost anyone in all the world might envy him his great good fortune.
