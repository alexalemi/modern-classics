Howard Pyle's *How Arthur Drew Forth ye Sword* (1903), one of this book's own plates, which appears again in its place inside. The young Arthur, a squire in a hooded cloak with his face turned down, grips the sword set in the anvil on its block of marble, while bearded onlookers watch from behind. Pyle drew every picture in the book in pen and ink, and this is the moment the whole story starts from.
