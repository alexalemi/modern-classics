THE 1927 MATTER: the correspondence lessons the volume revises were
published 1926-28 and are public domain by age (pre-1930).

CONCLUSION: Volume II (1942) is in the US public domain for want of
renewal. Not public domain: Vol. 1 in its 1944 revised text, Vols. 3-5.
The 1941 first printing of Vol. 1 (A160117) was not renewed either.
