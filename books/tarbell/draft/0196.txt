MeEtHop 4 (Second Dealing):

This is a method that Joe Berg uses and is for those who like a novelty style of dealing with a snap of the card as cards are dealt. This method isa modification of the second deal used by Cardini.

Hold deck, backs ups, in left hand, asin Fig. 1. First finger at upper narrow edge of deck, other fingers curled around right edge and thumb on “top” card.

Bring the thumb back to the left edge of deck, Fig. 2, and push to een the two “top” cards, Fig. 3. Deck will fan out slightly but this is all right.

Bring the right hand up over the “top” cards as in Fig. 4, the left thumb sliding “top” card back, leaving the second card held by the second
