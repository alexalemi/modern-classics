Hold left arm outstretched. Follow the ~ imaginary coin with your eyes—look upward as though watching it go up and then down to the hat. Pretend to catch it in hat, and at the right instant drop a sixth coin from left hand into hat. Make this as real as possible. Remember that pantomime is very important in illusion.

Reach out for another coin and pretend to drop it into hat, allowing a’ - seventh coin to fall from left hand. Then repeat again and let an eighth coin fall from left hand.

You now have one coin remaining in your left hand and one coin palmed in your right hand.
