FRANK KELLY VANISHING WAND ROUTINE

This is an interesting routine for the vanish and appearance of a magician’s wand. EFFECT:

Magician wraps his wand in a sheet of paper and gives to spectator to hold. Upon tearing the paper, wand is found to have vanished, and is finally discovered hanging on the spectator’s back.

PARAPHERNALIA:

1—A Petrie-Lewis Vanishing Wand, consisting of paper shell and two wooden plugs.

2—A regular wand to match.
