AN ODD CIGARETTE VANISH

EFFECT:

Performer shows a cardboard box with a number of cigarettes in it. He takes them out one at a time and places them in another cardboard box. . Picking up the second box, he causes the cigarettes to vanish. Magician shows the empty box and tears it up to prove that the cigarettes are gone.

PARAPHERNALIA: 1—Two cardboard boxes, about 16 inches square.

2—A few cigarettes. 3—A Cigarette Clip.
