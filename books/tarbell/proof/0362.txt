## Chefalo’s Knot

I believe this knot was brought out by Chefalo, the famous Italian magician. It works very nicely in an Oriental program.

## Effect

Performer ties a double knot. Then to make knot even more secure, he loops an end of the rope through the knot several times. In spite of this, the knot disappears on command.

## To Perform

Tie a single knot in center of rope. Note just how knot is made with part 1 of the rope coming up behind part 2, Fig. 48. Tie another single knot. This time part 1 comes in front of part 2, Fig. 49.

Bring right end of rope down through loop A, Fig. 50. Now bring same end of rope up through loop B, Fig. 51.

Pull on both ends of rope and knots disappear.

## Tarbell’s Triple Chefalo Knot

This variation of the Chefalo knot has been popular in my magical programs for years. It is done as a Chinese effect. It looks almost impossible.

## Effect

A system of single and interlaced knots are tied in a piece of rope. Ends of rope are given to a spectator to hold. Without releasing ends each of the knots is quickly removed by performer, starting with lower end.

## Paraphernalia

A piece of soft rope five or six feet long.
