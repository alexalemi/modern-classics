## Method 2

The effect is similar to that produced by Method 1, but the mode of operation is different. Hand is in position as in Fig. 13, with cards on back. Curve the fingers in and place thumb on outer card. Push down the card with thumb until it is free of first finger, Fig. 24.

[FIG 24]

Bring index finger back of outer card, Fig. 25.

[FIG 25]

Push index finger between outer card and remaining four cards. Grip outer card between thumb and back of first joint of index finger, Fig. 26.

[FIG 26]

Release lower corner of outer card from little finger, Fig. 27.

[FIG 27]

Straighten fingers. The four cards are concealed again on back of hand and the produced card is shown between thumb and forefinger of right hand, Fig. 28.

[FIG 28]

## Production of Cards One at a Time from Front of Hand

In this production the back of hand is shown to audience. Palm several cards, say five, in regular fashion. Corner AC is gripped right in the ball of the forefinger, Fig. 29.
