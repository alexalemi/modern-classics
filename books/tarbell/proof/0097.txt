## To Perform

Open paper and freely show both sides. Hold paper in left hand as in Fig. 6. The slot side is away from audience. Show half dollar in right hand and bring it to center of paper, really slipping it into the pocket, Fig. 6. Coin being behind paper, audience cannot see this move. When coin is hidden in pocket, fold left side over, and then the right side and other sides as in Fig. 5.

The coin has apparently vanished when you open paper again in position of Fig. 2. Opening of pocket is upright. Freely show both sides of the paper.
