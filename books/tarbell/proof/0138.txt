# leaf 0138

## Front and Back Hand Palm

## Effect

Performer shows both sides of right hand empty. Then suddenly he produces a cigarette at his finger tips.

[FIG 16]

[FIG 17]

## To Perform

This may be done as a continuation of various cigarette vanishes. If you do it following the Pivot Vanish, allow cigarette to pivot into position shown in Fig. 16. The end of it goes right into crotch of thumb.

[FIG 18]

Cigarette can then be easily grasped in crotch of right thumb, Fig. 17. Drop right hand to side. Keep back of hand to audience, Fig. 18. Then raise right hand, still keeping back of it to audience. Fig. 19 shows hand as audience sees it.

[FIG 19]

[FIG 20]

Fig. 20 shows palm of hand away from audience with concealed cigarette. Now you are to turn hand over quickly to show palm empty to audience. As you turn hand, force end of cigarette between first and second fingers, Fig. 21.
