## To Produce Card

Have card on back of hand and palm towards audience. Curl fingers inward as in Fig. 3. Grasp card between tip of second finger and thumb.

Release bottom corner of card from little finger and allow card to spring up. Place first finger at rear of card also and produce as shown in Fig. 18.
