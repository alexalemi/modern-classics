## To Perform

The Ace of Diamonds, or other card, has been placed in the special card case in breast pocket, unknown to the audience. The card faces outward.

You pick up the deck from the table and have a spectator select a card, remember it, and return it to the center of the deck. Bring the card to the “top” of deck by the pass.

Holding the deck in the left hand, backs towards audience, bring up your hand to the deck and make a rather crude movement of palming a card off the deck. Apparently put the card in your right hand trousers pocket. This will get comments or recognition from the audience.

“*I heard you. You said I put a card in my pocket. I guess you caught me that time for I did put the gentleman’s card in my pocket.*”
