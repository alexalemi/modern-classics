## To Show Both Hands Empty by Showing Both Sides of Each

Bring right hand above left, still keeping back of it to audience. Fig. 7 shows view away from audience. Lower right hand, allowing ball to drop down behind left hand. Fig. 8 shows view away from audience.

Bring right hand down to position shown in Fig. 8 and separate hands as in Fig. 7. Now turn left hand so that backs of both hands are exposed. You have now shown backs and fronts of both hands.
