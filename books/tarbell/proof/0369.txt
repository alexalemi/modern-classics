## Variation of Looping

Rope can also be held as in Fig. 12. The right end is held in right hand. Upper part of loop is cut between the hands.

Another method of looping which is very illusive is shown in Fig. 13. The left thumb covers the secret loop. The visible loop is formed by just bringing rope at back of regular loop in front of hand on palm and holding with thumb against fingers. Cut the loop at top, and let the right side of rope fall down as in Figs. 6 and 7.
