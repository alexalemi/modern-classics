[FIG 43]

When this piece is held by one corner it hangs down as a whole silk. Also when it is tied between two other silks, it looks like a whole silk. The advantage of using the half silk is that it can be tucked into a small space. This principle is valuable when you desire to produce a number of silks as the half silks show up as whole ones and you can use twice the number in the same amount of space.
