A poster for the American magician Harry Kellar's levitation illusion, printed in 1894 by the Strobridge Lithographing Company. Kellar, bald and in evening dress, passes his hands over a young woman in a red skirt who floats beside him, apparently asleep, with painted sparks coming off his fingers. Posters like this sold the big stage illusions, and the last lesson in this volume teaches how such effects are built, levitations among them.
