Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. Couples of every kind, two circles, two squares, two stars, two triangles, are gathered inside a single loop: Russell's definition of number, in which the number 2 is nothing more mysterious than the bundle of all couples, and two collections have the same number when their members can be paired off one to one. The line at the top is from his second chapter.
