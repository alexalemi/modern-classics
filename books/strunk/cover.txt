Gabriël Metsu's *Man Writing a Letter* (1665). A young man in black sits at a table spread with a red patterned carpet, his head bent over the page and his pen moving, with a globe by the window behind him, a landscape painting on the wall and his hat hung on the back of the chair. It is a picture of a person taking care over a piece of writing, which is the whole subject of Strunk's little book.
