Bruno Liljefors' *Wild Geese* (1898). A flock is coming down to a shore at sunset: four birds glide in over the water with their feet already lowered, and the geese on the ground stretch their necks up to call to them, all dark against a pink sky reflected in the shallows. Liljefors spent his life painting animals in the wild, and his geese are a flock arriving together, which is the kind of thing Kropotkin's first chapters, on animals, are made of.
