*The Conjurer*, painted in the early sixteenth century by a follower of Hieronymus Bosch. A performer in a red gown and tall black hat holds up a small ball between finger and thumb for two onlookers. On his table are two cups, more balls, a cone, a wand and a frog; a basket hangs from his belt, an owl peering out of it, and a little dog in a costume sits under the table. It is the cups and balls, a trick already old then, and one Hoffmann teaches in full.
