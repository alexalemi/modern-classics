Arnold Böcklin's *Odysseus and Calypso* (1883). The full painting has the goddess in red at the mouth of her cave; this cover keeps only Odysseus, standing on a heap of dark rock above a beach, wrapped to the chin in a blue cloak and turned away from everything towards a grey sky over the sea. It is how the poem first shows him, in book five, after seven years on her island and still trying to get home.
