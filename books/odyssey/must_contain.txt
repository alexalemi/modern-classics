# Passages the Odyssey is not allowed to lose, written FROM the finished
# translation and never from memory (the epictetus rule).  Almost every
# Homeric phrase an English reader knows belongs to some TRANSLATOR --
# Chapman, Pope, Butler, Fitzgerald, Lattimore -- rather than to Homer, so
# a pin taken from memory could only be satisfied by a rendering that had
# drifted to somebody else's wording.

# I.1 -- the first line, and the word the whole poem hangs on. POLYTROPOS
# is "of many turns": turned about by the world, and turning it.
Tell me, Muse, of the man of many turns

# I.3 -- the second half of the proem, which says the poem is about a mind
# and not about a voyage.
He saw the cities of many peoples and came to know their minds

# IX.366 -- the pun the Cyclops episode turns on. It has to work in
# English when the other Cyclopes shout back that NOBODY is killing him,
# and go away; Murray's "Noman" does not.
Nobody is my name.

# IX.408 -- and the joke landing.
it is Nobody who is killing me by guile

# XI.489 -- the most quoted lines in the poem, and the flat contradiction
# of everything the Iliad is about. Pinned in the plain form.
I would rather be above ground still and labouring for some poor man

# XVII.291 -- Argos. Pinned on the sentence that must NOT be softened: he
# lies in the dung, and it is stated without comment.
There the dog Argos lay, full of vermin.

# XVII.326 -- and the death, which gets one clause and no more.
when he had seen Odysseus in the twentieth year

# XIX.163 -- Penelope refusing to be put off about his parentage.
you were not born from an oak of the old story, or from a stone

# XIX.562 -- the gates of horn and ivory.
one is made of horn and one of ivory

# XXII.473 -- the hanging of the twelve women. Pinned because it is the
# hardest passage in the poem and the one most often quietly softened:
# the simile is domestic, the sentence is short, and Homer does not
# comment. Neither does this edition.
And they writhed a little while with their feet, but not long.

# XXIII.183 -- the bed, and the recognition. Pinned in the form that keeps
# the anger in it: he does not ask, he accuses.
Who has moved my bed somewhere else?

# XXIII.190 -- the token itself, which is the marriage.
A bush of long-leafed olive was growing inside the court
