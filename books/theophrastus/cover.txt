A Roman floor mosaic of two theatre masks, made by an unknown artist in the second century AD. On the left is a tragic mask with long ringlets, a ribbon and a pair of reed pipes; on the right a comic mask, wreathed in ivy, with a scowl and a huge open mouth. Theophrastus' sketches are close cousins of the stock characters of the comic stage, and Menander, who wrote the best of those comedies, was said in antiquity to have been his pupil.
