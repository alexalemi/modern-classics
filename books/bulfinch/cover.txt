John William Waterhouse's *Pandora* (1896). She kneels in a blue dress beside a stream in a dark wood, lifting the lid of a gold casket set on a rock, and a wisp of smoke is already curling out. Bulfinch tells her story in his second chapter, in two versions: in one the box held all the troubles of the world, in the other all its blessings, and in both only hope stayed behind.
