Rembrandt's *Aristotle with a Bust of Homer* (1653). The philosopher, in a broad black hat and wide white sleeves, rests one hand on a marble head of the poet and looks past it, lost in thought. A heavy gold chain crosses his chest, hung with a medallion usually taken to be a portrait of Alexander, his pupil. Homer is quoted again and again in the *Rhetoric*, his lines held up as examples of how to move an audience.
