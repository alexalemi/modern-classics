The *Plato's Academy* mosaic from Pompeii, the work of an unknown Roman artist in the 1st century. Seven men, bearded and wrapped in cloaks, sit and stand together in a grove, with a tree, a row of columns and buildings on a hill behind them, and a globe sits in a box on the ground among them. Nobody can say for certain who they are. It is philosophy as Xenophon remembered it: men talking in the open air.
