Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after the first diagram in the book: Example 1, the ending Rook and King against King. White has King and Rook on the first rank, and Black's King stands alone in the far corner. The arrow is Capablanca's first move, the Rook to the seventh rank, which shuts the Black King in on the last line; the line at the top is the principle he draws from it.
