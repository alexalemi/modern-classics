Chapter 9: How Sir Lancelot made La Cote Male Taile lord of the Castle of Pendragon, and how he was afterwards made a knight of the Round Table

And in the meanwhile Sir Kay the Seneschal and Sir Brandiles came, and at once they joined their company. And then, within ten days, those knights of Arthur's court left these fortresses. And as Sir Lancelot came by the Castle of Pendragon, he put Sir Brian de les Isles out of his lands, because he would never take service with King Arthur; and all that Castle of Pendragon and all its lands he gave to Sir La Cote Male Taile. And then Sir Lancelot sent for Nerovens, whom he had once made a knight, and he gave him the whole rule of that castle and of that country, under La Cote Male Taile. And so they all rode together to Arthur's court. And at the next Pentecost, Sir Plenorius and Sir La Cote Male Taile, whose right name was Sir Bruin le Noire, were both made knights of the Round Table; and King Arthur gave them great lands. And there Bruin le Noire married that damsel Maledisant. And afterwards she was called Beauvivante, the fair-living, but ever after, for the most part, he was called La Cote Male Taile. And he proved an exceedingly noble knight, and a mighty one, and he did many honourable deeds afterwards in his life. And Sir Plenorius proved a noble knight and full of prowess, and all the days of their lives, for the most part, they waited on Sir Lancelot; and Sir Plenorius' brothers were always knights of King Arthur. And also, as the French book mentions, Sir La Cote Male Taile avenged his father's death.
