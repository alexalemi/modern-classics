Chapter 10: How Sir Lancelot came to the court with Sir Percival and Sir Ector, and of the great joy over him

Then they left, and within five days' journey they came to Camelot, which in English is Winchester. And when Sir Lancelot had come among them, the king and all the knights rejoiced greatly over him. And there Sir Percival de Galis and Sir Ector de Maris began and told all the adventures: that Sir Lancelot had been out of his mind the whole time of his absence, and how he called himself Le Chevaler Mal Fet, the knight who had done wrong; and how in three days Sir Lancelot struck down five hundred knights. And all the while Sir Ector and Sir Percival told these tales of Sir Lancelot, Queen Guinevere wept as if she would die. Then the queen gave him a joyful welcome.

"O Jesus," said King Arthur, "I wonder what made you, Sir Lancelot, go out of your mind. I and many others think it was for the love of fair Elaine, the daughter of King Pelles, by whom it is rumoured you have had a child, and his name is Galahad, and men say he will do marvels."

"My lord," said Sir Lancelot, "if I did anything foolish, I got what I went looking for."

And with that the king said no more. But all Sir Lancelot's kin knew for whom he had gone out of his mind. And then great feasts were held and there was great joy; and many great lords and ladies, when they heard that Sir Lancelot had come back to the court, rejoiced greatly.
