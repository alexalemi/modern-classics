Chapter 13: Of a dream of the King with the Hundred Knights

So on Merlin's advice scouts were sent out ahead to scour the country, and they met the scouts of the north, and made them tell which way the army was coming, and then they told it to Arthur. And on the advice of King Ban and King Bors they had all the country burned and laid waste ahead of them, wherever the enemy would ride.

The King with the Hundred Knights dreamed a marvellous dream two nights before the battle: that a great wind blew, and blew down their castles and their towns, and after that came a flood and carried it all away. All who heard of the dream said it was a sign of a great battle. Then on Merlin's advice, when they knew which way the eleven kings would ride and where they would camp that night, they attacked them at midnight, as they were in their tents. But the sentries of their army cried out, "Lords! To arms! For here are your enemies upon you!"
