Chapter 5: How at the feast of King Arthur's wedding to Guinevere a white hart came into the hall with thirty couple of hounds, and how a little hound bit the hart and was carried off

Then the high feast was made ready, and the king was married to Dame Guinevere at Camelot, in the church of Saint Stephen's, with great solemnity. And when every man was seated according to his rank, Merlin went to all the knights of the Round Table and told them to sit still, and that none of them should leave his place. "For you shall see a strange and a marvellous adventure."

Just as they sat there, a white hart came running into the hall, and a little white hound right behind him, and thirty couple of black running hounds came after with a great baying. And the hart went round the Round Table, and past the other tables. The little white hound bit him in the haunch and pulled out a piece, and at that the hart leapt a great leap and knocked over a knight who sat at the side of a table. And with that the knight got up and picked up the hound, and so went out of the hall, and took his horse and rode away with the hound.

At once a lady came in on a white palfrey, a lady's riding horse, and cried aloud to King Arthur, "Sir, do not let me suffer this insult, for the hound that the knight led away was mine."

"I can do nothing about it," said the king.

With this a knight came riding in, fully armed, on a great horse, and took the lady away with him by force, and all the while she cried out and made great lament. When she had gone the king was glad, for she made such a noise.

"No," said Merlin, "you cannot let these adventures go so lightly; for these adventures must be brought back, or else it would be a dishonour to you and to your feast."

"I wish," said the king, "that everything be done by your advice."

"Then," said Merlin, "have Sir Gawain called, for he must bring back the white hart. Also, sir, you must have Sir Tor called, for he must bring back the hound and the knight, or else kill him. Also have King Pellinore called, for he must bring back the lady and the knight, or else kill him. And these three knights will do marvellous deeds before they come back."

Then all three were called, as was told before, and each of them took on his charge, and armed himself securely. But Sir Gawain had the first request, and so we will begin with him.
