Chapter 12: How Galahad and Percival found in a castle many tombs of maidens who had bled to death

Now the story says that all night Galahad and Percival were in a chapel at their prayers, praying for Sir Bors to be kept safe. Then in the morning they put on their armour and went towards the castle, to find out what had happened to the people in it. And when they came there they found that there was neither man nor woman who was not dead, by the vengeance of Our Lord.

With that they heard a voice that said, "This vengeance is for the shedding of maidens' blood."

They also found, at the end of the chapel, a churchyard, and in it they could see some sixty fair tombs. And that place was so fair and so delightful that it seemed to them there had been no storm there; for there lay the bodies of all the good maidens who had been martyred for the sick lady's sake. They also found the names of each of them, and what blood they came from, and all were of kings' blood, and twelve of them were kings' daughters. Then they left and went into a forest.

"Now," said Percival to Galahad, "we must part. So let us pray Our Lord that we may meet again soon."

Then they took off their helmets and kissed each other, and wept at their parting.
