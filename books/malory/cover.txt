N. C. Wyeth's illustration *And When They Came to the Sword That the Hand Held, King Arthur Took It Up*, from *The Boy's King Arthur* (1917). Arthur stands in armour and a blue cloak in the bow of a gilded boat on a still lake, a hooded figure beside him, and an arm rises out of the water holding up a sword, while swans fly low through the mist. It is Excalibur and the Lady of the Lake, from early in Malory's first Book.
