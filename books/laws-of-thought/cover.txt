Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. It is Boole's "development" from Chapter V: the universe, 1, cut by a class x into x and not-x, each part cut again by y and again by z, until eight constituents stand in the bottom row. A premise says which of them are empty, and those are struck out; reading off what survives is how every problem in the book is solved, and it is the ancestor of the truth table.
