Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after the wheel Fortune speaks of in Book Two, in the words Philosophy gives her. She turns it for the pleasure of watching the high come down and the low go up, and anyone may climb on, so long as he does not call it an outrage when the rules of her game bring him down again. The line at the top is hers.
