*Philosophy Instructing Boethius on the Role of God*, a manuscript illumination by the Coëtivy Master (c. 1460–1470). Boethius, in a long rose-coloured gown, holds his red hat in his hands and looks up at Philosophy, a tall lady in a blue gown and a high white headdress, with a walled town behind them. The painter dressed both of them in the fashions of his own day. Nearly a thousand years after Boethius wrote, his book was still being copied and pictured as a scene from the reader’s own world.
