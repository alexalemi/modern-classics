Thomas Eakins's *The Thinker: Portrait of Louis N. Kenton* (1900). A man in a black suit and spectacles stands against a bare background with his hands in his pockets and his eyes down, lost in thought. There is nothing else in the picture. Eakins called it *The Thinker* but could paint only the outside; this book, from the same decade and the same country, is an attempt to describe what goes on inside.
