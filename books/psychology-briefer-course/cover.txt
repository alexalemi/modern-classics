Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after James's own figure in his chapter on the stream of consciousness. Several lines of thought start together from A and wander by very different paths, straight, wavy, looping and jagged, until all of them arrive at the same conclusion, Z. His point is that what matters in thinking is where it stops, not the stuff it travels through, and the line at the top says so.
