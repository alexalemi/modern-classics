Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s. A length of rope is tied in the simplest of true knots, crossing over and under itself three times. Carroll called each of his ten puzzle stories a Knot, and each one hid a problem in arithmetic or algebra inside a comic tale for his readers to untie; the line at the top is from his preface, where he says so.
