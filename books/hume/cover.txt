Drawn for this edition, in the manner of the Dover science paperbacks of the 1950s and 60s, after Hume's billiard balls in Section IV. One ball rolls in and strikes a second, and the second moves off to where the outline shows it will be. Hume's point is that this is all anyone ever sees: one event, then another, and never the connection between them that we call cause. The line at the top is his.
