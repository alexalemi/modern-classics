Joseph-Siffred Duplessis's portrait *Benjamin Franklin* (1785). Franklin is seventy-nine, in a plain grey coat and waistcoat with a white stock, his long thin hair loose to the shoulders, and he faces the viewer with a slight, closed smile. Duplessis painted him in France, where he was the American minister. He had written Father Abraham's speech nearly thirty years before, and he is dressed as plainly as the speech recommends.
