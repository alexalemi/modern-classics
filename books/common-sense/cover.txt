John Trumbull's *Declaration of Independence* (1819). The committee that drafted the Declaration stands at the table in the Pennsylvania State House: John Adams with his hand on his hip, Roger Sherman and Robert Livingston behind him, Thomas Jefferson laying down the draft, and Benjamin Franklin at his side, with the delegates seated around them. Common Sense came out that January and argued, before anyone else would say it in print, for exactly the step they took that July.
