Eugène Delacroix's *Last Words of the Emperor Marcus Aurelius* (1844). The emperor lies dying on a couch, bearded and bare-chested, and reaches out to the young man in red standing at the bedside, his son Commodus, who looks away. Older men in philosophers' cloaks stand around the bed, one sits on the floor with his head bowed, and a scroll lies on the step. It is the end that the notebook is always preparing for.
