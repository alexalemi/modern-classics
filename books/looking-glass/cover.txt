John Tenniel's frontispiece for this book (1871), engraved on wood. The White Knight sits his horse in a dark wood, mild and drooping, his saddle hung with gear and the horse's ankles ringed with spikes, while Alice walks alongside and looks up at him. He is the knight who rescues her from the Red Knight, sings her a song and sees her to the last brook before she becomes a queen, and Carroll and Tenniel put him at the front of the book.
