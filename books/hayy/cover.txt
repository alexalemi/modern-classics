Aqa Mirak's *Majnun with the Animals in the Desert* (1539–1543), a page from the *Khamsa* made for Shah Tahmasp of Iran. Majnun, the lover driven out of his mind by Layla, kneels bare-chested among rocks painted lilac and white, stroking a gazelle, while lions, deer, foxes, ibex and a leopard keep him company and cranes cross the gold sky. A young man at home in the wild, with animals for his only family, is where Hayy's story begins too.
