A hand-coloured frame from Georges Méliès' film *A Trip to the Moon* (1902): the man in the moon, with a capsule stuck in his right eye. Méliès was not working from Lucian. His sources were the novels of Verne and Wells. But he got to the same place by the same road, a straight face, a moon with a face of its own, and no pretence that any of it was true.
