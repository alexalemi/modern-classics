Arthur Rackham's *The Gnat and the Lion*, one of his colour plates for this 1912 edition. The Gnat, with a long nose and a startled face, hangs tangled in a great web while the Spider, scowling, climbs down to it; below are bare treetops and a grey sky. The Gnat has just beaten the Lion and flown off boasting. Rackham drew the end of the fable, not the triumph.
