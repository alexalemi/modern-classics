BURKE — RUNNING CONSISTENCY LEDGER
==================================
Read text_analysis.txt first.

FILE / PART DISCIPLINE
----------------------
Thirteen sections; 13 of the 22 files are section parts. Check the
manifest for "of" > 1 BEFORE writing any file. The shape is exactly:

    Section Ten: The Church Establishment, and the Seizure of Its Property
    (Part 2 of 4)

    <body>

Parts 2 and later NEVER re-introduce the section; non-final parts NEVER
conclude it. assemble.strip_front drops the first non-blank line of
EVERY file in a group and takes the heading from part 1 only, so a
wrong heading in a later part vanishes silently and a missing part
marker is invisible to verify.py.

The thirteen headings are fixed in prep.py's TITLES and must be
reproduced EXACTLY. Cross-references in the text use the same word form
("as I said at the beginning of this letter"), never a file number.

MARKUP
------
  - _x_ and *x* render as <em>; everything else is convention.
  - Gutenberg's _italics_ are already correct and mostly need only to be
    carried across onto the equivalent English word.
  - A tab indent is a quotation block. Six of them: the Latin verse
    tags and the long French quotations near the end. Keep them
    indented and keep their line breaks.
  - NEVER write an all-caps line: assemble.py reads one as a heading.
  - The opening delimiter may not follow a word character, so write
    "can *not*", never "can*not*".

SENSITIVE PASSAGES — DECIDED IN ADVANCE
---------------------------------------
THE VERNE RULE, IN FULL, AND NO EDITOR'S NOTE ANYWHERE IN THE VOLUME.
Kept entire and unsoftened:
  - "a swinish multitude". The most notorious phrase he ever wrote,
    thrown back at him for a century -- radicals published journals
    called Pig's Meat in reply -- and never retracted. Softening it
    would delete the reception history of the book.
  - The contempt for the National Assembly as country attorneys and
    obscure practitioners raised above their station.
  - The Marie Antoinette passage entire, ten thousand swords and all,
    which even sympathetic contemporaries thought absurd.
  - His partisan and in places inaccurate account of 5-6 October, as
    HIS account.
  - The defence of prejudice as a positive good, of prescription, of
    hereditary rank and of an established church.
  - His hostility to Dissenters, to "political men of letters", and the
    references to Jewish financiers in the assignat passages. This is
    the ugliest thing in the book, it is his, and it stands.
A note saying that some of this has aged badly would be the wink the
Verne rule forbids. A reader in 2026 does not need the help.

SOURCE MISPRINTS CORRECTED, EACH LOGGED HERE
--------------------------------------------
A source oddity is either KEPT or corrected WITH A REASON WRITTEN DOWN;
it is never quietly fixed while translating. Two so far:
  - Section Two prints Horace as "Condo et compono quae mox depromere
    PASSIM". The line is "possim", and I corrected it in draft without
    saying so, which is the defect and not the fix. RESTORED to the
    source reading. Burke's printer is not ours to correct in passing.
  - Section Three prints "he charged him with having broken the
    _original CONTRAST_ between king and people". Two paragraphs above,
    the footnote quotes the same statute with "_original contract_",
    which is also what the Declaration of Right says. This is a
    transmission error and not a claim, so the Verne rule does not
    protect it, and it is CORRECTED to "contract" -- the hume precedent,
    where "necessary and evitable" for "inevitable" reversed the very
    argument it concluded.

RATIO
-----
Target 0.90-1.05. EN->EN modernisation runs about 1:1: unstacking a
200-word period costs roughly what cutting the throat-clearing saves.
Verify with --min-ratio 0.85 --max-ratio 1.15.
