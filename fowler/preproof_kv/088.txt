chorale. Pronounce korah’l. As to spelling, the -e is strictly incorrect, but both usual & convenient, obviating confusion with the adj. choral; cf. LOCALE & MORALE, & see A L OUTRANCE.

c(hjord. There are two words chord, one of which, that used in Harmony, has no connexion with cord; the other (touch the right chord; the chord of an arc; the vocal chords; the spinal chord) is the same as cord, but has had its spelling corrected after the Greek original. It is well to remember that in the four phrases mentioned chord means simply string; but the spelling cord, which would have been legitimate & avoided confusion in any of them, is ruled out by custom except in the last & possibly the last but one.
