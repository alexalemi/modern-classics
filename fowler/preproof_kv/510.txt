residue, -uum, -ual, -uary. There are two. special uses, to each of which one noun & one adjective are appropriated—the legal sense concerned with what remains of an estate after payment of charges, debts, & bequests; & the mathematical, chemical, & physical sense of what remains after subtraction, combustion, evaporation, &e. The legal noun & adjective are residue & residuary, the chemical &c. are residuum & residual, though the differentiation is occasionally infringed in both directions.
