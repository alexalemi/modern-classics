trecento, ~tlSt. Pronounce traché’nt6, -tist. This & quattrocento, -ist, cinquecento, -ist, are words constantly used by writers on Italian art. Though their true meaning is 300, 400, 500, they are used as abbreviations for the centuries 1300- 1399 (1301-1400 is with us the 14th c.), 1400-1499 (our 15th c.), & 1500- 1599 (our 16th). There is therefore a double puzzle, Italian 300 for Italian 1800, & Italian 13th c. for English 14th c. The words in -ist mean painters &c. of the century.
