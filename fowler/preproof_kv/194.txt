4. Fixation. Most literary men know some Latin; that Latin is chiefly of the classical kind, & a little of it is enough to make them aware that figere, & not fixare, is the classical Latin for fix. Consequently they feel an instinctive repugnance to the word fixation, &, perhaps unreasonably, prefer to say fixing instead of it whenever they can; it is mostly left to those who need it in technical contexts. If a comparison could be made between fixing & fixation on the one hand, & (say) liberating & liberation on the other, it would be found that avoidance of fixation was far the more frequent.
