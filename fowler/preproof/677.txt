-TRIX. As any Latin agent noun in -tor could form a feminine in -trtz, some of these when taken into English continue to do so, especially such as are, like testator & prosecutor, in legal use. It is a serious inconvenience that the Latin plural is -tces (-I’séz); if the Latin quantity is preserved, the accent has to be shifted in the plural, which makes the word hardly recognizable. The result is that it is somctimes given up as a bad job; OED gives c.g. prosecu trices & ra‘dices (radix is like the -trix words, with Latin pl. radt'ces), & allows matriz a popular ma‘trices by the side of a correct matri’ces; but for cicainea & mediatrix it allows only -tri’ces, & for ditrectriz, executriz, heritriz, states only that the pl. is -ices & leaves us to deal with quantity & accent as we please.
