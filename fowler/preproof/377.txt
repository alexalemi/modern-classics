-most. The commoner words with this ending are best pronounced -ost, not -dst, though mistaken effort is often expended on satisfying the supposed demands of the spelling. The origin was not the word most, but a double superlative suflix -mest, & it is not unlikely that the ordinary colloquial pronunciation records this fact. Foremost, innermost, uppermost, topmost, inmost, utmost, hindermost, are examples of the words in which the obscure o is better than the clear 6.
