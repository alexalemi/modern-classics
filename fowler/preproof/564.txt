1. Meiosis. ‘ This is some. war’, with strong emphasis on some, is modern colloquial for ° This is a vast war’, * This is indeed a war, if ever there was one’. It is still felt as slang, & it comes to us from America; but it results from that love of meiosis (see TECHNICAL TERMS) which is shared with the Americans by us. We say a place is some distance off, meaning a long way; we say ‘ It necds some faith to believe that ’, meaning a hardly possible credulity. So far the effect is exactly parallel to the emphatic use of rather in answer to a question —' Do you like it?’ ‘ Rather!’, meaning not somewhat, but exceedingly.
