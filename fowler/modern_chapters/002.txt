Key to Pronunciation

**VOWELS**

	ā ē ī ō ū o͞o (*mate, mete, mite, mote, mute, moot*)
	ă ĕ ĭ ŏ ŭ o͝o (*rack, reck, rick, rock, ruck, rook*)
	ā̆ ē̆ ī̆ ō̆ ū̆ o͞͝o = ā or ă, o͞o or o͝o, &c.
	*a e i o u ar er or* These italic letters stand for light vague sounds (stigm*a*, sil*e*nt, cous*i*n, c*o*ntain, s*u*bmit, begg*ar*, p*er*tain, mot*or*).
	a͞r e͞r i͞r o͞r u͞r (*mare, mere, mire, more, mure*)
	ar er or (*part, pert, port*)
	ah aw oi oor ow owr (*bah, bawl, boil, boor, brow, bower*)

**CONSONANTS**

**of which the value needs defining**

	ch (*child, each*: not as in *chaos, champagne, loch*)
	dh (dhăt, mŭ'dher, = *that, mother*)
	g (*gag, get*: not as in *gentle*)
	j (jŭj = *judge*)
	ng (*singer*: not as in *finger, ginger*)
	ngg (fĭ'ngger = *finger*)
	s (saws = *sauce*: not as in *laws*)
	th (*thinketh*: not as in *this, smooth*)
	zh (ro͝ozh, vĭ'zhn, = *rouge, vision*)
	For h, r, w, in ah, ar &c., ow, owr, see Vowels
