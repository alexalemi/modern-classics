FIGURE CAPTIONS — house style
  One sentence, descriptive, no terminal flourish. Say what the plate shows
  and, if it is not obvious, what it demonstrates. Present tense.
  Photographs are identified as photographs; diagrams are not labelled as
  diagrams. Multi-panel plates say so ("left, centre and right").
  [Figure 39b] stays caption-less — it is a scale bar, not a figure.
