Restored Editions

The author’s own words, properly presented

Not every old book needs retelling. Some are already clear and simply deserve a better edition than they have. In these volumes the prose is the author’s, unchanged; what has been added is the apparatus — a caption and descriptive alt text for every plate, modern typesetting, and an ebook worth reading on a real device. Where a book captioned its own illustrations, those captions are the author’s and stand as printed.
