Bird banding stations are operated at many Federal Refuges to study migrations. The birds are caught without injury in cage traps and a numbered metal band or bracelet is attached to the leg. The sex, estimated age, and weight are recorded. Black ducks banded in the Parker River area have been recovered in all the Canadian Maritime Provinces, Newfoundland, and Labrador, and in every coastal State from Maine to Georgia with the exception of South Carolina.
