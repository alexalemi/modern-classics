UNCERTAIN READINGS -- Tenzo Kyokun, 1909 Eiheiji edition (NDL 823141)

p011  the rice-grain passage (喫一粒米添一粒米 ...)
  Several columns of arithmetic about grains of rice and the Luling
  rice of Qingyuan are set very close and I could not read them with
  confidence. Transcribed only as far as I am sure of, and the passage
  is marked here rather than guessed at. Re-read against the 1806
  impression before publishing.

p011  典座在佛殿前晒苔
  The object being dried in the sun in front of the Buddha hall: read
  as 苔 (moss/mushrooms) but not certain. The story is the old cook
  drying something on the hot stones, which is what matters, but the
  noun should be settled.
