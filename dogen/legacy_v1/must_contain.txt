# Passages this edition is not allowed to lose, written FROM the
# finished translation and never from memory (the epictetus rule).
# That rule bit twice already in this book: the famous 身心脱落 is
# 身心自然脱落 in the Fukanzazengi, with 自然 in the middle of it, and
# my predicted word/character ratio was out by a factor of two.

# Fukanzazengi, opening. The whole argument of Dogen's Zen is in the
# first two sentences: if the way is already complete, what is
# practice for? The book is the answer.
the way is basically complete and reaches everywhere

# The hair's breadth. Pinned because it is the hinge of the opening.
a hair's breadth of difference and it is the gap between heaven and earth

# The step backwards, and the phrase Dogen received from Rujing --
# with 自然 kept, which is what the source actually says.
Body and mind will drop away of themselves, and your original face

# The posture instructions, pinned because the edition's claim is that
# these are instructions and not poetry.
The ears should be in line with the shoulders, and the nose in line with the navel

# The most famous sentence in Zen, and the one most often softened.
# 非思量 is BEYOND thinking, not "not thinking", which asserts the
# opposite of what the passage argues.
Think of the thing that does not think. How do you think of the thing
that does not think? Beyond thinking.

# The dragon and the tiger.
a dragon that has got to water, a tiger that has got to its mountain

# The close of the Fukanzazengi.
The treasure house will open of itself, and you will use it as you please.

# Gakudo Yojinshu I -- the definition, and the target.
the mind that observes impermanence

# Gakudo Yojinshu III. The single most quoted line of the ten sections.
The treasure of your own house does not come from outside.

# Gakudo Yojinshu IV -- the rule the whole section builds to.
He must practise the buddha-dharma only for the buddha-dharma.

# Gakudo Yojinshu V. Dogen's verdict on his own country, kept entire
# and unhedged (the Verne rule): it is the reason he went to China and
# the reason he thought the book was needed.
in our country there has never yet been a true teacher

# and the counting of another man's treasure, which is the image the
# whole indictment turns on.
Day and night they count another man's treasure, and have not half a coin

# Gakudo Yojinshu VI, against easy practice.
do not set your mind on easy practice

# Gakudo Yojinshu VIII -- Zhaozhou's dog, in the flat form. The whole
# koan dies if "no" is glossed or expanded.
does a dog have buddha-nature or not? Zhaozhou said: no.

# and the instruction that follows it.
Please try letting go. Just let go and look.

# --- Tenzo Kyokun ---
# The instruction the whole text turns on, and the reason it is in this
# volume: the cook's work IS the practice, not a support for it.
Take up a single blade of grass and build a sanctuary for the
treasure-king; enter a single mote of dust and turn the great wheel of
the dharma.

# The pot and the water. Pinned because it is the plainest statement of
# the same argument and is easy to soften into metaphor.
treat the pot as your own head, and treat the water as your own life

# The old cook at Tiantong, drying things on the scorching stones at
# sixty-eight. Two answers, four words each, and the whole book is in
# them. Pinned in the flat form; any expansion kills them.
other people are not me

when, if not now?

# The exchange on the ship and at Tiantong. The joke only works if the
# answers stay this blunt.
one, two, three, four, five

nothing in the whole world is hidden

# The three minds, named. A reader must be able to find them.
the joyful mind, the parental mind, and the magnanimous mind

# The parental mind, which is the passage people remember.
They do not mind their own cold, and they cover the child.

# The colophon, which dates the book and names what it is for.
shown to gentlemen who study the way hereafter
