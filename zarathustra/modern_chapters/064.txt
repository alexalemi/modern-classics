Epigraph to Part Four

Ah, where in the world have greater follies happened than among the pitying? And what in the world has caused more suffering than the follies of the pitying? Woe to all lovers who do not yet have a height that is above their pity! Thus spoke the devil to me once: “Even God has his hell: it is his love of man.” And recently I heard him say this word: “God is dead; God died of his pity for man.”

Zarathustra, “On the Pitying”
