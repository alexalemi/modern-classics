Dedication

To the beloved and mourned memory of her who was the inspirer and in part the author of all that is best in my writings — the friend and wife whose lofty sense of truth and right was my strongest spur, and whose approval was my chief reward — I dedicate this volume. Like everything I have written for many years, it belongs as much to her as to me; but the work as it stands has had, to a very inadequate degree, the priceless benefit of her revision, since some of the most important parts were held back for a more careful re-examination that they are now never to receive. If I were capable of conveying to the world half the great thoughts and noble feelings that are buried in her grave, I would be the means of a greater benefit to it than is ever likely to come from anything I can write without her prompting and without the help of her almost unrivalled wisdom.
