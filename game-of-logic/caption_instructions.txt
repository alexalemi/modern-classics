CAPTION AND PROOF PASS: Lewis Carroll, The Game of Logic (1887)

A RESTORED EDITION: Carroll's text unchanged. His diagrams were set in type
in 1887 (ruled boxes; the digit 1 for a red counter, 0 for a grey one; a
counter "on the fence" printed across a division line) and are REDRAWN here
from Gutenberg's ASCII transcription. Gutenberg's ASCII HAS ERRORS: answer 7
on page 60 was transcribed with two 1s where the print has one. So this pass
does two jobs for every plate: PROOF the redrawing against the printed page,
and CAPTION it for readers who cannot see it.

Read /home/alemi/projects/modern-classics/CAPTIONS.md first.

FOR EACH PLATE ID IN YOUR RANGE
  the drawing:   site/images/game-of-logic/fig<id>.png   (Read it)
  its place:     game-of-logic/chapters/NNN.txt, find "[Figure <id>]"
                 (a table row "7. | [Figure ab] | Some x are y'." puts the
                 exercise number and the reading beside the plate)
  the print:     game-of-logic/_src/png/<page>.png, where <page> is in
                 game-of-logic/_src/pagemap.json. THE MAPPING IS APPROXIMATE:
                 if the diagram is not there, look one or two pages either
                 side. Pages are named by scan leaf, not printed page.

1. PROOF: compare the drawing with the printed diagram. Same shape (half,
   smaller or larger Diagram; which half), same counters (1s and 0s) in the
   same compartments, a counter on the fence where the print has one, same
   letters (x, y, m, x', y', m') where printed. REPORT every difference as
   "<id>: printed ... / drawn ..." with the page. Do not change any file
   for this; the orchestrator fixes the source and redraws.

2. CAPTION: one sentence, 10-30 words, saying exactly what is marked, in
   Carroll's own vocabulary: the smaller Diagram (four compartments,
   numbered 5 and 6 above, 7 and 8 below), the larger Diagram (the outer
   compartments 9, 10, 15, 16 and the inner square's 11, 12, 13, 14, the
   inner square being the m part), the upper half (x), the lower half (x'),
   the left half (y), the right half (y'); a red counter (1), a grey counter
   (0); "on the line between" for a counter on the fence. Name compartments
   by their numbers where you can and by position too
   ("a red counter in No. 5, the upper left compartment").
   DESCRIBE THE PRINTED DIAGRAM: if the drawing is wrong, caption what the
   page prints (and report the difference).
   A CAPTION MUST NOT GIVE AN ANSWER THE BOOK ASKS THE READER TO FIND: in
   "Symbols to be interpreted" exercises, say where the counters are, never
   what the diagram means. Where the reading is printed beside the plate
   (the answers), the caption still describes only the marks.
   Plain prose, no markup, no tabs.

WRITE game-of-logic/captions/<first-id>.txt (one file per agent, named by
the first id of your range), one line per plate: <id><TAB><caption>.
Then run: python3 game-of-logic/prep.py   and confirm it completes.

RETURN: the ids captioned, and the full list of proof differences.
