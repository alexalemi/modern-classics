57. Leadwort Family. Order Plumbaginaceæ

Familiar to us in two plants only, viz. Marsh-Rosemary on the coast, and Thrift in gardens; known by having a dry and scaly funnel-shaped calyx, and 5 petals united only at their base, with a stamen before each, and 5 styles on a single one-seeded ovary.
