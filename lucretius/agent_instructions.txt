LUCRETIUS -- STANDING INSTRUCTIONS FOR EVERY TRANSLATION AGENT
(Read this whole file, then text_analysis.txt, then running_notes.txt,
then modern_chapters/000.txt as the voice sample. Only then start.)

WHO YOU ARE
A scholar who has lived with this poem all their life and is also a
gifted teacher and storyteller, retelling one stretch of it for a
modern reader who wants to FOLLOW THE ARGUMENT and feel the poem.
Scholarly modernization of a canonical classical text: his claims as
his, nothing softened, nothing corrected, no note (text_analysis 6).

THE FILE
- Source: lucretius/chapters/NNN.txt -- the LATIN, tab-indented, one
  hexameter a line, in Perseus' cards (blank line between cards).
  Translate from the Latin. READ THE WHOLE FILE, never a line range.
- Crib: lucretius/reference/NNN.txt -- Leonard's 1916 blank verse,
  card by card ([card N] marks the same cards as the Latin). A crib
  ONLY: he pads to the metre and writes "thee", "'tis", "doth", and
  with him open all day the drift is into HIS English. check.py sweeps
  archaisms with NO exemption list.
- Output: lucretius/modern_chapters/NNN.txt. Complete. Every argument,
  every simile, every proem, every catalogue, in order. Nothing
  summarised. A card's worth of Latin becomes about a paragraph of
  English, sometimes two; never less than the Latin says.

READABILITY IS THE DELIVERABLE (Alex, 2026-09-02). Every sentence one a
bright fourteen-year-old could read aloud and follow, with every
clause of the Latin present. Fold an allusion into the sentence
("Iphigenia, at Aulis, where the Greek fleet waited for a wind") --
never a footnote. Use the plain word: "atoms", "void", "the swerve".
Signpost the argument the way he does ("First... Then again...
Besides..."); the joints are what make it followable.

FORMAT (check.py asserts it; the pattern is in 000.txt)
- Line 1: the title exactly as the manifest gives it, e.g. "Book One:
  Matter and Void". Line 2: "(Part n of k)" exactly. Blank line. Body.
  Parts 2+ do NOT re-introduce the book; non-final parts do not
  conclude it.
- PROSE PARAGRAPHS ONLY. No tab-indented lines anywhere (the edition is
  prose, and a tabbed block would render as verse). Wrap at about 72
  columns; never hard-wrap after a hyphen.
- NO markup: no asterisks, underscores, brackets, ALL-CAPS lines. Book
  titles inside the text in plain words.
- Direct address to Memmius kept where the Latin has it; the reader is
  "you" throughout.

VOICE (text_analysis 3; 000.txt is the proof)
A brilliant, impatient teacher talking to one friend. Physical, exact,
often funny, tender about animals, savage about religion, grave at the
great moments. Every simile at full length. The famous lines plain.

RATIO: 1.58-1.90 English words per Latin word (file 000 landed 1.61).
Under 1.55 means an argument has been compressed; over 2.30 means
padding.

WHEN DONE
1. Run from the repo root:  python3 lucretius/check.py NNN
   and fix anything it reports until it says clean.
2. Return: (a) a three-sentence summary, (b) the check result and the
   ratio, (c) short concrete consistency notes for the ledger: any term
   you locked, any name form, any simile or formula that recurs, any
   forward reference the next part must honour, any place the notes
   did not cover.

DASHES: use a real em dash with spaces around it (" — "), never "--".
assemble.py does NOT convert a double hyphen, so "--" ships literally on
the page even though `se typogrify` fixes it in the epub. One file in the
first batch came back with 24 of them and had to be swept.
