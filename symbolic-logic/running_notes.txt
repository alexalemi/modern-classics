WHY THIS BOOK IS THE TRAPPED ONE
Victorian notation, a diagram method that is Carroll's own and nobody
else's, and a terminology later logic abandoned — so a modern reader
WITH logic training is more lost than one without. The diagrams are the
notation, not decoration: the method is a square divided into
compartments with red and grey counters laid on it, and the prose says
"this we can represent by placing a Red Counter on the partition which
divides it" and then shows you.

WHAT PREP ALREADY HANDLES
- 509 PRINT-PAGE CROSS-REFERENCES rewritten to name the section they
  point at, because "p. 12" means nothing in a reflowable edition and
  Carroll's argument is cumulative and leans on them constantly. This is
  the star-land print-page rule at a scale that had to be mechanical.
- 314 DIAGRAMS carry Carroll's own alt text through as the caption seed,
  to be MODERNISED not replaced (the ball/ rule).
- 108 TABLES emitted as indented blocks. A CELL IS NOT A PARAGRAPH —
  the lesson thompson/ paid for.
- 130 BLOCKQUOTED ASIDES kept and marked with brackets; they are how he
  teaches, not ornament.

STILL TO DECIDE BEFORE TRANSLATION
- Three stub files (013, 018, 030) are Book subject-lines that should be
  folded into their dividers rather than standing as 6-word sections.
- Chapter titles are still in the source's SHOUTING CAPITALS; they want
  title case.
- THE BIG ONE: how much of Carroll's terminology to keep. His
  "Proposition of Existence", "Proposition of Relation", "Retinend",
  "Eliminand", "Cosmophily" and so on are load-bearing inside his system
  and meaningless outside it. The Fleming rule (dated CLAIMS kept, dated
  WORDS modernised) does not settle this, because here the words ARE the
  system. Decide it once, in text_analysis, before a line is translated.
