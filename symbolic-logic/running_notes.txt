WHY THIS BOOK IS THE TRAPPED ONE
Victorian notation, a diagram method that is Carroll's own and nobody
else's, and a terminology later logic abandoned — so a modern reader
WITH logic training is more lost than one without. The diagrams are the
notation, not decoration: the method is a square divided into
compartments with red and grey counters laid on it, and the prose says
"this we can represent by placing a Red Counter on the partition which
divides it" and then shows you.

SUBSTITUTED TERMS IN THE EXERCISES OF BOOK EIGHT (decided with the
project owner, 2026-08; the reading text carries no note)
Five of Carroll's exercises used racial or antisemitic terms as the
Classes to be reasoned about. In every one the logic is indifferent to
the choice -- any Class serves, the subscripts are unchanged, and the
Answers and Solutions still come out as printed. That is exactly why
they go: the word is doing no work that a neutral Term cannot do, and
this is a book addressed to children of twelve to fourteen who are
being invited to manipulate these propositions as their own. This is
NOT the Verne rule, which protects a dated CLAIM the author is making
as part of his subject (the aether, lunar volcanoes, Ball's astronomy).
Nothing here is Carroll's subject. The Bunyan precedent (the Flatterer
rendered "black of body") is the one that governs: change it, record
it, do not wink at the reader in the text.

CAPTIONS ON THE EXERCISE DIAGRAMS MUST NOT PRINT THE ANSWER. § 3 of the
Examples sets twenty marked Triliteral Diagrams to be interpreted, and
the source's alt text on each is the interpretation -- "Diagram
including all y prime are x prime", or, for the two that yield nothing,
"Diagram including no useful information". A sighted reader sees the
diagram; a reader using a screen reader was being handed the answer
under the question. All twenty are captioned by their MARKS instead,
and every one was checked against the printed Answer to make sure the
marks read correctly.
