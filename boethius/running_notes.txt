THE VERSE RULE, which is the book's central decision

Boethius wrote the 39 metra UNRHYMED, in some twenty-eight different
classical quantitative metres. THE RHYME IS JAMES'S VICTORIAN ADDITION.
Dropping it removes a costume the TRANSLATOR put on — the same argument
that governs Pusey's "Thou" in augustine/ and Burton's invented English
in nights/.

So: VERSE STAYS VERSE — never prose, which is this book's silent
summarisation — in clear modern English with a strong audible rhythm,
unrhymed, EXCEPT where a rhyme arrives at no cost to sense. Never
contort a line to reach a rhyme; contortion is what we are removing.

MECHANICALLY ENFORCED by check.py, per file, all three exactly:
  - the number of indented BLOCKS
  - the number of indented LINES
  - the INDENT PATTERN — which lines are the deeper-indented ones. The
    source marks the shorter line of each couplet that way, so the
    pattern is the metrical shape, and matching it proves the couplets
    survived where counting alone would not.
If a line genuinely has to be split or merged, that is a DECISION and
it goes in this ledger; it does not get absorbed by loosening the tool.

NEAR-MISSES REGISTERED: NOT "everlasting life" or "life without end"
for the definition of eternity — the whole point is that eternity is
not endless duration but possession all at once. NOT "all fortune is
good fortune" (the source has "every"). NOT "the wheel of fate".


GLOSSED ONCE, THEN USED BARE — the "caps spent" list

  Fortune           the antagonist. Never "luck", never "chance".
  the highest good  Book Three's hinge.
  Providence / Fate DISTINGUISHED at IV.6 and never blurred: Providence
                    is the plan seen whole, Fate the same plan worked
                    out in time. The distinction IS the answer to the
                    problem of evil.
  necessity         V.6 turns on ABSOLUTE necessity versus necessity
                    CONDITIONAL on knowledge. Blur it and the book's
                    conclusion collapses.
  eternity          NOT everlastingness. See the pin.
  substance/accident, form/matter — glossed at first use.

MODERNISED SILENTLY: the whole thou-family, plus erstwhile, ofttimes,
haply, wot, ween, vouchsafe, peradventure. FALSE FRIENDS confirmed
present: want (= lack), still (= always), prevent (= come before),
sensible (= perceptible), several (= separate), presently (= at once),
estate (= condition), suffer (= allow).


VOICE

PHILOSOPHY is a clinician who is fond of the patient. She diagnoses,
prescribes, checks whether the medicine has worked, and escalates from
"gentler remedies" to "stronger" as the books go on — the medical frame
is explicit and load-bearing. Brisk, a little severe, and frequently
funny at Boethius' expense.
BOETHIUS objects, concedes and loses the thread. Keep him distinct. In
Book One he is pure self-pity; by Book Five he is asking the hard
questions himself, and that progression is the plot.
FORTUNE speaks in her own voice at II.2 and is not to be neutralised.


THE VERNE RULE, in its strangest application

There is NOT ONE WORD of Christianity in this book, written by a man
who wrote theological tractates. No Christ, no Scripture, no church;
Philosophy consoles him with philosophy alone. The silence is
deliberate and is the most discussed fact about the Consolation.
DO NOT CHRISTIANISE AND DO NOT DE-CHRISTIANISE, and add no note about
the absence. Where James's phrasing tips devotional — he is a little
more pious than the Latin — bring it back to neutral.
The Platonism stands as written: recollection, the pre-existent soul,
the world soul in the Book Three hymn. So do the astronomy, the
geography of the known world at II.7, and the account of the wicked as
beasts at IV.3.

THE GREEK LETTERS ARE GLOSSED INLINE (I.1). Philosophy's robe carries Π
at the hem and θ at the collar with a staircase between them. James
prints the bare letters and puts the meaning in an endnote; a modern
reader cannot possibly recover it, and without it the image — a
staircase climbing from the practical life to the life of thought — is
just two foreign characters. So the gloss is folded into the sentence
("the Greek letter Π, which stands for the practical life... θ, for the
life of thought") rather than added as a note. This is the "glossed
once at first use" rule applied to a symbol instead of a word, and it
is the only place in the book where James's endnotes are drawn on.

"stagey little tarts" for James's "playacting wantons" (scaenicas
meretriculas). Philosophy is being contemptuous and the Latin is blunt;
Victorian decorum softened it and the modern rendering takes it back.
Not coarse for its own sake — the whole point of the scene is that she
throws them out.
