STATE: prep done and verified structurally. 9 files, 24,377 words, 64
plates, 2,436 formulas converted from LaTeX to Unicode by tex.py.

THE SOURCE IS UNLIKE ANY OTHER IN THE PROJECT. There is no plain-text
edition because the mathematics is not text: it is 2,436 separate SVG
files pulled in by <img>, one per symbol. Every one carries a data-tex
attribute with the LaTeX it was rendered from, and across all of them
there are only 55 distinct commands and three environments. See tex.py.

DECIDED BEFORE TRANSLATION (Alex): MODERNISE THE NOTATION, and render it
in both formats rather than falling back to plates. The opposite of the
symbolic-logic ruling, and for a stated reason — there the words were the
machine, here the notation is incidental to the argument.

BUGS FOUND AND FIXED IN THE CONVERTER, every one of which produced
plausible, readable, WRONG arithmetic:
  - The mid-height decimal point read as multiplication: 1.5430806
    became "1· 5430806". Settled against the MathSpeak in the alt text,
    which says "18 dot 65 degree" in all 41 places a digit follows.
  - A literal "." between atoms is his multiplication sign; "1/2.c/2" is
    not a number. But the decimal point and the dots in "i. e." must
    survive that pass, so both ride on sentinels.
  - The vinculum is a BRACKET: "2×10 - x-1-with-a-bar" is 2×10 - (x-1),
    and dropping the bar flips the sign of the 1 silently. Over a bare
    pair of letters it is a line segment and means no such thing.
  - "\\&c." is a row break followed by content; protecting the ampersand
    before splitting rows eats the break and welds two lines together.
  - AN EXPONENT MUST SWALLOW ITS COMMAND'S ARGUMENTS. "2^\tfrac{3}{4}"
    is 2 to the three-quarters; reading only the command name left the
    exponent as a bare "/" and spilled the 3 and the 4 into the line —
    "2^/34" — in nineteen formulas, including the one whose making the
    Introduction narrates (Problem 63).
  - "A. P." and "A. M." are arithmetical progression and arithmetic
    mean, not products. They are set as mathematics and cannot be told
    from a product by shape, only by being listed.
