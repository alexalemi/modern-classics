cj	Three-card monte: the right hand holds two cards, the upper by the second finger and thumb and the ace below by the third finger and thumb, while the left hand shows the third card.
ck	The right thumb and first finger slide the hand card under the right side of the card on the table until it is about two-thirds concealed.
cl	The thumb carries the table card away to the left against the second finger while the first fingertip turns over the card beneath.
