bj	Top palm, first method: the right hand covers the deck held in the left, its little finger pulling the top cards up about half an inch at the corner, clear of the left fingers.
bk	The right first finger is straightened and the left third finger presses the cards to be palmed up into the right palm, which now arches over them above the deck.
bl	Seen from beneath, the right hand holds the palmed cards against its palm while the deck is drawn half out from under it, and the left hand has let go.
bm	Bottom palm, first method: the right hand holds the deck on top by the ends while the left second finger grips the bottom cards at the corner and pulls them down past the right thumb.
bn	The right little fingertip catches the projecting corner of the under cards and draws them in toward the right thumb until they lie along the left palm.
bo	Bottom palm, second method: the right hand holds the deck by the ends, first finger curled on top, while the left little finger at its first joint grips the corner of the under cards.
bp	The finger end of the upper portion of the deck is swung out against the left thumb, pivoting on the right thumb, until the finger ends of the two packets are just past each other.
bq	Replacing the bottom palm: the right hand lays the deck by its ends into the left hand, where the palmed cards lie face out along the left fingers, first finger curled underneath.
br	After a riffle the deck is raised on its end on the table, held at the sides between the second finger and thumb of each hand, and tapped to square it.
bs	The deck lies on its side on the table, the right second finger and thumb at its ends and the left fingers brought round to the end and against the bottom.
bt	The finger end of the top portion is swung against the left thumb, pivoting on the right thumb, until the ends of the two packets are past each other, the under packet showing below.
