bb	Locating cards for stocking: the deck held back to palm in the left hand with the right hand covering the face, the left thumb springing the cards so that the indices can be seen.
bc	The lower side of the deck opened at a located card and fingers of the left hand inserted, slipping the card out to the top while the right hand covers the face.
bd	Jogging a located card: the packet held by the right thumb and little finger is tilted outward past the corner of the packet in the left hand, opening the deck at the desired card, a club.
be	The left hand draws off the top packet and with the same movement slides out the jogged cards, dropping them on the table, while the right hand holds the rest of the deck for the running cut.
