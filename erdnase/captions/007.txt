ae	The top stock riffle begun: two packets end to end on the table, both hands resting on their outer ends, the left first and second fingers covering the in-jogged top card of the left packet.
af	The two packets riffled together on the table, seen from the performer's side, with the last card still held up by the left thumb above the interlaced ends.
ag	Squaring up after the top stock riffle: the left hand covers the deck with its thumb on the top card while the right hand, held more openly, pushes the right packet in.
ah	The position for the bottom stock riffle: each packet held at the sides near the adjoining ends between second finger and thumb, the thumb corners raised and springing together at the middle.
ai	Telescoping the riffled packets: the hands have shifted to the outer ends, holding the side corners with thumbs and second fingers, and the interlaced cards are pushed about two-thirds together.
aj	Squaring up the deck on the table, a second finger at the middle of each end and the thumbs together at the inner side, sliding the cards into position.
