9. Mr. J. Paul White's Harmon.

Mr. James Paul White, of Springfield, Massachusetts, U.S. America, a tuner by profession, having been much impressed by Mr. Poole's papers in *Silliman's Journal*, cited under No. 7, determined to realise them so far as possible by means of the 53 division of the Octave.
