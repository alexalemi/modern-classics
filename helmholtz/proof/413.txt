From the observed curves, fig. 23 B, C, p. 82b, and fig. 24 A, B, p. 83b, it follows that all points of the string ascend and descend alternately, that the ascent is made with a constant velocity, and also the descent with a constant velocity, which is however different from the velocity of ascent. When the bow is drawn across a node of one of the upper partials of the string, the motion takes place in all nodes of the same tone precisely in the manner described. For other points of the string, little crumples are perceptible in the vibrational figure, but they do ¶ not prevent us from clearly recognising the principal motion.

If in fig. 62 we reckon the time from the abscissa of the point \(\alpha\), so that for \(\alpha\), \(t = 0\), and further for the point \(\beta\) put \(t = \tau\), and for the point \(\gamma\) put \(t = T\), so that the last represents the length of a whole period; then \[\left.\begin{aligned} y &= ft + h, \text{ from } t = 0 \textit{ to } t = \tau; \\ y &= g (T - t) + h, \text{ from } t = \tau \text{ to } t = T, \end{aligned}\right\} \tag{1}\] whence for \(t = \tau\), it results that ¶ \[f\tau = g (T - \tau).\]

Now suppose \(y\) to be developed in one of Fourier's series: \[\begin{aligned} y = {} & A_1 \cdot \sin \frac{2\pi t}{T} + A_2 \cdot \sin \frac{4\pi t}{T} + A_3 \cdot \sin \frac{6\pi t}{T} \text{ \&c.} \\ & + B_1 \cdot \cos \frac{2\pi t}{T} + B_2 \cdot \cos \frac{4\pi t}{T} + B_3 \cdot \cos \frac{6\pi t}{T} \text{ \&c.} \end{aligned}\] then it results from integration that \[A_n \cdot \int_0^T \sin^2 \frac{2n\pi t}{T} \cdot dt = \int_0^T y \cdot \sin \frac{2n\pi t}{T} \cdot dt\] ¶ \[B_n \cdot \int_0^T \cos^2 \frac{2n\pi t}{T} \cdot dt = \int_0^T y \cdot \cos \frac{2n\pi t}{T} \cdot dt,\] and this gives the following values for \(A_n\) and \(B_n\): \[A_n = \frac{(g + f) \cdot T}{2n^2\pi^2} \cdot \sin \frac{2n\pi t}{T}\] \[B_n = -\frac{(g + f) \cdot T}{2n^2\pi^2} \cdot \left\{1 - \cos \frac{2n\pi t}{T}\right\}\] and \(y\) may then be written in the form \[y = \frac{(g + f) \cdot T}{\pi^2} \cdot \sum_{n = 1}^{n = \infty} \left\{\frac{1}{n^2} \cdot \sin \frac{\pi n \tau}{T} \cdot \sin \frac{2\pi n}{T} \left(t - \frac{\tau}{2}\right)\right\} \tag{2}\]
