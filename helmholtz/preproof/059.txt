But if the ear is able to analyse a composite musical tone produced by two tuning-forks, it cannot but be in a condition to ¶ carry out a similar analysis, when the same motion of the air is produced by a single flute or organ pipe. And this is really the case. The single musical tone of such instruments, proceeding from a single source, is, as we have already mentioned, analysed into partial simple tones, consisting in each case of a prime tone, and one upper partial tone, the latter being different in the two cases.
