¶ The following four are distinguished as the principal modes of the Mahamat: —

1. Uschak = Pythagorean F major.

2. Rast = Natural mode of the Fourth of G, or natural F major with acute

Sixth.

3. Husseini = Natural mode of the minor Seventh of F.

4. Hidschaf = Natural mode of the Fourth of F.

We find, then, a decided predominance of scales with a perfectly correct natural intonation, which has been attained by a skilful use of a continued series of Fifths. This makes the Arabic and Persian tonal system very noteworthy in the history of the development of music. Moreover, in some of these scales we find ascending leading notes, which are perfectly foreign to the Greek scales.
