As before, it is convenient to have a single number as a tag for the sequence. We agree therefore to associate with it the number which is the product of the first two primes in order of magnitude (i.e., the primes 2 and 3), each prime being raised to a power equal to the Gödel number of the corresponding formula in the sequence. If we call this number *k,* we can write *k* = 2^{*m*} × 3^{*n*}. By applying this compact procedure we can obtain a number for each sequence of formulas. In sum, every expression in the system, whether an elementary sign, a sequence of signs, or a sequence of sequences, can be assigned a unique Gödel number.
