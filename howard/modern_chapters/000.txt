Frontispiece and Epigraph

[Figure aa: Ebenezer Howard — A head-and-shoulders photograph of Howard in spectacles, with a heavy moustache, dark jacket and patterned tie, looking off to one side; beneath it is his handwritten signature, “Yours very truly E. Howard.”]

	“New occasions teach new duties;
	Time makes ancient good uncouth;
	They must upward still, and onward,
	Who would keep abreast of Truth.
	Lo, before us, gleam her camp-fires!
	We ourselves must Pilgrims be,
	Launch our ‘Mayflower,’ and steer boldly
	Through the desperate winter sea,
	Nor attempt the Future’s portal
	With the Past’s blood-rusted key.”
