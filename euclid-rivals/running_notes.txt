WHAT THE TRANSLATION PASS ACTUALLY INVOLVES, which is more than
translating. Every paragraph has to be read against the OCR at the same
time, and the residue is exactly the class no check can see:

  - REAL-WORD OCR ERRORS. "lie" for "he", "Kerens" for "Here's", "AYe"
    for "We", "hoik" for "both". A dictionary cannot see any of them.
  - GEOMETRICAL LABELS, which repair.py deliberately refuses to touch
    (see its guards). "For A Gil and EGB are equal because vertically
    opposite ... therefore AGE is equal to GIID" has to be read back into
    "For AGH and EGB are equal ... therefore AGH is equal to GHD", and
    the only way to do that is to reconstruct the geometry: E above and F
    below, the transversal cutting AB at G and CD at H, so AGH and EGB
    are the vertically opposite pair.
  - PROPOSITION NUMBERS, AND BOTH SCANS GET THEM WRONG IN DIFFERENT
    PLACES. In one exchange the 1885 says "this proof of I. 39" and the
    1879 says "I. 29"; the proposition concerns alternate angles and
    parallels, so it is I. 29 and the 1885 is wrong. Six lines later the
    1885 has "= 2 right angles" and the 1879 "= 3"; the angles of a
    triangle are two right angles, so this time the 1879 is wrong. ONLY
    THE MATHEMATICS SETTLES IT. repair.py refuses to touch a numeral for
    exactly this reason -- it cannot know -- so every one is a question
    for the reader.

THE SPEAKER SEQUENCE IS AN EXACT INVARIANT. chapters/ now carries every
tag Carroll printed, in order, correctly attributed; the modern file's
sequence must match it name for name. Files 000-002 do. If check.py
reports a difference, the translation is wrong, not the source -- eight
separate prep bugs were fixed to make that true (mangled tags, tags the
scan never marked italic, tags buried mid-paragraph, tags with an empty
body). Never "fix" a mismatch by adding or dropping a tag to make the
count agree; find the speech.

CONVENTIONS, all three checked mechanically:

  ASIDES take Carroll's own form -- tag, period, then the parenthetical:
      Minos. (thoughtfully) Well, if we had him in the Schools ...
      Euclid. (preserves a disgusted silence)
  NOT the modern playscript form "Minos (thoughtfully).", which breaks the
  sequence check. Forty-odd of these remain, most of them Niemand's
  evasions in Act Two -- and the manner is the comedy, so keep every one.

  CONSECUTIVE SPEECHES BY ONE SPEAKER STAY TWO SPEECHES. Carroll prints
  "Euc. ... / Euc. In the second place, ..." and a heading often stands
  between them. Merging them into one speech with two paragraphs reads
  better and fails the check; the tag is his, so keep it.

  SECTION HEADINGS TAKE NO TERMINAL PERIOD AND ARE TITLE CASE:
      § 5. Playfair's Axiom          NOT  § 5. Playfair's Axiom.
      § 7. The Omission of Diagonals in Euclid II
      Table II / Table III / Euclid's Axiom / Clavius's Axiom
  ball/'s trap: assemble.is_subheading() rejects anything ending in
  ".;:,--" and anything not majority-capitalised, so the period alone is
  the difference between an <h4> and a paragraph shouted in capitals.
  Fourteen headings in 001-002 were written wrong and fixed.

  NEVER WRITE AN ALL-CAPS LINE. assemble.py reads one as a heading.

MANIFEST TITLES ARE FIXED and prep asserts the eleven-section structure
against the book's own ARGUMENT OF DRAMA. Head each file with the exact
manifest title, e.g. "Act Two, Scene Six: Wilson, Pierce and Willock" and
"(Part 2 of 3)". Act Four has no Scene: its title is just "Act Four".
The printer's descriptive line under each Scene heading ("Treatment of
Parallels by equidistances") is furniture and is dropped -- the scene's
own opening exchange always states the method.

PRINT-PAGE REFERENCES TO OTHER AUTHORS' BOOKS ARE KEPT as printed --
"pointed out by Mr. Todhunter (see pp. 222, 241)". Dropping it is how the
numeral check earned its keep in 001. References to THIS book's own pages
go to word form.

THE II. 4 PROOF (file 002) had to be rebuilt from the standard figure --
the 1885 scan reduces it to "but ^Z= C6^, and (^Z^ CT". The square is
ABED on AB (A bottom-left, B bottom-right, E top-right, D top-left), BD
the diagonal, CF parallel to AD or BE cutting BD at G, HK parallel to AB
or DE. Then CK is the square on CB, HF the square on AC, and AG, GE the
complements. Any later reference to it must use these letters.

CARROLL'S WORD COUNTS (128 for his proof, 145 for Wilson's, "seventeen
words" longer) ARE KEPT AS PRINTED. They count the 1885 text, not the
modernisation, and the joke is the difference. Do not recount them.
