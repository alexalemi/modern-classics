WHAT THE TRANSLATION PASS ACTUALLY INVOLVES, which is more than
translating. Every paragraph has to be read against the OCR at the same
time, and the residue is exactly the class no check can see:

  - REAL-WORD OCR ERRORS. "lie" for "he", "Kerens" for "Here's", "AYe"
    for "We", "hoik" for "both". A dictionary cannot see any of them.
  - GEOMETRICAL LABELS, which repair.py deliberately refuses to touch
    (see its guards). "For A Gil and EGB are equal because vertically
    opposite ... therefore AGE is equal to GIID" has to be read back into
    "For AGH and EGB are equal ... therefore AGH is equal to GHD", and
    the only way to do that is to reconstruct the geometry: E above and F
    below, the transversal cutting AB at G and CD at H, so AGH and EGB
    are the vertically opposite pair.
  - PROPOSITION NUMBERS, AND BOTH SCANS GET THEM WRONG IN DIFFERENT
    PLACES. In one exchange the 1885 says "this proof of I. 39" and the
    1879 says "I. 29"; the proposition concerns alternate angles and
    parallels, so it is I. 29 and the 1885 is wrong. Six lines later the
    1885 has "= 2 right angles" and the 1879 "= 3"; the angles of a
    triangle are two right angles, so this time the 1879 is wrong. ONLY
    THE MATHEMATICS SETTLES IT. repair.py refuses to touch a numeral for
    exactly this reason -- it cannot know -- so every one is a question
    for the reader.
