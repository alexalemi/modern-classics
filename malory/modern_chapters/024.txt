Chapter 24: How Merlin saved Arthur's life, and cast an enchantment on King Pellinore and put him to sleep

With that Merlin came and said, "Knight, hold your hand! For if you kill that knight you will do this realm the greatest harm that any realm ever suffered. For this knight is a man of more honour than you know."

"Why, who is he?" said the knight.

"It is King Arthur."

Then he would have killed him, for fear of his anger, and raised his sword. And with that Merlin cast an enchantment on the knight, so that he fell to the earth in a deep sleep. Then Merlin took up King Arthur, and rode off on the knight's horse.

"Alas!" said Arthur. "What have you done, Merlin? Have you killed this good knight with your arts? There is no knight alive as honourable as he was. I would give a year's revenue from my land to have him alive again."

"Don't worry," said Merlin, "for he is in better health than you; he is only asleep, and will wake within three hours. I told you," said Merlin, "what a knight he was. You would have been killed here if it had not been for me. Besides, there is no bigger knight alive than he is, and from now on he will do you very good service. And his name is Pellinore, and he will have two sons who will be exceedingly good men; except for one, they will have no equal in prowess and in good living. And their names will be Percival of Wales and Lamorak of Wales. And he will tell you the name of your own son, fathered on your sister, who will be the destruction of all this realm."
