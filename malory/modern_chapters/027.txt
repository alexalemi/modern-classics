Chapter 27: How all the children born on May Day were sent for, and how Mordred was saved

Then King Arthur had all the children born on May Day sent for, fathered by lords and born of ladies; for Merlin had told King Arthur that the one who would destroy him would be born on May Day. And so he sent for them all, on pain of death. And so many lords' sons were found, and all were sent to the king, and so Mordred was sent by King Lot's wife. And all were put in a ship and sent out to sea, and some were four weeks old, and some less. And so by chance the ship was driven against a castle, and was broken all to pieces, and most of them were lost, except that Mordred was cast up, and a good man found him, and brought him up until he was fourteen years old, and then he brought him to the court, as it is told afterwards, towards the end of the Death of Arthur. So many lords and barons of this realm were displeased, because their children were lost like this, and many put the blame on Merlin more than on Arthur; so, what with fear and what with love, they held their peace. But when the messenger came back to King Rience, he was mad with rage beyond measure, and gathered a great army, as it is told later in the book of Balin le Savage, which follows next, and how by chance Balin got the sword.

Here ends the first book.
