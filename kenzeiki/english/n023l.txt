The Founder accordingly went in company with the reverend Myozen. They
left Higashiyama on the twenty-second day of the second month, made for
the port of Hakata in Chikuzen on the western sea, and in the last ten
days of the third month boarded a merchant ship and cast off.

The two travel permits for that voyage were kept in the treasury at
Eiheiji until they were burnt on the ninth day of the third month of
the year of the horse, in the Shotoku era. They read:

	Myozen, resident monk of Kenninji, accompanied by two or three
	of his disciples, is going to the port of Hakata in order to
	cross to Tang. On the roads of the Saikaido, at the harbours
	and the barriers and the rest, he is to be passed through
	without hindrance. Issued by command of the retired emperor's
	office. So it stands.

	The twenty-first day of the second month, the second year of
	Joo. Sealed by the lieutenant of the Left Guard.
