The third year of Ninji, the year of the tiger. On the twelfth day of
the fourth month he gave a dharma talk before Lord Konoe. Afterwards
there was a question: had this school ever been transmitted to our
country in earlier ages?

He answered: since the buddha-dharma of names and forms came to our
country, barely four hundred years and more have passed, and the
spreading of the Buddha-mind school falls exactly in this present time.
In China the buddha-dharma of names and forms was first transmitted in
the Yongping years of Emperor Ming of the Later Han —
