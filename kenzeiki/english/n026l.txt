In the autumn, in the seventh month, he went up Mount Tiantong for the
first time and was given a place in the hall. The abbot at that time
was Wuji, whose name was Liaopai; he had received the dharma from
Fozhao Guang, and was a dharma-grandson of Chan master Dahui Gao.

But because the Founder and his companions were foreigners from a
barbarous country, they were ranked in the row of the newly ordained.
Myozen was forty at the time. The Founder spoke, and said: in the
house of Shakyamuni, precedence goes by the years since ordination —
