Second. On the old site of Gokurakuji at Fukakusa in the district of
Uji, a vow was made to found Koshoji. It was finished in the spring of
the first year of Tenpuku, the year of the snake, and named Kosho
Horinji. The dharma hall was built by the nun Shogaku, and the dharma
seat was put up by the lord of Guseiin.
