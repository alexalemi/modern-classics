Wishing to found a temple, he searched about the estate for a
convenient place among the mountains and woods, and east of Ichinoyama
and west of Sansho he found ground that suited a temple, and was glad
of it. On the seventeenth day of the intercalary seventh month of that
year the governor of Izumo levelled the hillside and cleared the ground
with his own hands, and the Founder moved from the thatched hut at
Yoshimine to this place.

The second year of Kangen, the year of the dragon.

In that year the building of the Great Buddha Temple was going on, so
perhaps his own lodging was not yet settled.
