The Founder's poems: those made on the night of the mid-autumn moon on
which he died; five on themes from the Lotus Sutra; the poem on the
first snow, the twenty-fifth day of the ninth month of the second year
of Kangen; the poems made at Kamakura in the first year of Hoji at the
request of the lord of Saimyoji; and thirty-four poems made in the
grass hut as they came.
