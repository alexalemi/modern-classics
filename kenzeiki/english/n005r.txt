One. The poem in the picture and the picture in the poem: we wanted
from the first that they should be closely fused. For that reason we
have put a little of our own into the plates — making what was long
there short here, or what was small there large; adding flowers where
we render spring, and bringing in the moon where we draw autumn, and
things of that kind. Why should anyone call this the illustrator's
groping? Because the whole figure, from first to last, is not falsified.
