In the winter of the third year of Baoqing, the year of the boar, he
took his leave of Tiantong. That very night, at midnight, he was called
into the abbot's room, and Rujing gave him the dharma robe of the
ancestor Furong Daokai, the Precious Mirror Samadhi, the Secret
Teaching of the Five Ranks, and a portrait of himself with his own
inscription on it. And he said:

I give these to you, a man from a barbarous country, as a token of
trust. Go back to your own country and spread the teaching there, and
do good widely to gods and men. Do not live in cities and towns. Do not
go near kings and ministers. Live in the deep mountains and the quiet
valleys, and take in one man or half a man, and do not let my school be
cut off.
