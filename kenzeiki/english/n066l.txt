Do not sneer that the head monk is not fit for it. Take up Dongshan's
three pounds of flax, he said, and show it to the assembly — and having
said so he came down from the seat. Then the drum was struck again and
the head monk took the whisk. That was the first such discourse at
Koshoji, and Ejo was thirty-nine.

The third year of Katei, the year of the cock. On the day the summer
retreat was closed he compiled the Rite for Leaving Home and Conferring
the Precepts.
