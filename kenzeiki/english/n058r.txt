	I shall keep the donors' names in the heart of the sacred monk,
	as seed of the wisdom of the ten thousand characters, and as an
	adornment for myself and for others. If among them there should
	be one who goes ahead of the rest and attains the way, I shall
	make him the guide of this assembly. Would he not be a good
	friend indeed?

	And this does not urge on men alone: it should convert the
	heavens and the dragon palace too, and be heard of in the world
	of the immortals and in the halls of the dead. It is simply the
	wheel of the dharma that Shakyamuni turned, and it will reach
	within the dharma realm and beyond it. Respectfully set out.

	A day in the twelfth month of the first year of Katei. The
	chief solicitor, resident of Kannon-dori-in, a monk of the
	Shakya clan.

From that summer the retreat was formally opened.
