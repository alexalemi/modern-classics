The whole assembly of the mountain heard him, but nothing was done. The
Founder pressed the point and would not let it drop. The senior monks
said: it is handed down that when monks came from Japan under the
former dynasty — Kukai, Saicho, Eisai and the rest — every one of them
was ranked with the newly ordained on entering a Chinese monastery.
That is the old precedent, and it is not to be altered now.

At that the Founder sighed to himself and said: that the standard of a
thousand buddhas should fall to the level of ordinary human feeling is
a thing to grieve over. Shall I not put it right?

And he drew up a memorial and laid his case before the Emperor of Song.
