wands and made three bows; the wands were of the five colours. A horse
was given to the master carpenter, and a bolt of silk to every one of
the under-carpenters and the rest, and to the thousand and more men and
women who had come to see, a white rice-cake apiece.

Within the one year both the dharma hall and the monks' hall were
finished. Even so, it seems the full seven halls were not built in the
Founder's lifetime. The record of his life says: the timber was not yet
ready, and there were barely two or three halls standing.

The third year of Kangen, the year of the snake.

On the sixth day of the third month he first set out the fifty-six
fascicles of the Treasury of the True Dharma Eye.

On the day the summer retreat opened, in the fourth month of that year,
heavenly flowers came scattering down before and after his discourse
in the hall, falling on the dharma seat and on the seated monks and
into the very tea bowls. It is recorded that nothing like it had ever
been known.
