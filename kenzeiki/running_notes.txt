THE ILLUSTRATED LIFE OF DŌGEN -- RUNNING NOTES

--------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD (settled before transcription)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- READ FROM pages_nijl/ (1817), CHECK AGAINST pages/ (1806). Both are
  119 openings, page for page, and both are split into half-leaves:
  n007r.jpg is the right half of opening 7 in the 1817 copy, v1_007r
  the same leaf in the 1806. Right comes before left: a Japanese book
  opens the other way.
- WHERE THE TWO COPIES DISAGREE, OR WHERE A CHARACTER IS UNCERTAIN IN
  BOTH, MARK IT. A guessed character that reads plausibly is exactly
  the defect this edition cannot otherwise catch. Uncertainties go in
  source_notes.txt with the leaf and the reading taken, never silently
  resolved.
- The picture captions are an independent outline of the narrative.
  Transcribe the caption for a scene BEFORE the prose around it, and
  use it as a check on the prose: a reading that contradicts the plate
  beside it is wrong somewhere.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
LOCKED RENDERINGS (caps spent, no re-gloss)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
(filled in as the work proceeds)
  祖師          "the Founder" -- Kenzei's standing term for Dōgen, used
                far more often than his name. Not "the Patriarch".
  御            the honorific prefix is NOT rendered as a word; the
                respect is carried by the register, or the English
                becomes unreadable.
  永平寺        Eiheiji, and 永平 alone is Eiheiji where it names the
                temple and Eihei where it names the era.
  建撕記        the Kenzeiki, when the book refers to itself.
